# Topic Cluster 05: Operations, Staffing, and Founder Capacity

## Questions

16. **First hire decision**: The first employee fundamentally changes the business — payroll taxes, training burden, scheduling complexity, HR exposure. What role (prep cook, service window, part-time driver) provides the highest operational leverage for a solo founder, and what minimum revenue threshold justifies that hire?
17. **Operational systems for quality consistency**: Filipino-Mexican fusion involves significant prep complexity. What systems — standardized recipes, pre-portioning protocols, commissary prep schedules — are essential before the second revenue stream (catering or a second truck) is added, to ensure the product doesn't degrade as volume increases?
18. **Founder skill gap prioritization**: Moving from culinary professional to business owner surfaces gaps in financial management, marketing, hiring, and regulatory compliance simultaneously. Given limited bandwidth, which of these gaps poses the greatest near-term operational risk, and what is the most efficient way to address it — self-learning, hiring, or outsourcing?
19. **Customer feedback loops**: Early-stage food trucks often rely on informal social media signals to understand what is working. What more structured feedback mechanisms — loyalty program data, event debrief surveys, repeat customer interviews — would generate actionable insight about menu performance and pricing tolerance without requiring significant time investment?
