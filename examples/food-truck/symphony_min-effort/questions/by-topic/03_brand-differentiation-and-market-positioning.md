# Topic Cluster 03: Brand Differentiation and Market Positioning

## Questions

9. **Competing against established fusion concepts**: With four similar fusion trucks already operating, what dimensions of differentiation — ingredient sourcing story, authentic cultural narrative, service experience, visual identity, price positioning — are most likely to build loyal repeat customers rather than one-time curiosity visits?
10. **Filipino-Mexican fusion storytelling**: The cuisine carries a genuine cross-cultural story (Philippine-Spanish colonial history, California migration corridors, shared flavor profiles). How could that narrative be embedded into the brand, menu language, and customer experience in a way that feels authentic rather than marketable gimmick?
11. **Signature item as entry point**: Menu anchors like lumpia tacos and ube horchata can function as both discovery hooks and shareability drivers. What criteria should guide which one item is elevated to flagship status — operational simplicity, margin, photo-worthiness, repeat purchase frequency — and how does that choice constrain or enable the rest of the menu?
