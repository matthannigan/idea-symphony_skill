# Topic Cluster 06: Revenue Mix and Channel Strategy

## Questions

1. **Revenue-channel contribution margin and sequencing**: Daily vending, catering, and events each have different margin profiles, booking cycles, and operational demands — what is the effective margin per dollar of revenue for a catering booking vs. a peak street-vending shift vs. a well-attended festival booth (after booth fees, event insurance riders, and long-hour staffing), what is the honest month-by-month revenue picture in year one including months where two of three streams are silent, how many festival bookings per quarter stabilize cash flow through slow street-vending months, and does building around high-margin catering early make daily vending secondary infrastructure — or does catering crowd out the street presence that builds the brand equity that makes the catering desirable in the first place?
2. **The distraction question**: Revenue diversification sounds prudent, but each channel requires its own sales effort, logistics, and relationship-building — does pursuing all three in the first year dilute the core operation before it has a stable identity and customer base?
3. **Revenue mix as resilience**: Catering and events can smooth the seasonal and regulatory volatility that daily vending can't control. What combination of revenue streams would let you lean into what you do best — the food, the experience, the story — while giving the business enough stability to invest in the next step?
4. **Whose celebrations get served**: From the perspective of a Filipino community organization planning a cultural event, or a family celebrating a quinceañera, does the fusion framing serve their needs or create an awkward mismatch — and since high-margin private events (corporate lunches, wedding receptions, festival organizers) tend to skew toward clients with existing social capital, what would it look like to deliberately hold capacity for community events with smaller budgets, and what would that cost in revenue versus what it might build in loyalty and rootedness?
