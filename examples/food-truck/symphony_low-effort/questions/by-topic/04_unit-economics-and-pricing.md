# Topic Cluster 04: Unit Economics and Pricing

## Questions

1. **Food cost ratios by SKU and margin weight**: Breaking this down by item — what food cost percentage is realistically achievable for Filipino-Mexican fusion given that ingredients like ube, adobo proteins, and lumpia wrappers may carry higher unit costs than standard taco components, what price point per item sustains a 28–32% food cost ratio while remaining competitive in a mid-size city market, and which items (lumpia tacos, adobo burritos, ube horchata) must carry the margin weight to let the business survive a slow week?
2. **Daily break-even transaction volume**: At a $12–15 average ticket and a 28–32% food cost ratio, what daily gross margin is generated per operating hour, and how many covers per service period does the truck need to clear fixed daily costs (commissary kitchen rental, truck depreciation or lease, fuel, permit amortization, labor) before generating owner income — and at what point does a day become net-positive versus break-even?
3. **Price elasticity and economic access**: How sensitive is the target customer to a $1–2 price increase per item, what does comparable food-truck-market evidence say about the price range at which Filipino-Mexican fusion items optimize for both volume and margin — and if break-even math pushes prices to $12–16 per item, what does that mean for the segment of the city that eats lunch on $8, and how does a premium price signal interact with aspirations to serve a *community* food experience rather than a specialty-market one?
