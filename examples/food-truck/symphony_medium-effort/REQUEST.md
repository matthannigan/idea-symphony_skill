# Request: Food Truck Startup and Expansion Strategy

**Request Date:** 2026-02-24

## Topic
Launching a food truck in a mid-size city, with a plan to grow into a small fleet and potentially a brick-and-mortar location within 3 years.

## Context
- **Business:** Solo founder with culinary background, no prior business ownership
- **Cuisine:** Filipino-Mexican fusion (lumpia tacos, adobo burritos, ube horchata)
- **Location:** Mid-size city (~250K population) with growing food truck scene
- **Starting Capital:** $60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan
- **Regulations:** City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees
- **Competition:** ~30 active food trucks in the area, 4 with similar fusion concepts

## Goals
Develop a sustainable food truck business with clear pricing strategy, unit economics, and a realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation (catering, events, fleet expansion, or brick-and-mortar).

## Key Considerations
- Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)
- Revenue diversification: daily vending vs. catering vs. event bookings
- Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations
- Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs
- The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process
- Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association
- Whether to pursue the SBA loan or bootstrap more conservatively
