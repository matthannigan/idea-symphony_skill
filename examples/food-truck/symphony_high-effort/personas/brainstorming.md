## Topic Cluster 01: Foundational Vision and the Definition of Success

**Topic type:** Strategic/Vision (with a strong creative-reframing streak — "burn the truck," "truck as traveling proof-of-concept membrane").

**7 personas:**

1. **Devil's Advocate (core)** — Interrogates the unspoken assumption that a truck is even the right vehicle for this vision; presses on whether brick-and-mortar is genuinely desired or a socially legible marker.
2. **Pragmatist (core)** — Anchors the vision talk in what a solo founder with $60–100K can actually operate and recognize as "alive" day to day.
3. **Visionary (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Strategic/Vision)** — Engages directly with the "what is actually being sold" and "truck as membrane" reframes; paradigm-shifting reading of success.
4. **Systems Thinker (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Strategic/Vision)** — Maps how definitions of success feed back into daily operating choices, marketing, and expansion triggers.
5. **Futurist (Middle Ring — completes Forward-Looking pair)** — Grounds the "graduate out of trucking" question in trend lines (ghost kitchens, creator-economy food brands) instead of pure abstraction.
6. **Empath (Middle Ring — directly addresses Q2's "emotional signals that the business is alive")** — Names the felt experiences (the weekly regular, the clean service day) that would tell the founder the venture is worth continuing.
7. **Provocateur (Middle Ring — completes Challenge Triad with DA; matched to Q4/Q5 burn-the-truck energy)** — Takes the absurd reframes seriously ("what if the truck is the worst possible delivery mechanism?").

**Cluster-family check:** Challenge (DA, Provocateur) = 2; Forward-Looking (Visionary, Futurist) = 2; External (Systems Thinker) = 1; Human-Centered (Empath) = 1; Analytical/Lenses (Pragmatist) = 1. OK.

## Topic Cluster 03: Unit Economics, Menu Design, and Pricing

**Topic type:** Financial/Business (with a Creative sub-question on pricing-as-cultural-communication).

**7 personas:**

1. **Devil's Advocate (core)** — Challenges the 28–32% food-cost band assumption given ube/calamansi supply volatility; interrogates "break-even is the trap."

3. **Accountant (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Financial)** — Build the portfolio contribution-margin model, blended cost ratio math, and three capital scenarios (bootstrapped/full SBA/conservative ramp).
4. **Simplifier (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for menu design; tension pair with future Momentum Builder)** — Enforces the 2–3-highest-margin-item discipline that the $100K ceiling demands.
5. **Constraint Flipper (Middle Ring — directly matched to Q6 "$100K ceiling as forcing function" and Q5 "break-even as trap")** — Turns capital scarcity into menu design leverage.
6. **Empath (Middle Ring — matched to Q4 "pricing as cultural communication, not just unit economics")** — What a $14 vs $9 lumpia taco communicates about who the experience is for.
7. **First Principles Thinker (Middle Ring — deconstructs the food-cost ratio to ingredients, not industry-benchmark)** — Rebuilds the cost band from first principles rather than inheriting fast-casual defaults.

**Cluster-family check:** Analytical Core + Specialized Lenses (Pragmatist, Accountant, FPT) = 3 (at cap); Challenge (DA, CF) = 2; Progressive (Simplifier) = 1; Human (Empath) = 1. OK.
