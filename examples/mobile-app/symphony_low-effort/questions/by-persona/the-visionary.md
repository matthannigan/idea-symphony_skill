# Brainstorming Questions from The Visionary: Feature-Bloated Mobile App MVP Reset

---

## The Nature of the Core

1. **Single irreplaceable moment**: What if we completely reimagined the concept of "core feature" — not as a function users perform, but as an irreplaceable emotional moment that only this app creates? If 180K people downloaded this app, what singular feeling were they hoping to experience that no other product has ever given them?

2. **The product that doesn't exist yet**: Imagine if the concept of a "fitness app" didn't exist yet and you were inventing it from scratch in this moment — given what you know about these users' actual lives and struggles, what would you build that has no precedent in the current app-store landscape?

---

## Subtraction as Invention

3. **Radical omission as creative act**: What if stripping down to the core wasn't a retreat but an act of invention — and the question isn't which features to keep, but what entirely new kind of relationship with users becomes possible only when you eliminate everything familiar about fitness apps?

4. **The anti-feature**: Imagine a world where the most powerful thing this product could do is deliberately refuse to do something that every other fitness app does — what would that refusal be, and what new category of product would it create?

---

## The User's Hidden Life

5. **47 seconds of truth**: A 47-second average session is usually read as failure, but what if it's a signal pointing toward a fundamentally different product paradigm — one where the entire value is delivered in a single, charged moment rather than sustained engagement? What product could be built around that constraint as a design principle rather than a problem to solve?

6. **The unarticulated need**: Imagine the 8% of users who stayed aren't staying because of any of the 15 features — they're staying for something the product does accidentally, something it never intended. What would it mean to build an entire product around that accidental value, even if it looked nothing like a fitness app?

---

## Reinventing the Category Itself

7. **The team as the product**: What if the burned-out team of 6 engineers and 1 designer — people who know better than anyone what it feels like to build something that's lost its soul — became the design constraint itself? Imagine a world where the product's simplicity was a direct expression of the team's capacity for genuine craft, and "what can we truly love building?" replaced "what do users want" as the organizing question. What product emerges from that inversion?
