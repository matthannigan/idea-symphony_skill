# Request: Feature-Bloated Mobile App MVP Reset

**Request Date:** 2026-02-24

## Topic
Radically simplifying a feature-bloated mobile app by stripping it down to its essential core and relaunching as a focused product.

## Context
- **Product:** Social fitness app that tried to be a workout tracker, meal planner, social feed, challenge platform, marketplace, coaching service, habit tracker, meditation guide, sleep tracker, community forum, progress journal, recipe library, workout video library, supplement store, and friend finder
- **Current State:** 15 features, none executed well; UI requires 6 navigation tabs and a hamburger menu
- **Metrics:** 180K downloads, 8% 30-day retention, 2% DAU/MAU ratio, average session length 47 seconds
- **User Feedback:** "Overwhelming," "don't know where to start," "too many notifications," "jack of all trades, master of none"
- **Team:** 6 engineers, 1 designer, 1 PM; team is burned out from maintaining 15 half-built features
- **Runway:** 8 months of funding remaining

## Goals
Identify the 3-4 features that represent the app's true core value, cut everything else, and relaunch as a focused product that users actually want to open every day.

## Key Considerations
- How to identify which features matter (data analysis vs. user interviews vs. strategic vision)
- What to do with features that have small but passionate user bases
- How to communicate the simplification to existing users without triggering churn
- Technical debt implications of removing deeply integrated features
- Team morale and the psychology of "killing your darlings"
- Whether to simplify incrementally or do a clean relaunch
- Redefining success metrics for the simplified product
