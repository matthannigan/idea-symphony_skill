# Topic Cluster 99: Additional Questions

## Questions

1. **What if the unit of accountability was a context, not a person**: Every social habit tool puts humans in relationship with other humans — accountability partners, leaderboards, challenges. What if we completely rethought accountability as a relationship between a person and a defined context or environment — a specific chair, a time of day, a physical object — and the app's role was to strengthen that context-behavior bond rather than the person-to-person bond, producing something without a current analog in the habit app space?
2. **The data export as memoir**: The PRD includes CSV export as a feature. Imagine a user who, after eight months, downloads their habit log and opens it in a spreadsheet. What do they see, and what story does that data tell about who they were and who they became — and how might the product design the export experience (or the data itself) so that it reads less like a database dump and more like a quiet record of effort?
