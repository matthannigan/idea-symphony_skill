# Topic Cluster 01: Financial Viability and Fiscal Modeling

## Questions

1. **True cost calculus**: Beyond the $1.2M administrative savings estimate, what one-time transition costs — severance, system integration, legal fees, facility remediation — would offset those savings, and over what payback period does consolidation become net-positive?
2. **Enrollment decline stress testing**: If the projected 15% further decline by 2035 materializes (or accelerates), what per-pupil funding levels and minimum enrollment thresholds does the consolidated district need to remain solvent, and how does that compare to the status quo trajectory?
3. **Debt and liability inheritance**: How should the consolidated district structure the assumption of each legacy district's bonded debt and deferred maintenance obligations — particularly the $4.2M backlog — to avoid inheriting a liability crisis on day one?
4. **Property tax equalization**: Given that the three districts likely have different tax rates and assessed values, what approach to property tax equalization minimizes shock to taxpayers in the lowest-tax district while meeting the consolidated district's revenue needs?
5. **State funding timing**: The 12% per-pupil funding increase from the state formula takes effect at what point in the consolidation process, and how does the district bridge cash flow during the transition window before that revenue arrives?
