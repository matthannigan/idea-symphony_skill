# Topic Cluster 02: Facilities Strategy and Transportation

## Questions

6. **Facility decision framework**: What criteria — utilization rate, building condition, geographic centrality, community anchoring role, remediation cost — should drive decisions about which of the seven buildings to retain, close, or convert, and who has standing to weigh in on those criteria?
7. **Closure sequencing and politics**: If at least one school building must close, what sequencing or phasing approach minimizes political resistance while still capturing facility savings on a meaningful timeline?
8. **Repurposing vs. disposal**: For buildings that are closed, what alternative uses — community centers, early childhood programs, municipal services, lease to nonprofits — preserve community investment in those structures and generate offsetting revenue or goodwill?
9. **Transportation cost modeling**: Across a larger geographic district, what does the true per-student transportation cost increase look like under different facility configurations, and at what point does transportation spending erode the administrative savings consolidation was supposed to produce?
10. **Rural access and equity**: For students in the most remote communities, how does increased travel time affect participation in extracurriculars, early/late programs, and family engagement — and what mitigation strategies are financially realistic?
