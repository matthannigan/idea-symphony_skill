# Topic Cluster 04: Community Identity and Political Coalition Building

## Questions

15. **Identity preservation strategies**: Beyond keeping a school building open, what structural or symbolic elements — team names, local advisory councils, feeder school identities, community use agreements — can a consolidated district offer to communities worried about losing their local character?
16. **Coalition asymmetry**: If the fiscal case for consolidation is strongest in the most financially distressed district and weakest in the district with the healthiest fund balance, what benefits or assurances would make consolidation compelling to voters in the district with the least to gain?
17. **Winning the voter approval campaign**: Given that all three districts must approve consolidation simultaneously, what sequencing of public engagement — joint forums, district-specific listening sessions, feasibility study rollout — builds the broadest possible informed consent before the vote?
18. **Governance design for trust**: What board structure, representation formula, and transition governance arrangements for the new unified district would give each legacy community confidence that its interests won't be steamrolled by the combined majority?
