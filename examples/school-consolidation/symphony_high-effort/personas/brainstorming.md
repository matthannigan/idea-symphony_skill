# Brainstorming Persona Selections (Phase 2D)

Seven personas per topic cluster: **Core (2)** = Devil's Advocate + Pragmatist (always); **Inner Ring (2)** = topic-matched from Strong Fit; **Middle Ring (3)** = cluster-completers and outer-ring specialists where genuinely warranted.

Retired personas (Questioner, Analyst, Synthesizer) are excluded throughout, per the selection guide.

## Detailed Per-Topic Rationale

### Cluster 01 — Problem Framing and Strategic Alternatives
- **Topic type:** Strategic/Vision + Novel/Ambiguous. Questions interrogate whether consolidation is the right frame at all (Chapter 9 bankruptcy template, hospital-merger parallel, "designing with decline").
- **Inner Ring:**
  - **First Principles Thinker** — Strong Fit for Strategic/Vision; the cluster's lead question explicitly asks "from-zero institutional design… regardless of legacy district boundaries." FPT is the right lens for Q1, Q4, Q5.
  - **Analogist** — Strong Fit for Novel/Ambiguous; cluster contains explicit cross-domain prompts (Chapter 9 municipal bankruptcy, hospital mergers). The selection guide flags Analogist as the strongest pick when a topic doesn't map cleanly to one domain.
- **Middle Ring:**
  - **Visionary** — Cluster repeatedly invites paradigm shifts (Q4 "plan with decline," Q5 "different institutional form"). Pairs with FPT to widen the alternatives surface.
  - **Connector** — Completes External Perspectives Triad with Analogist (Systems Thinker is selected in adjacent clusters); Connector's structural-parallels lens is useful for Q6/Q7 (insolvency, hospital mergers as templates).
  - **Provocateur** — Q5 ("merger as the disease") and Q4 are explicit absurdist reframes; Provocateur extends DA's critique into "what if the entire frame is wrong?"
- **Cluster-family check:** Analytical Core 1 (Pragmatist; FPT counted under analytical). External Perspectives 2 (Analogist + Connector). Forward-Looking 1 (Visionary). Challenge 2 (DA + Provocateur). No family exceeds 3. PASS.

### Cluster 02 — Financial Transition, State Funding, and Tax/Bond Restructuring
- **Topic type:** Financial/Business + Regulated/Compliance. Hard numbers (break-even, state formula, equalization arithmetic, bond legal restructuring).
- **Inner Ring:**
  - **Accountant** — Strong Fit for Financial/Business; the cluster is the Accountant's home turf ($1.2M savings vs. front-loaded transition costs, formula step-change, equalization arithmetic).
  - **Systems Thinker** — Good Fit for Financial/Business; Q8 (re-emergent coordination overhead) and Q11 (winners/losers feedback into voter sentiment) are systemic loops.
- **Middle Ring:**
  - **Lawyer** — Outer ring genuinely warranted: bond obligation legal restructuring (Q12), state funding formula compliance (Q10 — REAP/Small Rural School Achievement reauthorization), tax equalization legal mechanics (Q11). This is exactly the "real regulatory dimension" the guide gates on.
  - **Futurist** — Good Fit for Financial/Business; Q10 stress-tests state formula revisions and reauthorization risk over a 10-year horizon — trend-grounded extrapolation.
  - **Constraint Flipper** — Reframes the deferred-maintenance liability (Q9) and bond-restructuring constraints (Q12) as design opportunities rather than inherited burdens.
- **Cluster-family check:** Analytical+Specialized lens family: Pragmatist + Accountant + Lawyer = 3 (analytical core counts with specialized lenses per the guide). At cap, not exceeded. External 1 (ST). Forward-Looking 1 (Futurist). Challenge 2 (DA + CF). PASS.
