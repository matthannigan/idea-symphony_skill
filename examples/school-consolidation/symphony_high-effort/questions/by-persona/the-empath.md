# Brainstorming Questions from The Empath: Rural School District Consolidation

---

## The Grief of Losing "Our School"

1. **The loss people won't name**: When a parent says "our school IS our town," what are they actually mourning — and how does that unspoken grief differ from what they'll say at a public meeting?

2. **The child's felt experience**: For a student who has grown up in a small district where everyone knows their name, what does it emotionally feel like to be absorbed into a larger institution — and how might that shift in belonging affect their engagement and sense of self?

3. **The teacher's emotional stakes**: Beyond job security, what are teachers in these three districts emotionally invested in — and what would make them feel like something irreplaceable had been lost, even if their paycheck survived?

4. **The wound of the closed building**: When a school building closes and a community watches it sit dark or get sold, what emotional scar does that leave — and how long does that feeling persist in how people talk about the district?

---

## The Hidden Emotional Landscape of the Vote

5. **What people won't admit they fear**: What fears will voters carry into the ballot booth that they'll never articulate in a town hall — and how do those silent anxieties differ across the three communities, given their different financial situations?

6. **The resentment that consolidation creates**: Even if consolidation passes, what seeds of resentment get planted in the community that loses the most — and how does that lingering bitterness shape the new district's culture for years afterward?

7. **The emotional asymmetry between districts**: The smallest district faces insolvency in two years; the others are not at that cliff yet. How does the felt urgency differ across these three communities — and does that emotional distance make coalition-building feel like one community is being rescued at the expense of the others?

---

## What Feels Like Betrayal vs. What Feels Like Care

8. **The difference between efficiency and erasure**: Administrative consolidation saves $1.2M — but for the town that loses its superintendent, its school board, its local HR office, how does that efficiency read emotionally? What is the line between "smart restructuring" and "they just don't care about us anymore"?

9. **The governance question as an emotional one**: When parents imagine the new unified board, do they feel represented or outvoted? What does it emotionally mean for a small community to go from having direct access to their own superintendent to being one of many voices competing for attention?

---

## The Emotional Energy That Could Power or Poison This

10. **The longing this could tap into**: Beyond the fear and grief, is there anything about this consolidation that could tap into a genuine longing — for stability, for a school their kids can actually thrive in, for not living under the cloud of impending insolvency? What emotional case for consolidation hasn't been made yet?
