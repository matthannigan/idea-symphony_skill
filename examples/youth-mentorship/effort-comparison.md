## 1. Headline Finding

The core thesis survives intact across all four levels, almost verbatim: **the mechanisms a program installs to protect itself and stay legally defensible are the same mechanisms that push away the at-risk teens it exists to serve, and the design job is to make each safeguard fail toward inclusion and feel like care rather than surveillance.** Every BRAINSTORM.md opens on this finding. What scales is not the thesis but its sharpness — `min` states it as a design challenge, `high` traces it through twelve clusters and pairs it with a second-order reversal (chronic absence is accurate information about a failing school, not a teen deficit).

**USER-QUESTIONS.md is a confound and must be flagged.** The user supplied 10 specific questions (background-check criteria, meeting-location policy, mandatory-reporter training, liability/SAM coverage, juvenile-justice consent, matching criteria, concern escalation, burnout cadence, outcome measurement, match termination). All 10 are preserved as `[User Q]` markers at every level (confirmed in each QUESTIONS.md and PLAN.md). So the convergence on those ten topics is partly *seeded*, not independently discovered — when comparing what each level "found on its own," the user questions are the floor, and the genuine discovery signal lives in the material *around* them.

## 5. Productive Drift Between Levels

Five zones where recommendations sharpen (not contradict) as effort rises.

**Scale / launch size.** `min` implies caution via tiered mentor routing but names no number; `low` sets a post-attrition target of 10–12 active matches; `medium` reasons from per-coordinator capacity (15–25 pairs) to a ~5-pair documented pilot; `high` defends a 25–30-match year-one ceiling ("recruit ~50 to land 35–40"). *Direction: vague restraint → defended capacity number derived from coordinator economics.*

**Insurance / SAM coverage.** `min` says buy a standalone SAM policy before launch; `low` sharpens to "excluded by endorsement — get the endorsement page, not a verbal assurance"; `medium` adds a required broker letter on whether the church policy extends to off-site mentoring (~$4–6K/yr); `high` specifies occurrence basis, rejects the common $250K abuse sub-limit, and reframes the stack as *the program, not overhead*. *Direction: "buy coverage" → precise policy-language interrogation.*

**Mandatory reporting / escalation.** `min` flags the grey-zone gap but leaves it unsolved; `low` routes any harm-touching disclosure to a safeguarding lead; `medium` narrows the mentor's job to "notice, document, tell one person" with staff making the legal call; `high` makes it unanimous and reverses the framing — name the limit honestly, accept some suppression, measure by child safety. *Direction: gap → architecture → reframed goal.*

**Outcome measurement.** `min` decouples internal learning from funder reports; `low` runs two tracks and presses the absenteeism values question; `medium` adds selection bias as the deepest cost plus a last-quarter measurement freeze; `high` makes structured mentor observation the *primary* instrument, caps tracked metrics at ~3, and tells mentors they are not evaluated on attendance/grades. *Direction: decoupling → relationship-protective instrumentation.*

**Endings / lifecycle.** `min` builds closure debriefs pre-launch; `low` requires a pre-funded, board-owned wind-down; `medium` converts the flat one-year minimum into a tiered ladder with month-3/6/9 checkpoints; `high` reframes endings as the *signature capability*, designed as heavily as the first meeting. *Direction: operational hygiene → core programmatic offering.*

Weakness: it accepts the brief wholesale — no challenge to "at-risk," the referral pipeline, the one-year model, or survival odds — so it cannot tell you whether you are solving the right problem.

Weakness: the two lenses are "structurally similar" (the BRAINSTORM says so), so the same blind spots — the teen's affective experience, intra-population equity — recur across nearly every cluster.

**`medium`.** Best when you need reconciled multi-perspective depth with traceable dissent — the `attributed/` and `_synthesis.md` layers make per-persona reasoning recoverable. **`high`.** Best when the stakes justify interrogating the foundations and you need calibrated confidence (N-of-M convergence). Weakness: cost, and a dependency it names against itself — nearly every recommendation rests on coordinator time the $15K budget "barely covers," so the richest output is also the most at risk of being theater without funded capacity.
