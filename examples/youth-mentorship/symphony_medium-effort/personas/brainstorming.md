## Per-Topic Rationale

### Cluster 01: Vision, Purpose & Strengths-Based Foundations
- **Topic type:** Strategic/Vision with novel/ambiguous framing (Q5 reimagines power gradient, Q6 imagines a new institutional category).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Visionary, Systems Thinker
- **Rationale for P3 (Visionary):** Q4 ("2029 model-program vision"), Q5 ("co-inquiry instead of guidance"), and Q6 ("a new kind of civic institution") are explicit paradigm-shift prompts that require ignoring current reality to imagine what the program could become — Visionary is the strongest fit per Strategic/Vision affinity.
- **Rationale for P4 (Systems Thinker):** Q2 maps existing congregation/school-counselor/foundation assets as an asset base, and Q3's "five pairs as discovery data" frames the program as a feedback system — Systems Thinker zooms out to map these dynamics and asset interdependencies.
- **Cluster-family check:** Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Visionary (Forward-Looking) + Systems Thinker (External Perspectives) + DA (Challenge) — 4 distinct families, well within the ≤3 limit.

### Cluster 02: Volunteer Recruitment, Screening & Background Checks
- **Topic type:** Regulated/Compliance (statutory background-check floors, discrimination exposure) layered on Implementation/Ops (pipeline design).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Lawyer, Constraint Flipper
- **Rationale for P3 (Lawyer):** Q1 explicitly invokes "state-mandated absolute bars," "discrimination claims," and decision-authority tiering for borderline records — this is a statutory-floor/case-law topic where Lawyer is the strongest fit per Regulated/Compliance affinity.
- **Rationale for P4 (Constraint Flipper):** Q2 (screening-funnel attrition stranding enrolled teens) and Q4 (volunteer-rhythm problem across institutional cycles) are pipeline constraints that need reframing as design opportunities — Constraint Flipper turns the 40-volunteer attrition gap and misaligned calendars into structural features rather than failures.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Lawyer (Specialized Lens) + Constraint Flipper (Challenge Triad) — no family exceeds 2.

### Cluster 03: Mentor Training, Mandatory-Reporter Competency & Ongoing Support
- **Topic type:** Regulated/Compliance (mandatory reporter law, escalation chains) + User-Facing (mentor experience, burnout, trust ruptures).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Lawyer, Empath
- **Rationale for P3 (Lawyer):** Q1 (mandatory-reporter knowledge components, competency verification), Q2 (regulatory filing timelines, conflicting institutional mandates), and Q5 (shared protocol layer across faith-org/school/juvenile-justice) are explicit compliance-architecture problems — Lawyer is essential.
- **Rationale for P4 (Empath):** Q3 (training as gift not burden), Q4 (reciprocal protection from secondary trauma), Q7 (the hard week when a teen ghosts), and Q8 (the moment trust breaks) are emotional-resonance prompts — Empath operates on the felt experience of mentors and teens that compliance training tends to flatten.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Lawyer + Empath — 4 distinct families.

### Cluster 04: Matching Logic & Relationship Dynamics
- **Topic type:** User-Facing/Community + Novel/Ambiguous (Q4 imports conservation biology; Q5 inverts who selects whom).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Empath, Analogist
- **Rationale for P3 (Empath):** Q2's early-warning signals depend on reading relational micro-signals; Q5 (teens vetting mentors) and Q6 (the quiet Tuesday that changes everything) require attention to felt experience and unscripted moments — Empath catches what compatibility scoring misses.
- **Rationale for P4 (Analogist):** Q4 explicitly invokes conservation-biology habitat-matching as a structural parallel, and the cluster as a whole asks what predicts durable bonds — Analogist imports concrete matching solutions from other domains (sponsor programs, foster-care matching, ecological fit), which the guidance flags as most valuable when topics span multiple framings.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Empath (Human-Centered) + Analogist (External Perspectives) — 4 distinct families.

### Cluster 05: Consent, Referral Integrity & Juvenile-Justice Privacy
- **Topic type:** Regulated/Compliance (juvenile-record statutes, layered consent) + Governance (referral institutions with conflicting incentives).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Lawyer, Audience Advocate
- **Rationale for P3 (Lawyer):** Q1 (layered consent, contested guardianship, state custody) and Q2 (juvenile-record confidentiality statutes, lawful disclosure boundaries, retention) are core statutory questions — Lawyer is the strongest fit and the cluster cannot be properly explored without this lens.
- **Rationale for P4 (Audience Advocate):** Q3 maps three distinct stakeholder groups (school counselors, juvenile justice officers, teens themselves) with different incentives and risk thresholds; Q4 (warm handoff) and Q5 (referral as trust transfer) are about whose needs the intake protects — Audience Advocate maps these stakeholder segments and the conflicts between them.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Lawyer + Audience Advocate (Human-Centered) — 4 distinct families.

### Cluster 06: Safety Architecture, Meeting-Location Policy & Insurance
- **Topic type:** Regulated/Compliance (liability surface, insurance architecture, prohibited settings).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Lawyer, Constraint Flipper
- **Rationale for P3 (Lawyer):** Q1 (meeting-location liability matrix, categorically prohibited combinations) and Q2 (general liability, D&O, SAM coverage, church policy extension) are insurance and tort-exposure questions — Lawyer is the strongest fit for the cluster's statutory and contractual content.
- **Rationale for P4 (Constraint Flipper):** Q3 (threshold where safeguards become burdens), Q4 (what if safeguards cause the harm), and Q5 (safety as emergent property of relationships, not legal architecture) explicitly ask to invert the constraint — Constraint Flipper reframes liability infrastructure as a design variable rather than a fixed floor, which is the cluster's distinctive provocation.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Lawyer + Constraint Flipper (Challenge Triad with DA) — Challenge family has 2 (DA + CF), still ≤3.

### Cluster 07: Teen Voice, Family Engagement & Cultural Inclusion
- **Topic type:** User-Facing/Community (lived experience, dignity, cultural fit, faith-identity signaling).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Empath, Storyteller
- **Rationale for P3 (Empath):** Q1 (the first 15 minutes from the teen's perspective), Q3 (cultural-linguistic fit and the stigma of "at-risk"), and Q4 (invisible logistics read as disengagement) are gut-level felt-experience questions — Empath is the strongest fit for what the program signals to teens before policy ever speaks.
- **Rationale for P4 (Storyteller):** Q5 ("crossing-the-threshold moment" of a 15-year-old walking in) and Q6 (strip the church entirely) are explicit named-character scenario prompts; the cluster asks how the program reads from inside teen and family lives — Storyteller's lived-experience narrative is the natural lens.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Empath + Storyteller — Human-Centered family has 2 (Empath + Storyteller), still ≤3.

### Cluster 08: Outcome Measurement & Learning Loops
- **Topic type:** Strategic/Vision (what counts as success) + User-Facing (teens' own definitions, surveillance burden).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Systems Thinker, Audience Advocate
- **Rationale for P3 (Systems Thinker):** Q3 explicitly names the feedback dynamic where "the measurement apparatus starts degrading the participation it's trying to study"; Q2 (attrition exit data feeding back into matching) is a learning-loop design problem — Systems Thinker maps the reinforcing/balancing dynamics between measurement and the behavior being measured.
- **Rationale for P4 (Audience Advocate):** Q1 explicitly contrasts "the funder's metrics" with "a 15-year-old's definition of a good year"; Q4 (what mentors and teens experience as the metrics) and Q5 (qualitative transformation visible to the community) all turn on whose definition of success the framework serves — Audience Advocate maps these stakeholder definitions against each other.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Systems Thinker (External Perspectives) + Audience Advocate (Human-Centered) — 4 distinct families.

### Cluster 09: Sustainability, Funding & Institutional Continuity
- **Topic type:** Financial/Business (cost per match, $15K gap, behavioral lock-in) + Implementation/Ops (single-coordinator failure, caseload ratios).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Accountant, Systems Thinker
- **Rationale for P3 (Accountant):** Q1 explicitly asks for cost-per-match unit economics broken down across screening/training/coordination/overhead, the self-sustaining funding threshold, and grant-renewal contingencies — this is the cluster's distinctive content and Accountant is the strongest fit per Financial/Business affinity. Note: Phase 2A excluded Accountant from question generation because the REQUEST framed funding as background; here in Phase 2D the synthesized cluster has elevated funding to a core analytical question, so the lens is now genuinely warranted.
- **Rationale for P4 (Systems Thinker):** Q1's "behavioral lock-in" (which behaviors collapse if the grant isn't renewed), Q2's single-point-of-failure on the administrative coordinator, and Q3's reputation feedback loop (mentor burnout reshaping the future volunteer pipeline) are all reinforcing-loop and dependency-mapping problems — Systems Thinker is essential to surface what's load-bearing.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Accountant (Specialized Lens) + Systems Thinker (External Perspectives) — 4 distinct families.

### Cluster 10: Endings, Continuity & What Comes Next
- **Topic type:** User-Facing/Community (rupture, abandonment, launching) + Implementation/Ops (off-ramp protocols, phased commitment, cross-system coherence).
- **Personas:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Empath, Momentum Builder
- **Rationale for P3 (Empath):** Q1 (the one-year cliff and re-traumatization risk), Q2 (a teen feeling another failure when requesting a change), and Q4 (graduation that doesn't feel like abandonment) are emotional-rupture questions — Empath is essential for the felt experience of an ending that, done badly, undoes the program's core work.
- **Rationale for P4 (Momentum Builder):** Q1's phased-commitment alternatives, Q2's match-termination protocol, and Q5's "community memory project" all need progressive-elaboration design — what carries forward, what the off-ramp scaffolds, how a one-year ending becomes the first chapter of something else. Momentum Builder's "yes, and" forward motion fits the cluster's continuity framing better than a subtractive lens.
- **Cluster-family check:** DA + Pragmatist + Empath (Human-Centered) + Momentum Builder (Progressive/Elaborative) — 4 distinct families.

## Summary Notes

- All 10 clusters have exactly 4 personas.
- Devil's Advocate and Pragmatist appear in every cluster (core, mandatory).
- No retired personas (Questioner, Analyst, Synthesizer) selected.
- Lawyer appears in 4 clusters (02, 03, 05, 06) — every appearance is grounded in explicit statutory/liability content in the cluster's questions, not topic keywords alone.
- Accountant appears in 1 cluster (09), where the synthesized questions elevated unit economics from background context to a core analytical concern. This is consistent with Phase 2A excluding Accountant from question generation when funding was only background; the synthesis surfaced it.
- No cluster exceeds 3 personas from the same cluster family. The closest cases are Cluster 06 (DA + Constraint Flipper, 2 from Challenge Triad) and Cluster 07 (Empath + Storyteller, 2 from Human-Centered Triad) — both still well within the limit.
