# Request: Youth Mentorship Program for At-Risk Teens

**Request Date:** 2026-02-24

## Topic
Launching a one-on-one youth mentorship program pairing adult volunteers with at-risk teens in an urban community.

## Context
- **Organization:** Faith-based community organization with 20-year presence in the neighborhood
- **Program Model:** One-on-one mentoring, weekly meetings for at least one year commitment
- **Target Youth:** Teens ages 13-17 identified through school counselors and juvenile justice referrals
- **Volunteer Pool:** ~40 interested adults from the congregation and partner organizations
- **Community:** High-poverty urban neighborhood with limited youth programming; local schools report 35% chronic absenteeism
- **Funding:** $15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)

## Goals
Design a safe, effective mentorship program that creates meaningful relationships between adult mentors and at-risk teens while protecting all participants and meeting legal requirements.

## Key Considerations
- Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers
- Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors
- Liability insurance and organizational legal protections
- Parental/guardian consent frameworks and ongoing communication
- Boundaries and safety protocols for one-on-one interactions with minors
- Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice
- Matching criteria and process for mentor-mentee pairs
- Mentor training curriculum and ongoing support
- Measuring program outcomes without overburdening participants
