# Topic Cluster 02: Volunteer Recruitment, Screening & Background Checks

## Questions

1. **Disqualification tiering and borderline cases**: What framework should govern which background-check findings are automatic disqualifiers, which require case-by-case review, and which are irrelevant to working with at-risk teens — incorporating any state-mandated absolute bars (e.g., registered-sex-offender status) and discretionary review categories so the screening policy meets the legal floor without exposing the organization to discrimination claims, and how should borderline cases like old non-violent offenses or expunged records be handled, with whom holding decision authority at each tier? [User Q]
2. **Screening-funnel attrition and the launch gap**: Breaking down the volunteer intake process into its component stages — expression of interest, application, background check, interview, training, matching — at what rate do you expect candidates to drop out at each stage, and if half or more of the 40 "interested adults" don't complete the formal commitment process, how does the program avoid launching with mismatched pair counts that strand enrolled teens?
3. **Renewal cadence, off-cycle re-screening, and value-alignment screening**: What is the right interval for re-running background checks on active volunteers, and what events (arrests, address changes, role changes) should trigger an off-cycle review — and beyond criminal-record screening, what process surfaces the misalignment between a congregation member's worldview and a teen's lived reality before it becomes a failed match, supporting the standard of care needed to manage negligent-selection-and-retention exposure?
4. **Volunteer rhythm problem across institutional cycles**: Faith congregations, schools, and juvenile justice systems each operate on different annual rhythms (liturgical calendar, academic year, fiscal/court cycles) — how might the program design its volunteer onboarding and renewal cadence to account for the fact that mentor availability and organizational attention will peak and trough on different schedules depending on which institution a volunteer comes from?

**Cluster focus**: Designing a defensible volunteer pipeline that meets statutory background-check floors, screens for value alignment, and survives predictable attrition.
