# User-Defined Questions

Specific questions the user wants the brainstorming process to address.

1. What should our minimum disqualifying criteria be for volunteer background checks, and how should we handle borderline cases like old non-violent offenses or records that were later expunged?
2. Should mentor-mentee meetings always be held in public spaces, or are there specific circumstances where private or home settings are appropriate and defensible?
3. How do we structure mandatory reporter training so mentors actually internalize and act on it, rather than just checking a box during onboarding?
4. What level of liability insurance is appropriate for a program of our size, and should we carry a separate sexual misconduct policy in addition to general liability?
5. How do we obtain informed consent from teens referred through juvenile justice, given the power dynamics and the privacy stakes of their records?
6. What matching criteria produce the strongest mentor-mentee outcomes — demographic similarity, shared interests, personality fit, or something less obvious?
7. How should a mentor raise a concern about a mentee when they're unsure whether it rises to the level of a mandatory report?
8. What ongoing support and check-in cadence do mentors need to prevent burnout, especially during the first six months of a match?
9. How do we measure program outcomes without overburdening teens with surveys or making mentors feel surveilled?
10. What's the right protocol when a mentor-mentee pairing isn't working, and how do we transition without causing harm to the teen?
