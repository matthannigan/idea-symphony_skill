## Topic Cluster 01: Foundations, Purpose, and Theory of Change

**Topic classification:** Strategic/Vision + Novel/Ambiguous. Questions interrogate the program's underlying assumptions, mechanism of change, and whether inherited formats (one-on-one, year-long, weekly) are right at all — paradigm-shift territory rather than implementation.

**Core:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist
**Inner Ring:** Visionary, Futurist
**Middle Ring:** Provocateur, Constraint Flipper, Analogist

- *Visionary* — Strong fit for Strategic/Vision; questions like "what if mentorship weren't a one-way transfer" and "what if the constraint forces the single highest-impact element" are explicitly paradigm-shift prompts.
- *Futurist* — Completes the Forward-Looking Pair with Visionary; useful for grounding bold reframes against the trend (declining high-commitment volunteerism, evolving definitions of "at-risk").
- *Provocateur* — Completes the Challenge Triad with DA + CF; the cluster contains multiple absurdist provocations ("what if chronic absenteeism is the correct response") that this persona is purpose-built for.
- *Constraint Flipper* — Completes the Challenge Triad; the $15K seed funding and 40-volunteer pool are exactly the kind of constraints to reframe as design features.
- *Analogist* — Strong fit for Novel/Ambiguous; mentorship's mechanism of change is genuinely contested and benefits from cross-domain imports (apprenticeship, kinship networks, therapeutic alliance literature).

## Topic Cluster 02: Volunteer Screening, Background Checks, and Disqualifying Criteria

**Topic classification:** Regulated/Compliance with embedded user-facing dimension (relational capacity, congregation familiarity, savior energy).

**Core:** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist
**Inner Ring:** Lawyer, Systems Thinker
**Middle Ring:** Empath, Audience Advocate, Analogist

- *Lawyer* — Strong fit; FCRA, Pennsylvania Act 153, CANRA, and statutory floor questions are explicitly named in the cluster. Compliance architecture, not routine permits.
- *Systems Thinker* — Good fit for Regulated/Compliance; the cluster surfaces drift dynamics (congregation familiarity bending screening leniency) that need feedback-loop analysis.
- *Empath* — Question 11 explicitly asks how to differentiate genuine relational capacity from savior energy — a felt-sense distinction the Empath is built for.
- *Audience Advocate* — Maps the screening experience from the volunteer's side; the "10 deeply committed vs 40 moderately interested" question is stakeholder-needs work.
- *Analogist* — Imports screening models from clinical credentialing, foster-care licensing, or Big Brothers Big Sisters' published criteria; useful given the cluster's "validate from evidence rather than inheriting risk-aversion" framing.
