# Topic Cluster 07: Budget, DIY & Resource Tradeoffs

## Questions

1. **Concrete budget ceiling, per-child cost tiers, and the three highest-value elements**: What assumptions are embedded in "budget-friendly" — relative to what baseline, for whom — and have we set a hard number with a per-child cost breakdown across decorations, food, party favors, activities, and any hired entertainment at different tiers ($15/child vs. $30/child vs. $50/child) so we can identify the three elements that deliver the most perceived value per dollar and concentrate spending there, given that "budget-friendly" and "space-themed" are in tension (silver, stars, astronaut suits, inflatable rockets skew toward disposable plastics or expensive rentals) and without that hard number we risk scope-creeping into a party that costs three times the initial expectation?
2. **DIY-vs-purchased, waste analysis, and the leverage point in the vendor-or-DIY cascade**: For core elements like a space backdrop, planet balloons, and astronaut favor bags — where DIY decisions feel independent but compound (each element consumes time, and if one runs over it compresses the time available for others) — what is the realistic time investment to DIY each item, at what hourly value of the host's time does purchasing become more rational, which themed paper goods and single-use decorations have reuse potential across future events or siblings' parties (vs. generating significant waste), which single element if professionally handled or simplified is the leverage point that most reliably de-stresses overall preparation, and which low-effort/high-delight elements channel existing parental capabilities into the party experience so the plan can actually be pulled off without stress?
3. **Budget-friendly approaches that have produced outsized delight**: When parents have thrown memorable, immersive parties on modest budgets, what techniques — DIY elements, repurposed materials, community borrowing — have created the most impact per dollar? What does that suggest about where investment matters most and where creativity can substitute?
4. **One concrete first step this week**: Given everything that already exists — the birthday girl's imagination, the parents' skills, the friends' enthusiasm, the resources at hand — what single concrete action this week would move toward the most vivid version of this party? What asset is already in place that makes that first step achievable?
