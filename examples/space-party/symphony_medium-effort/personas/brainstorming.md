## Topic Cluster 01: Theme, Immersion & Wonder

**Topic classification:** Creative/Whimsical (theme design, immersion, wonder-production), with a strong human-experiential angle (5-year-olds' inner experience of "space").

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Several questions in this cluster carry "what if space is the wrong theme" and "decoration complexity trap" provocations — DA's critique stance keeps these challenges sharp rather than glossed.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Theme decisions must land as concrete, executable touchpoints (vocabulary, music cues, threshold props) — Pragmatist anchors creative ambition to a doable minimum-viable theming threshold.
- **Visionary (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Creative/Whimsical):** Questions about "the party as a world, not an event" and finding "the genuine emotional register of space" call for paradigm-shifting reimagination of what theming can be, not incremental decoration choices.
- **Empath (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Creative/Whimsical):** Several questions hinge on what wonder *feels like* to a 5-year-old at their 3.5-foot vantage point — Empath's emotional-resonance lens is the right instrument for designing for that felt sense.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Visionary (Forward-Looking) + Empath (Human-Centered) = 4 distinct cluster families. No family exceeds 1; constraint of "no more than 3 from same family" satisfied.

## Topic Cluster 02: The Birthday Girl's Experience

**Topic classification:** User-Facing (single primary user — the birthday girl) with a strong narrative/character dimension ("her role in the story", "the moment she walks in").

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Challenges the assumption that the parents' vision aligns with the birthday girl's, and that a "space-themed party" is what she actually wants — exactly the skeptical interrogation question 1 invites.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Translates "make her feel like the protagonist" into concrete executable rituals, keepsakes, and roles that can actually happen on the day.
- **Empath (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for User-Facing):** This entire cluster centers one child's emotional journey from arrival to goodbye, including risk of "feeling sidelined or pressured to perform happiness" — Empath's gut-feeling, emotional-resonance lens is the most direct match.
- **Storyteller (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for User-Facing):** Questions explicitly frame her as a character in a narrative ("the alien ambassador", "the star cartographer", a keepsake she'll retell months later) — Storyteller's named-character, lived-experience scenarios are precisely the right register.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Empath (Human-Centered) + Storyteller (Human-Centered) = 3 distinct families with Human-Centered at 2 of 4. Within limit (≤3 per family).

## Topic Cluster 03: Activity Design, Attention & Engagement

**Topic classification:** Primarily Implementation/Ops (activity sequencing, pacing, transition mechanics) with a strong User-Facing layer (what 5-year-olds actually experience and crave).

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Questions deliberately invite reverse-engineering from worst-case failure and provoking "productive discomfort" — DA's critique stance is the natural home for stress-testing the activity slate.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Activity feasibility within a 5-year-old's attention-span ceiling, motor capability, and frustration tolerance is a Pragmatist question by definition.
- **Momentum Builder (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Implementation/Ops):** "Pacing as a self-correcting system", buffer activities, transition cues, and the energy arc of the afternoon are progressive-elaboration design problems — Momentum Builder's "yes, and" applied to sequencing fits directly.
- **Empath (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for User-Facing/Creative):** Several questions are explicitly about when a 5-year-old's face lights up, what makes them feel like a crew rather than a crowd, and what they're "genuinely capable of and hungry for" — Empath's emotional-resonance lens is essential here and adds diversity beyond the implementation-leaning selections.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Momentum Builder (Progressive/Elaborative) + Empath (Human-Centered) = 4 distinct cluster families.

## Topic Cluster 04: Inclusion, Sensory Care & Emotional Regulation

**Topic classification:** User-Facing/Inclusion-focused (sensory profiles, allergies, mobility differences, the felt experience at 3.5-foot vantage point), with strong human-centered emotional regulation content.

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Surfaces hidden exclusion patterns — what the planning misses, where "looks fine" hides a child who can't participate, where the "galaxy food" visual masks an allergen.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Allergy profiling, accessibility audits, and identifying "soft landing zones" need concrete, doable design moves and pre-event checklists.
- **Empath (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for User-Facing):** "Overwhelm as a compounding dynamic", the emotional fraughtness of being the child who can't have the cake, and "the physics of wonder for five-year-olds" all live in Empath's emotional-resonance territory.
- **Audience Advocate (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for User-Facing):** This cluster is explicitly about mapping diverse stakeholder needs (children with sensory sensitivities, allergies, mobility differences, plus their accompanying caregivers) — Audience Advocate's segment-mapping discipline is the right instrument for not flattening that diversity.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Empath (Human-Centered) + Audience Advocate (Human-Centered) = 3 families with Human-Centered at 2 of 4. Within limit.

## Topic Cluster 05: Adult Capacity & Parent Engagement

**Topic classification:** Implementation/Ops (role pre-assignment, supervision ratios, attention-budget design) with a User-Facing layer for attending parents-as-stakeholders.

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Cluster includes the "no adults at all" provocation and challenges to default supervision logic — DA keeps the inversion honest and surfaces hidden assumptions about adult-centered logistics.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Adult-to-child ratios, role pre-assignment, and the host parent's confidence-to-let-go all need feasibility-grounded answers.

- **Momentum Builder (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Implementation/Ops):** Pre-assigning roles, designing spatial layouts as buffers, and the "moment the parent lets go" question are progressive-design problems where Momentum Builder's elaborative stance fits.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Audience Advocate (Human-Centered) + Momentum Builder (Progressive/Elaborative) = 4 distinct families.

## Topic Cluster 07: Budget, DIY & Resource Tradeoffs

**Topic classification:** Implementation/Tradeoffs at personal-scale. **Accountant explicitly excluded** per the guidance file's anti-pattern table (ROI analysis on $15 glow sticks, tiered participation pricing on a birthday) — this is frugal consumer choice within a single household, not financial architecture.

**Selected personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate (core):** Challenges the "budget-friendly" framing itself ("relative to what baseline, for whom") and the assumption that DIY is automatically the right call.
- **Pragmatist (core):** Per-child cost tiers, hourly-value-of-time math for DIY-vs-purchased, and the "single concrete action this week" question are Pragmatist's domain.
- **Constraint Flipper (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Implementation/Ops):** Budget constraints, time constraints, and the DIY-cascade compression are exactly what Constraint Flipper reframes as design opportunities — leveraging existing parental capabilities and household assets rather than spending them away.
- **Simplifier (Inner Ring — Strong Fit for Implementation; Tension Pair complement):** Identifies the "three highest-value elements" and the leverage point that "if professionally handled or simplified is the leverage point that most reliably de-stresses overall preparation" — subtractive thinking is the explicit ask.

**Family-balance check:** DA (Challenge) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) + Constraint Flipper (Challenge) + Simplifier (Progressive/Elaborative) = 3 families with Challenge at 2 of 4. Within limit.
