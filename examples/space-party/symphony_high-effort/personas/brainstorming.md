### Cluster 01: What "Space" Means to This 5-Year-Old

**Topic classification:** Creative/Whimsical with strong user-facing emotional-resonance dimension (the child's mental model and emotional register).

**Personas selected (7):**
- Devil's Advocate (Core)
- Pragmatist (Core)
- Empath (Inner Ring) — questions hinge on a 5-year-old's gut-level feelings (wonder vs. adventure, what she actually loves about space); Empath is the strongest fit for emotional-register topics.
- Visionary (Inner Ring) — Q3/Q4 ask whether "space" is even the right vehicle and what feeling we should design for; Visionary is built for paradigm-shift reframes.
- Provocateur (Middle Ring) — Q4 explicitly invites absurdist reframing ("space is the wrong metaphor"); completes the Challenge Triad with DA.
- Storyteller (Middle Ring) — child-mental-model questions need named-character lived-experience narratives ("what does she actually draw on") to surface authentic vocabulary.
- Constraint Flipper (Middle Ring) — closes the Challenge Triad and reframes the "space = inhospitable" critique as a productive design constraint rather than a defeater.

**Constraint check:** Challenge Triad = 3 (DA, Provocateur, CF) — at limit, OK. No retired personas.
