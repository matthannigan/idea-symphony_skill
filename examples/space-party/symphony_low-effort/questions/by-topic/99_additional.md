# Topic Cluster 99: Additional Questions

## Questions

1. **Deliberate mission failure as the premise**: Imagine the party's central narrative was that the rocket *crashes* on an alien planet and the children must survive and solve problems together rather than following a planned schedule of activities. The absurd version: there is no backup plan — if the kids don't "solve" the crisis, there's no cake. The real insight: what if structured unpredictability and collective problem-solving were more memorable and developmentally rich than a smooth, orchestrated event?
