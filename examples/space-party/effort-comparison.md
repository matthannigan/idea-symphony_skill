# Idea Symphony — Effort-Level Comparison: Space-Party

**Topic:** Space-themed 5th birthday party for a daughter and 10 friends (11 children total) — a personal/community planning topic, not a product.
**Effort levels covered:** `min`, `low`, `medium`, `high` (all four present).
**Scope:** This comparison reads one `REQUEST.md` (the `high` copy; copies differ only in frontmatter `session-dir`) plus `PLAN.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`, and `BRAINSTORM.md` from each of the four runs. Directory listings of `questions/`, `responses/`, and `synthesis/` were used to source exact counts for §2. `SUMMARIES.md` and `SYNTHESIS.md` were deliberately not read.
**Generated:** 2026-06-10.

---

## 1. Headline Finding

The core thesis survives intact across all four levels and *sharpens* rather than shifts. Every run converges on the same organizing principle: **a five-year-old's memory attaches to one vivid moment and a felt sense of role, not to thematic coverage or decoration count — so concentrate, subtract, and front-load preparation.** `min` already states "concentration beats coverage"; `high` restates it as "subtract rather than add" and "the child is the specification, not the theme." What scales with effort is not *whether* this is true but how rigorously it is interrogated, quantified, and stress-tested.

No `USER-QUESTIONS.md` is present in any run, and every `context/` directory is empty. This is the clean case for a fidelity comparison: each level discovered its material on its own, with no user-supplied questions to confound what "emerged" at a given level. All four worked from the same short `REQUEST.md` and nothing else.

## 4. Where Depth Scales with Effort

### 4.1. Surfacing dissent and tension

`min` reads as one confident voice — it has *one* generic brainstormer per cluster, so its "Central Tensions" section is the synthesizer naming tensions inside agreement, not perspectives in conflict. `low` introduces the first genuine tension by design: two named lenses (a risk-oriented Devil's Advocate and a feasibility-oriented Pragmatist), and its summary explicitly frames "a recurring split runs underneath the practical agreement." `medium` adds texture with a rotating 4-persona panel, surfacing distinct disagreements (e.g. the layout cluster's open "build-up versus front-load" split, left for the host to decide). `high` is the only level that *quantifies* convergence: with 7 lenses and an `attributed/` layer it can report "six of seven lenses independently named the same keystone peak," "six of seven also move cake to mid-party," and carry named non-droppable dissents (the two-adult ratio; the unknowability of memory). That "N of M" signal is structurally impossible below `high`.

### 4.2. Deepening of the critique

Ranked by how radically each level questions the input framing:

1. **`high`** — interrogates the foundation hardest: "what if space is the wrong metaphor and we're solving for the wrong feeling?" is carried as a live edge, and the strip-it-away test asks which elements would *collapse* without the theme. It even questions whether the recommendations can know what a child will remember at all.
2. **`medium`** — reframes the brief ("space is the costume; wonder is the content") and names that the budget-vs-theme tension is "self-imposed," but still treats space as the vehicle.
3. **`low`** — questions *whose* experience the party serves and whether the birthday girl is genuinely enthusiastic, but accepts the space frame.
4. **`min`** — accepts the framing wholesale; its critique is internal (failure modes, conspicuous absences) rather than foundational.

### 4.4. Executive-summary evolution

- **`min`:** Concentration beats coverage; pre-party prep unlocks in-party flexibility; over-programming is the failure mode; helper dependency is the unsolved gap.
- **`low`:** Memory attaches to one engineered moment the birthday girl triggers; good intentions don't survive eleven five-year-olds, so coordination (named jobs) is the lever; a productive risk/feasibility split runs underneath.

## 5. Productive Drift Between Levels

Five zones where recommendations *move* (sharpen, not contradict):

**Budget specificity.** `min` gives per-item dollar estimates ($5 airlock, $15–20 ceiling) but no overall ceiling. `low` introduces a hard ceiling split into non-transferable envelopes (e.g. $200 → $60 food, $40 activities, $30 favors, $50 decoration, $20 buffer). `medium` names a per-child figure ($20–25/child, ~$220–275 total) and adds an hourly-value DIY threshold ($20–30/hr). `high` ties dollars to a compounding logic ("money compounds on one sustained activity and one transformation, ~$50–80; evaporates on themed tableware"). *Direction: from itemized estimates → governed allocation → unit economics → marginal-impact reasoning.*

**The mission narrative.** Absent as a spine at `min` (a "named mission" is a personalization touch). `low` commits early to an "astronauts on a mission" arc as a decision test. `medium` elevates it to "the master mechanism" doing the most work for the least cost. `high` formalizes it into a one-page mission spine (briefing → training → launch → return) with crew roles mailed days ahead — and simultaneously names its limit (it can bore or sideline edge-case children). *Direction: from decoration → decision-test → master mechanism → formalized-but-bounded structure.*

**Centering the birthday girl.** `min`: involve her in one pre-party task. `low`: she activates the wow moment. `medium`: "authorship over assignment," every privilege droppable. `high`: reframe her as "mission commander who gives orders," but only if the authority is *real* and she wants it — faked command is worse than honest celebration. *Direction: from participation → activation → authorship → conditional empowerment.*

**Inclusion.** `min`: a labeled plain-fruit fallback bowl. `low`: "universal beats targeted," cross-contamination flagged. `medium`: "built-in variation, not bolted-on," two identical-looking cakes from day one. `high`: curb-cut framing — the inclusive option is the universal default, and success is "a child who feels known," not one converted into enthusiasm. *Direction: from fallback → universal-by-design → variation-from-the-start → redefining the goal of inclusion.*

**Supervision / adult capacity.** `min`: three adults, written role cards (helper dependency named as the unsolved gap). `low`: design against the confirmed floor, named jobs. `medium`: named visible roles beat more bodies (bystander diffusion). `high`: a carried dissent that two adults may be *unsafe* for eleven five-year-olds and ~four in rotating zones may be needed — promoted to next-step #1. *Direction: from "assume help" → design-against-floor → roles-over-headcount → contested-as-a-safety-question.*

## 6. Fidelity Check — What Gets Lost at Lower Levels

What appears only at `low`+ or only at `high`:

- **The risk/feasibility analytic split** is absent at `min` (one voice) and present from `low` onward. `min` cannot show *why* a recommendation is robust because nothing contests it.
- **Cross-contamination as a distinct hazard** (a reaction with no allergen in any dish) appears from `low`; `min`'s allergy strategy stops at ingredient avoidance plus a fallback bowl.
- **The single mission narrative as the structural spine** is a `medium`+ insight. At `min` and `low` a mission exists as framing/decision-test; only `medium` and `high` treat it as the master mechanism that ties theme, zones, transitions, and the adult-learning layer together.
- **Quantified convergence ("six of seven lenses")** exists only at `high` — the strongest possible confidence signal, and one lower levels structurally cannot emit.
- **The supervision ratio reframed as a safety question** is `high`-only. `min` *names* helper dependency as a gap but treats three adults as sufficient; only `high` argues two may be unsafe and four may be needed, and elevates it to the first action.
- **The epistemic counter-test on memory** ("you cannot know what she will remember; persistent traces become upkeep that can disappoint") is `high`-only and meaningfully tempers the otherwise-confident "design one peak" advice that all four levels carry.
- **Multi-zone "planet per room," the strip-it-away test, and curb-cut inclusion** are all `high`-only structural reframes.

The catch-all `99_additional` cluster appears only at `low`, where its single provocative question ("deliberate mission failure / crashed rocket premise") receives the same brainstorm-and-synthesize treatment as named clusters and earns its own `BRAINSTORM.md` topic block. `min`, `medium`, and `high` have no such cluster.

## 7. When Each Level Earns Its Cost

**`min`** is best for a fast, single-voice orientation when you already trust the framing and want a coherent, actionable digest. It produced a clean "concentration beats coverage" thesis and 15 concrete next steps from one generic pass. Its characteristic weakness is that nothing contests its claims: it states helper dependency as an unsolved gap but does not pressure-test the two-to-three-adult assumption baked into its own recommendations, and its critique never reaches the framing itself.

**`low`** is best when you want genuine tension at low cost — the Devil's Advocate / Pragmatist pairing reliably surfaces the first real disagreement (verify enthusiasm vs. just execute; design-against-failure vs. close-enough staging) and adds practical safety depth (cross-contamination, budget envelopes). Its weakness is breadth-without-spine: with only two lenses it identifies the mission arc as a decision-test but not yet as the organizing mechanism, and its tensions are a single axis rather than a field.

**`medium`** is best for a balanced working plan: four rotating lenses produce the load-bearing "space is the costume" reframe, a usable money picture, and the mission-narrative-as-spine insight, with an `attributed/` trail for traceability. Its weakness is that it resolves several tensions by handing them back to the host ("decide build-up vs. front-load based on your guest list," "name your own leverage element") rather than working them through — defensible, but it leaves real decisions open.

**`high`** earns its 77-response cost when the framing itself deserves scrutiny and you want quantified confidence plus carried dissent. It is the only level that interrogates whether space is even the right theme, quantifies convergence, flags the supervision ratio as a possible safety failure, and tempers its own advice with an epistemic counter-test. Its characteristic weakness is volume relative to marginal novelty: much of its content restates `medium` with more rigor, and a reader who only needs the plan (not the confidence and the edges) pays for depth they may not use.

## 8. Final Summary

- **Preservation:** The central thesis — concentrate on one engineered peak, subtract theming, front-load prep, name adult roles, center the child as agent, mind transitions, cap stimulation, gather allergy/sensory info early — is present and intact at all four levels. Lowest-to-highest fidelity is high.
- **Scaling:** Effort buys depth almost entirely in Phase 3 (5 → 20 → 36 → 77 persona responses). Clusters (5 → 10 → 9 → 11) and next-steps (15 everywhere) stay roughly flat; what grows is interrogation, quantification, and traceability.
- **Divergences:** Recommendations sharpen rather than contradict across five zones — budget (estimates → unit economics), the mission narrative (decoration → formalized spine), the birthday girl (participation → conditional command), inclusion (fallback → curb-cut default), and supervision (assume help → contested safety question).
- **Irreplaceable high-effort insights:** Quantified convergence ("six of seven lenses"), the supervision ratio reframed as a safety dissent and promoted to next-step #1, the strip-it-away test, curb-cut inclusion, and the epistemic counter-test that you cannot know what a five-year-old will remember. These do not exist below `high` and are the clearest case for paying its cost.
- **Confound check:** None. No run contains `USER-QUESTIONS.md` and every `context/` directory is empty, so each level's findings are its own.
