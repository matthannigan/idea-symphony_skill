# Topic Cluster 02: Activities and Engagement

## Questions

5. **Age-appropriate participation**: What kinds of structured activities and games translate the space theme into experiences that five-year-olds can genuinely participate in — not just watch — given their attention spans and motor skills?
6. **Mission or narrative arc**: Would organizing the party around a simple "space mission" story or sequence of challenges improve engagement, and if so, what's the simplest version of that structure that still feels magical?
7. **Hands-on crafts**: What space-themed craft activities give children a tangible take-home creation while being completable in 15–20 minutes with minimal adult facilitation per child?
8. **Managing the group**: How do you design activities for a group of eleven five-year-olds so that no one is waiting too long, no one feels left out, and the pace stays lively without becoming chaotic?
9. **Outdoor vs. indoor tradeoffs**: If outdoor space is available, how can it be incorporated into space-themed activities — and what contingency plans keep the party on track if weather or space constraints change?
