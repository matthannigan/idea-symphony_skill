# Topic Cluster 02: Business Model, Financial Sustainability, and Scaling

## Questions

6. **Fee structure and competitive positioning**: What management fee percentage or hybrid model (flat fee plus commission, tiered by service level) would cover costs at 5-10 properties while remaining competitive against platform-native management tools and larger regional operators who can spread overhead more thinly?
7. **Break-even and cash flow by season**: In a coastal tourist market with pronounced seasonality, how should the company model cash flow to ensure it can fund fixed costs—insurance, software, staff—through the off-season on revenue generated during peak months, and what credit or reserve strategy covers the gap?
8. **Capital deployment priorities**: With $80K in combined startup capital, what sequence of expenditures—legal entity formation, software stack, initial insurance, marketing, and working capital reserve—maximizes the probability of reaching profitability before the runway runs out?
9. **Scaling from 10 to 50 properties**: What operational bottlenecks (cleaning coordination, maintenance dispatch, guest communication response times) will become the binding constraint as the portfolio grows, and how should staffing and vendor relationships be structured from day one to avoid rebuilding them at scale?
10. **Pricing and revenue optimization**: What dynamic pricing approach—whether proprietary, third-party tool, or a blend—would let the company demonstrably outperform self-managed listings on revenue-per-available-night, and how does that performance become the primary trust-building metric in sales conversations with prospective property owners?
