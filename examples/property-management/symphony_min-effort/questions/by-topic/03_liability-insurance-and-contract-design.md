# Topic Cluster 03: Liability, Insurance, and Contract Design

## Questions

11. **Liability allocation in owner agreements**: In the management contract with property owners, how should liability for guest injuries, property damage caused by guests, and contractor negligence be allocated between the company and the owner—and what indemnification language and insurance requirements make that allocation defensible and insurable?
12. **Insurance stack design**: What combination of general liability, property damage, errors and omissions, and umbrella coverage should the company carry, and how does that coverage interact with the property owners' homeowner or landlord policies and platform-provided host protection programs to avoid gaps or double-counting?
13. **Contractor risk management**: Since cleaning and maintenance contractors enter properties directly and interact with guests, what vetting standards, written agreements, and insurance requirements reduce the company's exposure if a contractor causes injury, theft, or property damage?
14. **Termination and dispute resolution**: What termination clause structure—notice periods, cause thresholds, fee clawback provisions, transition assistance obligations—protects both the company's revenue continuity and the property owner's right to exit without being locked into a poor-performing management relationship?
