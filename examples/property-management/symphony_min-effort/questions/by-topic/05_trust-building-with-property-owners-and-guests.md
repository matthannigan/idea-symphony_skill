# Topic Cluster 05: Trust-Building with Property Owners and Guests

## Questions

18. **Owner trust and transparency**: What reporting cadence, financial transparency tools, and communication practices would give property owners enough visibility into occupancy, revenue, and expenses to feel genuinely confident that their asset is being managed well—especially in the early stage when the company lacks a track record?
19. **Guest experience as a differentiator**: Beyond standard hospitality basics, what distinctive elements of the guest experience—local guides, frictionless check-in, proactive communication, issue resolution speed—would drive five-star reviews that compound into higher search rankings and justify the company's management premium?
20. **Handling difficult situations publicly**: When a guest complaint, property incident, or negative review becomes visible on a platform or in local media, what response protocol preserves trust with both the affected property owner and the broader owner prospect pool who may be watching how the company handles problems?
