# Request: Short-Term Rental Property Management Company

**Request Date:** 2026-02-24

## Topic
Starting a short-term rental management company that handles Airbnb/VRBO listings for property owners in a tourist-heavy coastal city.

## Context
- **Business Model:** Full-service management (listing optimization, guest communication, cleaning, maintenance, pricing) for a percentage of booking revenue
- **Market:** Coastal city with ~800 active short-term rental listings; city recently passed new STR regulations with registration requirements and occupancy taxes
- **Starting Scale:** 5-10 properties in year one, targeting 30-50 by year three
- **Founders:** Two partners — one with hospitality background, one with real estate experience
- **Capital:** $80K combined savings for startup costs
- **Regulatory Landscape:** New city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time; state legislature considering statewide preemption of local STR bans; homeowner associations in several target neighborhoods have their own STR restrictions
- **Community Dynamics:** Significant tension between STR advocates (property owners, tourism businesses) and opponents (long-term renters concerned about housing costs, neighbors concerned about noise/parking); city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue

## Goals
Design a property management company that navigates the complex regulatory and community landscape while building a profitable, scalable operation that property owners trust with their investments.

## Key Considerations
- Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence
- Contract design with property owners (liability allocation, termination clauses, insurance requirements)
- Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting
- Zoning compliance and permit management across multiple neighborhood zones
- Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow
- Insurance requirements (general liability, property damage, errors & omissions)
- Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)
- Positioning the company as a "good neighbor" operation vs. absentee management stereotype
- Building trust with both property owners and the surrounding community
