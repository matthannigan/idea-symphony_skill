# Brainstorming Questions from The Analyst: Short-Term Rental Property Management Company

## Cluster 1: Unit Economics and Financial Architecture

**Break-even per property**: Breaking this down into its component parts — what does the cost structure actually look like per managed property (onboarding, ongoing labor, software, insurance allocation, and compliance overhead), and at what management fee percentage and average nightly rate does a single property reach monthly break-even?

**Seasonal cash flow management**: The key factors in coastal STR revenue are heavily seasonal — what does the cash flow distribution across months look like when you account for occupancy variance, cleaning volume spikes, and fixed overhead, and how much working capital buffer does $80K actually provide before the portfolio reaches self-sustaining revenue?

**Fee structure trade-offs**: Looking at the two primary fee models (flat percentage vs. tiered percentage with performance bonuses), what are the quantified trade-offs in terms of predictable revenue for the company versus alignment with property owner incentives, and which structure accelerates owner acquisition from 5-10 to 30-50 properties more reliably?

**Scale threshold analysis**: At what portfolio size — expressed in number of properties — does the operation tip from founder-labor-intensive to requiring dedicated staff hires, and what does the corresponding step-change in fixed costs do to the per-property margin curve between year one and year three?

## Cluster 2: Regulatory Complexity Mapping

**Multi-jurisdiction compliance load**: Breaking this down by layer — city STR ordinance, neighborhood-zone permit caps, HOA restrictions, state sales tax, municipal occupancy tax, federal income reporting — how should the company systematically map and track compliance requirements across each property, and which layer carries the highest operational risk if it changes mid-season?

**Permit cap constraint modeling**: The city limits permits per neighborhood zone, which constrains the addressable property pool in any given area — what analytical method would you use to model the effective portfolio ceiling under current zone caps, and how does a potential state preemption law shift that ceiling in quantifiable terms?

**HOA restriction identification**: Given that several target neighborhoods have HOA-level STR restrictions layered on top of city rules, what due-diligence process and data sources would allow the company to reliably screen prospective owner-clients before onboarding, rather than discovering conflicts after signing?

## Cluster 3: Operational Systems and Service Delivery

**30-minute response requirement decomposition**: The city ordinance mandates a local contact person available within 30 minutes — decomposing this operationally, what staffing model (W-2 on-call staff, subcontractor network, or founder coverage) minimizes cost per incident while maintaining reliable compliance across a portfolio that spans multiple zones?

**Cleaning and maintenance vendor structure**: At 5-10 properties the founders can likely self-manage vendor relationships, but scaling to 30-50 requires a different structure — what are the critical variables for deciding between a captive cleaning team versus a vetted vendor network, and how does each option affect per-turn cost, quality consistency, and liability exposure?

**Technology stack leverage points**: Looking at the operational workflow — dynamic pricing, channel management, guest communication, maintenance ticketing, owner reporting — which of these functions offers the highest return on automation investment at the early stage given the $80K capital constraint, and what does a sequenced build-out look like as revenue grows?

## Cluster 4: Owner Acquisition and Retention Dynamics

**Owner decision criteria mapping**: The key factors property owners weigh when choosing a manager likely include fee structure, occupancy performance, communication frequency, and regulatory peace of mind — what data or proxies would let the founders rank-order these criteria for the specific coastal city market before committing to a positioning strategy?

**Trust signal sequencing**: Breaking down the owner acquisition funnel for an unproven startup — what is the minimum credible evidence package (track record proxies, insurance documentation, reference network, sample reporting) that converts a skeptical first-time owner, and how does that package need to evolve as the company grows from 5 to 30 properties?

## Cluster 5: Community and Political Risk Assessment

**Regulatory scenario analysis**: The city council election in six months creates a binary regulatory environment — STR rules could tighten, loosen, or remain stable — what does a structured scenario analysis of these three outcomes look like in terms of impact on addressable portfolio size, compliance cost, and company valuation, and which operational decisions are robust across all three scenarios?
