# Brainstorming Questions from The Lawyer: Short-Term Rental Property Management Company

---

## Entity Structure and Liability Exposure

1. **Liability architecture**: Given that your management company will face simultaneous exposure from guest injuries, property damage caused by guests, contractor negligence, and owner disputes over revenue performance, what entity structure — LLC, S-Corp, or layered holding structure — most effectively compartmentalizes these distinct liability vectors, and which categories of exposure require separate insurance policy lines rather than structural solutions?

2. **Contract indemnification design**: The management agreement between your company and property owners must allocate liability for guest injuries, property damage, and regulatory violations — but where the allocation lands determines whether your company absorbs or transfers risk it cannot fully control; what specific indemnification clauses, hold-harmless provisions, and insurance mandates should the property owner agreement include to ensure that owner negligence (deferred maintenance, undisclosed HOA restrictions) does not create uninsured exposure for your company?

---

## Regulatory Compliance Architecture

3. **Multi-jurisdiction permit compliance**: The city STR ordinance creates permit quotas by neighborhood zone, mandates noise monitoring equipment, and requires a local contact person within 30 minutes — what compliance management system (tracking permit expiration dates, zone-specific cap status, contact-person rotation schedules, and noise monitoring certification) is necessary to avoid operating properties out of compliance, and how does permit-denial risk factor into your property onboarding contract with owners?

4. **HOA restriction discovery and liability**: Multiple target neighborhoods have HOA rules restricting or prohibiting STR activity that exist independently of city ordinance, are not publicly indexed, and may not be disclosed by property owners at onboarding — what due diligence protocol (CC&R review, HOA board inquiry, estoppel letter request) must your company conduct before taking on a property, and what contractual mechanism shifts liability to the owner if an undisclosed HOA restriction forces you to deactivate a listing post-launch?

5. **State preemption contingency planning**: The state legislature is considering statewide preemption of local STR bans, which could expand your addressable market but also invalidate the existing city permit framework mid-operation — what contract language protects your company if the regulatory ground shifts (city permits become invalid, or conversely, the preemption bill fails and the city tightens enforcement), and how should permit-dependent management agreements be structured to avoid stranded contractual obligations?

---

## Tax Compliance and Reporting Obligations

6. **Occupancy tax collection and remittance**: Your company collects booking revenue on behalf of property owners and must remit municipal transient occupancy tax (TOT), state sales tax on accommodations, and potentially county taxes — as the collecting agent, what is your company's direct liability exposure if a property owner disputes a remittance or if a platform (Airbnb, VRBO) collects and remits taxes independently but incorrectly, and how does your management agreement define the tax compliance role and indemnify your company against owner tax disputes with the city?

7. **Federal income reporting and 1099 obligations**: As a property management company receiving and disbursing rental proceeds, you have Form 1099-MISC reporting obligations to property owners for amounts paid, and property owners have their own Schedule E reporting obligations — what documentation and reporting architecture (monthly disbursement statements, year-end 1099 issuance, deductible expense categorization) must your company build into operations by launch to satisfy IRS requirements and avoid penalty exposure under 26 U.S.C. § 6721 for failure to file correct information returns?

---

## Data Privacy and Guest Consent

8. **Guest data collection and consent obligations**: Your company will collect guest identity information (for city-mandated registration compliance), payment data, and behavioral data (noise monitoring logs, check-in/check-out records) — which of these data categories triggers specific retention limits or consent requirements under California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) if you operate in a California coastal city, and what privacy notice, opt-out mechanism, and data retention policy must be in place before you process your first booking?

---

## Insurance Coverage and Risk Transfer

9. **Insurance gap analysis across policy lines**: STR property management creates overlapping but non-contiguous risks — general liability covers third-party bodily injury on premises, property damage coverage protects physical assets, errors and omissions (E&O) covers your management decisions and professional negligence, and platform host protection policies (Airbnb AirCover) have exclusions and sublimits that may leave material gaps; what minimum coverage levels and policy coordination structure (primary vs. excess, named insured vs. additional insured for property owners) should your company require, and which policy line covers the scenario where a contractor you hired negligently damages a guest's property during a mid-stay repair?
