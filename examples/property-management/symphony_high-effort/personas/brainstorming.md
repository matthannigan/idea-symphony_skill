## Per-Topic Rationale

### Topic Cluster 01: Foundational Value Proposition and Fee Architecture

**Classification:** Strategic/Vision (Q1 "what owners are actually buying" — irreducibles), Financial/Business (Q2 — fee structure as incentive design across occupancy distribution), Creative/Whimsical (Q3 — "living collective" with no analog).

**Selected (7):** Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, First Principles Thinker, Visionary, Futurist, Provocateur, Accountant

**Rationale:**
- **First Principles Thinker (Inner):** Q1 explicitly asks for the "irreducible thing" being purchased — pure FPT territory.
- **Visionary (Inner):** Q3 demands a paradigm shift ("no current analog in real estate or hospitality"); strategic reframing of the relationship itself.
- **Futurist (Middle):** Completes the Forward-Looking Pair with Visionary; grounds fee-architecture redesign in trend-based extrapolation (modular service menus, hybrid retainer models).
- **Provocateur (Middle):** Completes the Challenge Triad (DA core); Q3's "living collective" framing invites absurdist provocation against industry convention.
- **Accountant (Middle, Outer Ring):** Q2 is genuinely financial — incentive math across the 85%/40% occupancy distribution, fixed-cost floors, percentage-of-revenue vs. flat retainer modeling. Explicitly warranted by the topic.

**Family-cap check:** Pragmatist + FPT (analytical core) + Accountant (specialized lens) = 3 in the analytical+specialized family. At cap, not over.

- **Systems Thinker (Middle):** Multi-jurisdiction stack (city + HOA + state + federal) is a layered system with feedback loops — Q5's "compliance signals legitimacy → triggers more regulation" loop is explicit.
- **Connector (Middle):** Q7 (medieval guilds), Q8 (environmental consultants) both ask for structural parallels in other regulated professions.
- **Analogist (Middle):** Completes External Perspectives Triad with Systems Thinker + Connector; imports concrete tactics from licensed-profession barriers and EPA-consultant practice models.

**Family-cap check:** Pragmatist + Lawyer + Politician = 3 in analytical+specialized family. At cap.
