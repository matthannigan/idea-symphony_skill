# Topic Cluster 08: Validation Steps Before Committing

## Questions

1. **Minimum viable test of legal-fit**: Before committing to a three-year, six-figure investment, what is the smallest meaningful test you could run in the next 6-18 months — a sustained volunteer or paid role in direct legal services, deepening into immigration paralegal work, case management, or accredited-representative advocacy — that would generate enough real signal to distinguish genuine aptitude and satisfaction in legal practice from an idealized projection of what that work is like, and what would you be looking for to know what you found?
2. **What "not crazy" actually requires — milestones to "go"**: You asked whether this is a "crazy idea" — but what would the version of this plan that is definitively not crazy actually look like, and what specific information, milestones, or test results would have to be true (LSAT score range, scholarship offer, validated sustained interest, financial floor met, family alignment) before you hit "go" with full confidence rather than as an escape from something painful?
3. **The support structure that makes it real**: You have a supportive family, two years of resettlement volunteer work, and eight years of high-stakes clinical experience — who in your existing network has already navigated a transition like this, or is doing the kind of work you imagine, and what would it take to be in genuine conversation with them before you decide?
