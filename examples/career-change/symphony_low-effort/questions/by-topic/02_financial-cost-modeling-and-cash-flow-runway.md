# Topic Cluster 02: Financial Cost Modeling and Cash-Flow Runway

## Questions

1. **All-in transition cost model**: Law school at $50–80K/year at a mid-tier public interest-friendly school (or $60–100K at a top-14) puts the three-year direct cost at $150–240K in tuition alone — before living expenses. With $45K in savings and $82K in foregone salary annually, the total economic outlay (foregone income + living costs + tuition, net of savings) could reach $350–450K. What is your complete cost model year by year — tuition draws, living expenses, savings as runway — and which variables (school selectivity, in-state tuition eligibility, living arrangement, part-time work) have the biggest leverage on that number?
2. **Existing debt compounding while income is zero**: The $22K in nursing school loans will accrue interest across three years without income, compounding while law school debt is added on top. What is the realistic total debt load on day one of your first legal job — nursing loans, law school loans, and accumulated interest — and how would those obligations interact with income-driven repayment calculations and the PSLF timeline?
3. **Savings runway and monthly burn rate**: $45K in savings is a meaningful cushion but covers roughly 1–1.5 years at $30–45K annual living costs, not three years. What is your actual monthly burn rate today, how would it change in law school across different cost-of-living markets, and what minimum part-time income or fellowship income would be needed to avoid exhausting the buffer entirely before graduation?
4. **Part-time income realism during 1L–3L**: Many law schools restrict outside employment during 1L year, and JD demands make consistent outside income difficult; some students earn $15–25K through 2L/3L summer firm internships or fellowships. How much of the funding gap are you counting on part-time or summer income to close, how firm is that assumption, and what are the structural constraints (clinical scheduling, licensing, evening/part-time JD program availability) on maintaining per-diem ICU shifts or travel nursing during school?
