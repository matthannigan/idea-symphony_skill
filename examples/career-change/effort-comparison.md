## 1. Headline Finding

The core thesis survives intact across all four levels, and it is the same single claim at every effort: **the decision cannot be made by reasoning from the current burned-out state — the burnout must be cleared first, then the work must be tested cheaply and reversibly before any capital (three years, ~$250K) is committed.** Every level reaches this; every level pairs it with the same financial spine (PSLF is load-bearing but fragile; model the zero-forgiveness floor) and the same asset verdict (nursing is a narrow, conditional differentiator, not a general-purpose one).

## 5. Productive Drift Between Levels

Five zones where recommendations sharpen (not contradict) as effort rises.

**A. The "test the work" prescription.** `min`: observe an attorney for ~20 hours including one denial. `low`: 6 months of unglamorous document prep at a high-volume clinic. `medium`: a 12-month accredited-representative/paralegal trial at real intensity alongside per-diem nursing. `high`: a full-responsibility immersion (10–20 hrs/wk for 6 months), explicitly *not* shadowing, with the unglamorous daily grind done *first*, LSAT and interviews later. *Direction:* from passive observation → toward sustained, full-responsibility, sequenced-first immersion.

**B. The debt number.** `min`: "$150K+." `low`: a $155K–$330K range driven by three controllable choices. `medium`: a concrete ~$250–280K realistic landing. `high`: a prescribed $170–180K *debt ceiling* that filters school choice. *Direction:* from a floor estimate → toward an actionable budget constraint that drives decisions.

**C. The savings buffer.** `min`: treated as part of the picture. `low`: "$45K is operating capital, not a cushion." `medium`: "a four-to-six-month float that erodes to $40K before law school." `high`: the "false cushion" — covers only ~18 of 36 months; model months 19–36 explicitly. *Direction:* from asset → toward a quantified, time-bounded liability window.

**D. Alternatives to the JD.** `min`: BIA accreditation as a possible full substitute. `low`: DOJ accreditation, MSW, MLP roles, with a warning that intermediate paths can become permanent deferral. `medium`: hybrid roles with a modeled ~$200K JD premium, rational only for court/leadership/practice ownership. `high`: same paths reframed as an *evidence-gathering instrument* with an explicit year 3–4 decision checkpoint to prevent "delay" drifting into "never." *Direction:* from option → toward a gated experimental design.

**E. Who gets served.** `min`: silent (named as a conspicuous absence). `low`: flags a distributional gap (coastal concentration vs. the ICU population). `medium`: prescribes structured community listening *with veto power* before law school. `high`: elevates it to the session's premise-puncturing finding — attorney supply may not be the bottleneck at all. *Direction:* from absence → toward the single most decision-altering insight in the highest run.

## 7. When Each Level Earns Its Cost

**`min`** — Best for a fast, single-voice gut-check that still produces a defensible, well-sequenced answer and a usable 15-item action list. Its characteristic weakness is that it accepts the request's framing: it never asks whether "law or nursing" is the right question, and it presents contestable claims (PSLF, niche value) with more certainty than a multi-perspective run would.

**`low`** — Best when you want genuine tension surfaced cheaply: the two-lens (risk vs. feasibility) structure introduces real disagreement and the "separate the two decisions" reframe at a fraction of medium's cost. Its weakness is that with only two brainstormers it can name a split but cannot weigh it — the texture of *why* perspectives diverge stays thin, and emotional/identity dimensions are flagged as absent rather than developed.

**`medium`** — Best for a decision someone is seriously preparing to act on: four perspectives per cluster produce reframes "raised independently by all four," the financial modeling gets concrete numbers, and new load-bearing concepts (economic tail, dignity-as-infrastructure, community veto) appear. Its weakness is that it stops short of interrogating the premise itself and cannot yet quantify how broad its convergence is.

**`high`** — Best when the stakes justify exhaustive triangulation and you want the framing itself stress-tested: seven perspectives per cluster let it quantify convergence, surface inverting skills, and deliver the premise-puncturing "supply isn't the bottleneck" finding. Its weakness is volume and cost (77 response files) for a personal decision, and — by its own admission — it leaves the comparator "stay and reshape nursing" path underdeveloped relative to the law path it scrutinized so heavily.
