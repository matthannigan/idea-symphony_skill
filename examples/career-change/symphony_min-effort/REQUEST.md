# Request: Career Change from ICU Nursing to Public Interest Law

**Request Date:** 2026-02-24

## Topic
I'm considering leaving my career as an ICU nurse to become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees. Is this a crazy idea?

## Context
- **Current Career:** 8 years as an ICU nurse at a Level 1 trauma center
- **Motivation:** Burned out from bedside nursing but deeply committed to helping vulnerable populations; repeatedly saw immigrant patients navigating impossible situations with no legal support; volunteered with a refugee resettlement organization for 2 years
- **Education:** BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing); would need to complete an LSAT, 3-year JD program, and pass the bar
- **Financial Situation:** $22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year
- **Personal:** 32 years old, unmarried, no children; supportive family but they think this is risky

## Goals
Think through whether this career transition makes sense — financially, professionally, and personally — and what the path would actually look like if I decided to pursue it.

## Key Considerations
- Is this a genuine calling or burnout-driven escape?
- Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary
- Whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law
- Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers
- Timeline and opportunity cost at age 32
- Alternative paths that combine healthcare and immigrant advocacy without a full career change
- Emotional sustainability: trading one high-burnout field for another
