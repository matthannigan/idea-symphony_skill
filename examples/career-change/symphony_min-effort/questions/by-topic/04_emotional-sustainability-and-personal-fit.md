# Topic Cluster 04: Emotional Sustainability and Personal Fit

## Questions

16. **Burnout in law**: Public interest legal work carries its own burnout profile — crushing caseloads, underfunding, vicarious trauma from clients in crisis, and systemic losses — what makes you confident this would be more sustainable than bedside nursing rather than equally or more draining?
17. **Relationship to uncertainty**: Legal outcomes are probabilistic and often outside the lawyer's control; nursing interventions have more direct physical feedback loops — how do you relate to working in a domain where your best effort frequently ends in deportation or denial?
18. **Family and social context**: Your family sees this as risky — what specific concerns do they have, how much does their assessment weigh in your decision, and what would you need to demonstrate to yourself (not them) to feel confident moving forward?
19. **Support structures**: What does your current social and professional network look like in the legal world — do you know immigration attorneys, former patients who navigated the system, advocates who could mentor you — and how would you build that network intentionally?
