- **Constraint Flipper** (Middle — completes Challenge Triad) — Q6 reframes burnout as training rather than obstacle; CF specializes in inverting apparent constraints.
- **First Principles Thinker** (Middle — strategic deconstruction) — Q3 ("why law, not adjacent vehicles") and Q7 ("strip away career-change framing") explicitly require reasoning from values primitives, not from the JD-shaped default.

**Family-cap check:** Pragmatist + FPT = 2 from Analytical Core. Empath = 1 from Human-Centered. DA + Provocateur + Constraint Flipper = 3 from Challenge Triad (cap). Systems Thinker = 1 from External Perspectives. Within limits.
