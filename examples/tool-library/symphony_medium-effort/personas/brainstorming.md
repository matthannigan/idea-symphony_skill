## Per-Topic Detail

### Topic Cluster 01: Vision, Purpose, and What This Is Fundamentally For
**Topic type:** Strategic/Vision with Novel/Ambiguous overtones (questions reframe the library as civic commons, mycelial metabolism, or loneliness intervention)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Visionary** — The cluster explicitly invites paradigm-shift thinking ("living metabolism of the neighborhood," "civic commons identity") that rewards a persona who ignores current reality to articulate what this could fundamentally be.
- **Analogist** — Several questions already gesture toward cross-domain imports (public libraries, repair cafes, cooperatives); the Analogist can ground these by importing concrete structural moves from analogous institutions rather than leaving them as metaphor.

### Topic Cluster 02: Equitable Access and Hidden Barriers
**Topic type:** User-Facing/Community (with equity sub-emphasis)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Audience Advocate** — The cluster is fundamentally about mapping the needs of underserved stakeholder segments (renters, hourly workers, non-English speakers, newcomers) and designing against silent exclusion — Audience Advocate's exact strength.
- **Empath** — Barriers like "fear of judgment" and "distrust of institutions" are emotional rather than logistical; the Empath surfaces the felt experience that makes a barrier invisible to designers but decisive to potential users.

### Topic Cluster 03: Renter and Homeowner Realities
**Topic type:** User-Facing/Community (segment-comparison)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Audience Advocate** — The cluster is structured around two distinct stakeholder profiles whose divergent needs must be mapped explicitly; this is canonical Audience Advocate territory.
- **Storyteller** — Two of the three questions are explicitly scene-based ("Walk through the full journey of a renter," "Set two scenes side by side"), which calls for the Storyteller's named-character, lived-experience narrative mode rather than abstract analysis.

### Topic Cluster 04: Trust, Dignity, and the Borrower Experience
**Topic type:** User-Facing/Community (felt-experience design)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Empath** — The cluster centers on dignity, vulnerability, stigma, and the felt arc from stranger to regular — emotional resonance is the cluster's primary axis and the Empath's defining strength.
- **Storyteller** — The cluster includes named-character scenes (Maria peering through the window) and asks for the experience curve through specific moments; the Storyteller brings these to life as concrete narratives rather than abstract policy.

### Topic Cluster 05: Volunteer Sustainability and Succession
**Topic type:** Implementation/Ops with strong systems-dynamics overlay (burnout cliffs, reciprocity decay, keystone species)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Systems Thinker** — The cluster explicitly frames volunteer energy as a feedback system: coordination burden grows non-linearly with membership, keystone-species dynamics, reciprocity decay over time. Systems Thinker maps these reinforcing/balancing loops directly.
- **Momentum Builder** — Implementation cluster's progressive-elaboration persona; succession structures, recognition rituals, and role designs are exactly the kind of forward-built scaffolding Momentum Builder generates by chaining "yes, and" moves into concrete sequenced practices.

### Topic Cluster 06: Tool Inventory, Demand, and the Commons
**Topic type:** Implementation/Ops with strategic-curation and systems-dynamics elements
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Constraint Flipper** — The cluster is dominated by tight constraints (500 sq ft, modest budget, Tragedy of the Commons) and one question explicitly inverts scarcity into abundance ("800 tools dropped in your lap"); Constraint Flipper turns these limits into design features rather than obstacles.
- **Systems Thinker** — Tragedy of the Commons, demand-management feedback loops, leading vs. lagging indicators, and self-rationing dynamics are textbook stock-and-flow problems the Systems Thinker is built to model.

### Topic Cluster 07: Operations, Checkout, and Maintenance Systems
**Topic type:** Implementation/Ops (concrete operational systems design)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Momentum Builder** — Building checkout flows, intake checklists, inspection schedules, and reservation systems calls for progressive elaboration of concrete operational steps — Momentum Builder's defining mode.
- **Simplifier** — Volunteers run this; complexity kills volunteer-staffed retail-scale operations. Simplifier strips the design back to the minimum adequate system, generating productive tension with Momentum Builder's elaborative pull and protecting against system bloat.

### Topic Cluster 08: Liability, Damage, and Adjudication
**Topic type:** Regulated/Compliance (with adjudication and trust dimensions)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Lawyer** — This is the one cluster where the Outer Ring legal lens is genuinely warranted: real liability decomposition, insurance coverage adequacy, named risk categories (tool malfunction, user injury, property damage), and questions about whether standard nonprofit GL policy actually covers power tools. Exactly the topic where Lawyer earns its slot.
- **Empath** — The damage-dispute and "broken tool moment" questions hinge on preserving dignity and trust during accusations; without an emotional-resonance persona, the cluster collapses into pure legalism and loses the relational stakes the questions explicitly raise.

### Topic Cluster 09: Financial Sustainability and Space Risk
**Topic type:** Financial/Business (with single-point-of-failure strategic dimension)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Accountant** — Genuinely warranted here: explicit replacement-budget design, break-even membership pricing, sliding-scale deterrence thresholds, grant-cycle dependency modeling, and reserve drawdowns. This is the cluster the Accountant exists for.
- **Futurist** — The cluster repeatedly asks about decay over time (enthusiasm fade in months 6-12, grant treadmill drift, donated-space loss); Futurist's trend-grounded extrapolation builds plausible multi-year financial trajectories the Accountant can then stress-test.

### Topic Cluster 10: Governance, Norms, and Long-Term Mission
**Topic type:** Governance/Political with strong Strategic/Vision overlay (mission drift, paradigm-shifting reframes)
**Personas:**
- **Devil's Advocate** (Core)
- **Pragmatist** (Core)
- **Politician** — Genuinely warranted: governance structures (committee vs. staff lead vs. advisory board), member dispute adjudication, coalition between founders and newcomers, and the Success-to-the-Successful trap are exactly the multi-stakeholder governance dynamics the Politician handles.
- **Visionary** — Two questions explicitly invite paradigm-redefinition (collective stewardship as a new legal/cultural category, the library as proof-of-concept for a non-market neighborhood economy); Visionary articulates these futures rather than discounting them as impractical.
