# Request: Community Tool Lending Library

**Request Date:** 2026-02-18

## Topic
Creating a community tool lending library for a neighborhood nonprofit organization.

## Context
- **Organization:** Small neighborhood nonprofit with modest budget
- **Location:** Urban neighborhood with mix of homeowners and renters
- **Community:** Diverse neighborhood, ~2,000 households within walking distance
- **Space:** Access to a small storefront space (~500 sq ft) donated by a local landlord
- **Volunteers:** Core group of 8-10 committed volunteers

## Goals
Design and launch a sustainable community tool lending library that serves diverse neighborhood needs while building community connections.

## Key Considerations
- Sustainability beyond initial enthusiasm
- Equitable access for all community members
- Tool maintenance and replacement costs
- Liability and safety concerns
- Building community buy-in and participation
