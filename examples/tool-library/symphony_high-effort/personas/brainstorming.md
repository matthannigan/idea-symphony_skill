### Cluster 01: Foundational Purpose & What This Library Actually Is

**Topic type:** Strategic/Vision (with strong First-Principles framing — "what is this actually?")

**Selected personas (7):**
1. **Devil's Advocate** (core)
2. **Pragmatist** (core)
3. **First Principles Thinker** — Inner Ring. Q1's "irreducible unit of value," Q2's "lending vs. alternative mechanisms," and Q4's "remove all the tools and ask what's left" are textbook deconstruction-to-fundamentals prompts. FPT is the natural lens for "why does this need to exist as a library at all?"
4. **Visionary** (Strong Fit for Strategic/Vision) — Q5's "community metabolism" and the cluster's reframings of the library beyond inventory call for paradigm-shift thinking that ignores current constraints.
5. **Provocateur** — Middle Ring. Completes the **Challenge Triad** with DA (core) + CF. Q4's "remove all the tools" already has absurdist DNA; Provocateur's job is to push that absurdity productively.
6. **Constraint Flipper** — Middle Ring. Completes the Challenge Triad. Q2's institutionalization-vs-informality framing is exactly the "constraints as features" reframe CF is built for.
7. **Analogist** — Middle Ring. Q2 explicitly invites comparison to bulk-purchasing co-ops, repair cafés, skill-sharing programs; Q3 asks about existing informal practices. The Analogist imports concrete patterns rather than abstracting.

**Cluster-family check:** Challenge Triad fully completed (DA + Provocateur + CF = 3 from one family — at the cap, not over). FPT (Analytical Core) + Pragmatist (Analytical Core) = 2 from a second family. Visionary (Forward-Looking, solo) and Analogist (External Perspectives, solo) keep families balanced. No family exceeds 3.
