# Topic Cluster 04: Launch Strategy and Community Buy-In

## Questions

16. **Soft launch vs. full launch**: What would a phased or soft-launch strategy look like — starting with a limited tool set and a small pilot group before opening to the full neighborhood — and what specific milestones would signal readiness to expand?
17. **Early adopter recruitment**: Which neighborhood stakeholders (block club leaders, faith communities, local handypersons, school garden coordinators) would make the most credible and effective early champions, and how should they be engaged before the public launch?
18. **Success metrics for year one**: Beyond raw borrowing numbers, what qualitative and quantitative indicators — repeat borrowers, demographic reach, volunteer hours, community feedback — would tell the team whether the library is fulfilling its equity and community-connection goals?
19. **Navigating skepticism and opposition**: Some landlords, neighbors, or community members may have concerns about noise, traffic, liability, or the nonprofit's long-term intentions — what proactive communication and relationship-building strategies could defuse opposition before it becomes an obstacle?
