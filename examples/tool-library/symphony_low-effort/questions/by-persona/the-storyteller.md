# Brainstorming Questions from The Storyteller: Community Tool Lending Library

---

## Arrival and First Encounter

1. **The First-Time Borrower**: Imagine a renter in the neighborhood — someone who has never owned a drill — who hears about the tool library from a neighbor and decides to try it for the first time: what does that entire journey look like, from the moment they walk through the door to the moment they complete their project, and where along the way might anxiety, confusion, or delight shape whether they ever come back?

2. **The Reluctant Donor**: Picture a long-time homeowner who has a garage full of tools they rarely use but feels uncertain about lending out something valuable to strangers — what story would need to unfold, what assurances or moments of trust would need to happen, for them to eventually not just donate a tool but become one of the library's most vocal advocates in the neighborhood?

---

## Moments of Friction and Transformation

3. **The Broken Tool Crisis**: Walk through the scenario where a borrower returns a tool that is damaged — not out of carelessness, but because they genuinely didn't know how to use it properly — and trace how the library's response to that moment either deepens community trust and belonging or quietly discourages participation among people who were already uncertain about whether this resource was really meant for them?
