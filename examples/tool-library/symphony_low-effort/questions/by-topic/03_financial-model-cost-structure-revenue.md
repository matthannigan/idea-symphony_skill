# Topic Cluster 03: Financial Model, Cost Structure & Revenue

## Questions

1. **Tool acquisition and replacement economics**: Breaking the inventory down by category — power tools, hand tools, specialty equipment — what is the realistic cost per tool given a mix of donated versus purchased stock, and when a tool breaks or wears out, who decides repair-versus-replace, who authorizes the spending, and where does that money reliably come from year after year rather than being deferred to "we'll figure it out when it happens"?
2. **Break-even utilization and a stress-tested revenue mix**: What minimum checkout rate per tool per month is needed before maintenance, repair, and replacement costs are offset by membership fees or other revenue; what does a fully-loaded annual budget look like once you include the routinely-underestimated line items (sharpening, cleaning supplies, storage hardware, software subscriptions, insurance premiums); and which two or three revenue streams — membership fees, grants, donation drives, fee-for-service workshops — could realistically sustain the library if initial donor or grant funding dries up after 18 months, without pricing out lower-income residents?
