# Topic Cluster 04: Liability, Safety & Damage Accountability

## Questions

1. **Liability exposure decomposition**: The brief flags liability as a key concern — so what are the distinct risk categories the library faces (tool defect claims, borrower injury, property damage), how does each map to mitigation levers (insurance, waivers, inspection protocols, training requirements), and is the real exposure actually concentrated not in formal borrowing agreements but in informal loans, word-of-mouth sharing, and tools returned in worse condition than anyone checked — with current waivers and coverage actually reviewed by legal counsel?
2. **Damage attribution and dignity at the breaking point**: What inspection cadence and metrics would let the library distinguish normal wear-and-tear depreciation from damage attributable to a specific borrower; what does a fair, consistent, enforceable accountability policy look like when first invoked against a neighbor; and how does the conflict-resolution experience feel from the borrower's side — especially someone already economically vulnerable — so that the policy protects both the inventory and continued participation?
