### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Mobile-app matches the positive swap example for multi-stakeholder product systems verbatim — user needs (180K downloads, 8% retention, "overwhelming" feedback), team capacity (6 engineers, 1 designer, 1 PM, burned out), runway (8 months funding remaining), and codebase (15 deeply integrated half-built features) are structurally linked constraints that must be reconciled for the pruning decision. The reconciliation across these distinct stakeholder/constraint systems IS the brainstorming need, not just context.
- Effort-invariance check: This is a topic-structure decision; the same Connector selection would apply at high effort.
