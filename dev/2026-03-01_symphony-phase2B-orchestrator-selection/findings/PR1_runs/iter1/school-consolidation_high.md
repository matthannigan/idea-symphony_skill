### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The topic requires reconciling three distinct school districts (each with its own union contract, board, budget, and community), state education officials, parent groups, and voter communities across three districts. This meets criterion (b): multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate — districts + unions + communities + state — where governance structure, contract reconciliation, and cross-district voter approval must be designed across them. Effort-invariance check: this decision would be the same at medium effort; it is a topic-structure decision.
