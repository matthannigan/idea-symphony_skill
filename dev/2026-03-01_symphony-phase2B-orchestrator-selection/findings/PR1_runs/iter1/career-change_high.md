### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: REQUEST describes a career transition across two distinct professional/institutional systems — ICU nursing (healthcare clinical practice) and public interest immigration law — that must be reconciled by one person ("leaving my career as an ICU nurse to become a public interest attorney"; "Whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law"). This is the canonical positive swap case (career transitions across fields): the reconciliation of two constraining career systems IS the brainstorming need, satisfying criterion (a). Effort-invariant: would be the same at medium.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year… Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary… Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees… 3-year JD program, and pass the bar"
- Decision: exclude (career-change topics naming "lawyer"/"attorney" as a TARGET career are a documented false positive per R6 — the persona responds to the keyword and produces a regulatory implementation guide rather than decision-exploration questions; the brainstorming need is whether/how to transition careers, not compliance architecture)

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "supportive family but they think this is risky"
- Decision: exclude (no governance, coalition building, institutional change, or stakeholder politics — family dynamics are interpersonal, not political-institutional)

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "(none — no technical architecture content in REQUEST)"
- Decision: exclude (no engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, or ML/AI architecture; career-change legacy-code framing explicitly flagged as FP per R6)

**Notes:** — (Accountant is the only Tier 3 qualifier; ceiling of 2 not binding. Quantified financial modeling is central to the "financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary" question, which goes beyond consumer-spending framing and names specific dollar amounts, loan repayment program design, and opportunity-cost quantification — Accountant's distinctive territory beyond the Analyst.)
