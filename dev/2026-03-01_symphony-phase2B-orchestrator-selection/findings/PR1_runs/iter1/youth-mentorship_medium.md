### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Criterion (b) applies — the program spans distinct institutional systems that must interoperate: faith-based organization (volunteer management + congregational norms), public schools (counselor referrals, mandatory-reporting culture), juvenile justice (referral pipeline + minor-data privacy constraints), and a regional foundation (grant reporting). Referrals, consent frameworks, background-check regimes, and safeguards must be designed ACROSS these systems — the reconciliation IS the brainstorming need, matching the positive swap example for multi-institutional social programs. Effort-invariance check: this is a topic-structure decision and would be the same at high effort.
