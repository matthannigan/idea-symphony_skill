### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST names a multi-institutional social program — faith-based org, school counselors, juvenile justice referrals, partner organizations, parental/guardian consent, and a volunteer framework — where referrals, safeguards, mandatory-reporting pathways, and consent architecture must be designed *across* distinct institutional systems with their own logic (schools, courts, faith org, families). This meets criterion (b): multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate, and matches the prompt's explicit positive example of mentorship programs.
- Effort-invariance check: This is a topic-structure decision and would be the same at medium effort. ✓
