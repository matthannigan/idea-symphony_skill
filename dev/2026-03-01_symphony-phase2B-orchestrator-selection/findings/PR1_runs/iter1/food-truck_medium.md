### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck is a single-domain business strategy — one solo founder navigating one operating environment (permits, pricing, competition, a pending city ordinance, and a local food-truck association). Neither swap criterion applies: (a) there are no distinct constraining systems whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need, and (b) there are not multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate via designed linkages — the city ordinance and trade association are facets of the one hospitality business's operating environment, not independent institutional systems being bridged. This matches the stated negative example verbatim. Effort-invariance confirmed: the same Analogist decision would hold at high effort since it is a topic-structure judgment.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis) … Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations … Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs … $60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan … realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation (catering, events, fleet expansion, or brick-and-mortar)."
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees."
  - Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process … Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association."
  - Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "[no architectural/engineering content in REQUEST — operational food-prep equipment only]"
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Lawyer (moderate) and Politician (moderate) are deferred alternatives that would qualify at high effort; at medium, the 0-or-1 ceiling and absence of additional strong triggers beyond Accountant means they are excluded here. Accountant was the cleanly central pick given the brainstorming goal's financial framing (unit economics, cash flow, financing, expansion path).
