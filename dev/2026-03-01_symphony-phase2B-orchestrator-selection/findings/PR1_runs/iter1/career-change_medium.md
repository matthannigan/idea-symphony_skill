### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is a career transition across two distinct professional/institutional systems — ICU nursing (clinical practice, BSN credentialing, hospital employment) and public interest immigration law (JD program, bar admission, public interest legal practice) — bridged by one person. This matches criterion (a) "distinct constraining systems" and the explicit positive swap example "career transitions across fields (two career systems bridged by one person)." The reconciliation of the two career systems (e.g., "whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law") IS the brainstorming need, not mere context. Effort-invariance check: this decision is the same at high effort.
