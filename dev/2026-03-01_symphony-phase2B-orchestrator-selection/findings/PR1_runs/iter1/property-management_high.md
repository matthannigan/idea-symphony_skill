### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single hospitality business (the STR management company) navigating its operating environment — municipal STR ordinance, potential state preemption, HOA rules, community politics, owner contracts, and its own unit economics are all facets of ONE operating decision surface, not distinct institutional systems being structurally reconciled with each other. Criterion (a) fails because no two systems are being merged or bridged; criterion (b) fails because, while many stakeholders are present, there is only one decision-making entity (the company) acting within a regulated market — no referral, consent, or structural-linkage design across peer institutions is the brainstorming need.
- Effort-invariance check: same decision holds at medium — the topic structure is identical, only persona volume changes. Confirmed.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow" and "Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting"
- Decision: exclude (deferred — see Notes)

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "city recently passed new STR regulations with registration requirements and occupancy taxes… mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time; state legislature considering statewide preemption of local STR bans; homeowner associations in several target neighborhoods have their own STR restrictions" plus named liability structure, contract design, and insurance regimes (GL, E&O, property damage)
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)" and "city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue" with explicit multi-stakeholder conflict (STR advocates vs. long-term renters and neighbors)
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "—"
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** High-effort ceiling of 2 was binding — three strong triggers qualified (Accountant, Lawyer, Politician). The stated goal explicitly frames the work as "navigates the complex regulatory and community landscape while building a profitable, scalable operation," and the REQUEST's distinctive content (new STR ordinance, state preemption, HOA restrictions, 6-month council election) places regulatory-compliance and stakeholder-politics decisions at the center. Lawyer and Politician were selected as most central. Accountant is the deferred alternative; if the session is later re-scoped toward unit-economics/fee-structure as the primary question, substitute Accountant for one of the two selected.
