### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The topic requires reconciling distinct institutional systems — three separate school districts, three teachers' unions with separate contracts, state education officials, town/community identities, and the county board of education — where governance, contract, facility, and coalition structures must be designed across these systems. This matches Step 2 branch (b) (multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate) and is the canonical school-consolidation example cited in the swap guidance. Effort-invariance check: this decision would be identical at high effort; the multi-institutional structure is a property of the topic, not of effort level.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%… Budget modeling for the consolidated entity… property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — see Notes)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)… any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
  - Decision: exclude — REQUEST names no specific regulatory framework, statute, or compliance regime; union contract reconciliation is labor/stakeholder work rather than a regulatory-compliance architecture, and voter approval is political process rather than a legal-compliance regime.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts… Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)… Governance structure for the new unified board… town identity advocates ('our school IS our town')… state education officials encouraging consolidation"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Transportation logistics and costs for a larger geographic district"
  - Decision: exclude — no engineering, hardware, ML, or systems-architecture content; transportation logistics is operations, not architectural design.

**Notes:** Two strong triggers qualified (Accountant, Politician) but medium-effort ceiling is 1. Politician was chosen as most central because the stated goal hinges on whether "a consolidation vote can succeed" and explicitly calls for "Building a coalition that can win voter approval" — coalition-building and governance design are the decisive dimensions. Accountant is deferred as the alternative; at high effort both would be included. The Analyst (Tier 1) will cover much of the quantitative financial framing (budget modeling, funding-formula impact, cost savings vs. transition costs) at medium effort.
