### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST describes a single bounded event (one family planning a 5th birthday party for 11 children) — neither criterion (a) distinct constraining systems nor criterion (b) multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate applies. This is the canonical "bounded event planning (birthday party)" negative-swap example. Criterion (b) does not apply because there are no institutional systems (schools, agencies, regulators, etc.) whose constraints must be reconciled — just one household hosting guests. Effort-invariance check: this decision would be identical at high effort (topic structure, not effort, drives the call).
