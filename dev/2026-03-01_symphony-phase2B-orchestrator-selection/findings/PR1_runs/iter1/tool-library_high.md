### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The tool library is a single-community initiative — one neighborhood nonprofit, one storefront, one volunteer core serving one ~2,000-household community. Criterion (a) fails: there are no distinct constraining systems whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need. Criterion (b) fails: homeowners, renters, and volunteers are stakeholder segments within one community program, not distinct institutional systems (schools/courts/faith orgs/regulators) that must interoperate. This matches the explicit negative-swap example in Step 2 ("tool library — one community, multi-stakeholder is NOT multi-system"). Effort-invariance check: this decision would be identical at medium effort — it is a topic-structure call, not effort-dependent.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant

  - Topic citation: "Small neighborhood nonprofit with modest budget" and "Tool maintenance and replacement costs"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Rationale: Budget mention is nonprofit-frugality framing, not financial structure. No unit economics, pricing strategy, revenue model design, or capital allocation is named — maintenance/replacement costs are operational concerns, not the financial MECHANISM that triggers Accountant. This is the hobby/community-program trap.

- Persona: Lawyer

One nonprofit serving one community — governance mechanism is not central.
