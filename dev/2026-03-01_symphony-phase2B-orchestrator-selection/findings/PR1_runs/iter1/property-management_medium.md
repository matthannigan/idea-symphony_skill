### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single hospitality operation navigating its operating environment (STR ordinance, HOA restrictions, community politics, tax compliance, its own financial model). Per the Step 2 negative example, these are facets of one business, not distinct institutional systems whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need. Neither criterion (a) "distinct constraining systems" nor (b) "multiple institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate" is met — the regulatory bodies and HOAs are the business's environment, not co-equal systems being bridged by the founders. Effort-invariance check: this decision holds at high effort as well (topic structure is unchanged by effort level).

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow" and "$80K combined savings for startup costs" with percentage-of-booking-revenue fee model
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — medium ceiling of 1 forced a single pick)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time; state legislature considering statewide preemption … homeowner associations … have their own STR restrictions" plus "Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence" and "Contract design with property owners (liability allocation, termination clauses, insurance requirements)"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)" with "city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue" and active STR-advocate vs. opponent coalition tension
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — medium ceiling of 1 forced a single pick)

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "listing optimization, guest communication, cleaning, maintenance, pricing" — operational SaaS/ops vocabulary only; no hardware, ML, or architectural decisions
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three strong Tier 3 triggers (Lawyer, Politician, Accountant) qualified; medium ceiling forced one pick. Lawyer chosen as most central because the stated goal ("navigates the complex regulatory and community landscape") and the distinguishing topic content (new STR ordinance with zone permit caps, 30-minute local-contact mandate, state preemption watch, HOA overlays, multi-layer tax compliance, contract/liability architecture) hinge on regulatory navigation; Politician and Accountant are downstream of and generic compared to the regulatory axis. Deferred alternatives: Politician (city council engagement + coalition politics around the election) and Accountant (fee structure, break-even, seasonal cash flow, $80K capital constraint) — revisit if effort is raised to high.
