### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck is a single-domain business strategy topic (one entrepreneurial challenge with multiple facets — pricing, permits, competition, growth — all subordinate to one business). No distinct institutional systems to reconcile. Analogist-keeper example. Effort-invariance: same at high.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)"; "$60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan"; "Revenue diversification: daily vending vs. catering vs. event bookings"; "Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations"; "Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs"
  - Decision: include — Financial structure is central and named with specific mechanisms (dollar ranges, unit economics, revenue-model design, financing tradeoffs).

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances"
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — ceiling reached, Accountant selected as more central)

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "How to engage with the regulatory process"; "Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
  - Decision: exclude — Moderate only; medium requires strong.

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: — (no technical architecture)
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant chosen over Lawyer at the 0-1 ceiling — Goals explicitly frame unit economics and revenue-model design as the brainstorming deliverable; Lawyer ordinance/permit content is operating-environment context to that goal, not its central object.
