### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Two career systems (nursing, law) bridged by one person. Explicit positive-swap example. Effort-invariance: matches medium.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year"; "Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary"; "Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"; "Timeline and opportunity cost at age 32"; stated Goal: "think through whether this career transition makes sense — financially, professionally, and personally"
  - Decision: include — Finance is named as one of three decision axes with specific dollar content (loans, savings, income gap, LRAP programs, opportunity cost). Economic content is a core decision dimension even though the context is personal rather than organizational. Moderate qualifies at high.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Public interest attorney"; "law school"; "pass the bar"
  - Decision: exclude — Career-change-as-target-career false-positive trap per R6. "Attorney"/"lawyer" as target profession is not a Lawyer trigger; the brainstorming need is personal decision-exploration.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: none

  - Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
