**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Lawyer

  - Decision: exclude — These are negotiation/finance matters, not regulatory-compliance architecture with named legal frameworks.

- Persona: Politician

  - Decision: include — Governance/coalition/voter-approval is the central decision mechanism; exemplar Politician strong-trigger topic.

- Persona: Technical Expert

**Notes:** Two strong triggers (Politician + Accountant) at 0-1 ceiling. Accountant deferred as alternative; finance is the cause of the decision but politics is the mechanism by which it happens.
