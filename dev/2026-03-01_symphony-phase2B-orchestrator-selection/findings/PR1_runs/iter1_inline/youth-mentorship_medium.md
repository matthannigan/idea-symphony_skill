### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Multi-institutional social program — pairing adult volunteers with at-risk teens spans distinct institutional systems that must interoperate: faith organization (congregation pool + administrative infrastructure), school system (counselor referrals, chronic-absenteeism context), juvenile justice system (referrals, data-privacy rules for court-involved minors), foundation (funding framework), and volunteer-safeguarding framework (background checks, mandatory reporting). Referrals, safeguards, and consent frameworks must be designed *across* these systems — this is branch (b) of the swap criterion. Matches the "multi-institutional social programs" positive swap example. Effort-invariance: same at high.
