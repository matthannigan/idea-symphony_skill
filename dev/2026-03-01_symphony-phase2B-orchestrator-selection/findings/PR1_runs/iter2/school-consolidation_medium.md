### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: School-district consolidation involves multiple distinct institutional systems whose constraints must be reconciled — three separate districts, three teachers' unions with independent contracts, three town-identity communities, county board of education, and state education officials. Criterion (b) is squarely met: the brainstorming need is to design referrals, safeguards, and structural linkages *across* these institutional systems (governance structure, union-contract reconciliation, facility-closure politics across towns). Effort-invariance check: the same swap decision would hold at high effort — this is a topic-structure property, not an effort-level property.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%... saving an estimated $1.2M annually"
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — medium ceiling forces one pick; see Notes)
  - Q1b gating satisfied: Financial structure is explicitly a core decision axis (goal names "political, financial, and community identity dimensions"). Removing financial content materially changes the central question — the feasibility hinges on fiscal survivability (smallest district has 2-year fund balance), the per-pupil funding formula math, transition costs vs. savings, and debt/bond consolidation. This is not a product-strategy list with monetization bolted on.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)" / "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Rationale: No named statutory regime or compliance architecture to design. Union contract reconciliation is labor-political negotiation (Politician territory), and voter approval is governance (also Politician). State per-pupil formula is operating-environment, not regulatory architecture being designed.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)... Union contract reconciliation and staffing transition plans"
  - Decision: include
  - Rationale: Coalition assembly, voter approval across three jurisdictions, institutional merger governance, and multi-stakeholder conflict are CENTRAL to the brainstorming goal (explicitly: "determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed").

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: (no relevant content — no engineering, ML, hardware, or systems-architecture dimensions; transportation logistics is operational)
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant deferred despite strong trigger. Medium-effort ceiling of 1 Tier 3 persona forced the pick between Accountant and Politician. Politician selected as most central because the goal statement foregrounds the consolidation vote as the binding constraint ("will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed") — without coalition success across three districts, no financial plan is realized. If this session were re-run at high effort, Accountant should be added: the REQUEST's financial content is unusually rich (per-pupil funding formula, property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations, structural deficits, $1.2M admin-duplication savings, $4.2M deferred maintenance, transition-cost modeling).
