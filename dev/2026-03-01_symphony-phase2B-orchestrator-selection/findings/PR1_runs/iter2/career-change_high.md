### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST bridges two distinct professional systems (ICU nursing / clinical healthcare and public interest immigration law) through one person. Reconciling "whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law" and exploring "alternative paths that combine healthcare and immigrant advocacy without a full career change" is the central brainstorming need, matching the checklist's "career transitions across fields" positive-swap example (criterion a — distinct constraining systems whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need). Effort-invariance check: this decision is topic-structural and would be identical at medium effort.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year ... Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary ... Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
  - Decision: include
  - Q1b gating satisfied: the financial dimension is a core decision axis, not a single bullet among many. The REQUEST's Goals line explicitly frames the decision as "financially, professionally, and personally," and Key Considerations lists the financial reality (3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary) and loan-repayment-assistance programs as standalone considerations. Applying the removal test: if all financial content were stripped, the central question ("is this career transition survivable and sensible?") would change materially — the viability hinges on the financial pivot. Not a birthday/hobby consumer-budget trap; not a product-strategy monetization-bullet trap. Named dollar mechanisms ($22K, $45K, $82K) clear the moderate bar per Q2.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees"
  - Decision: exclude (career-change-to-lawyer R6 false-positive trap — the REQUEST names "lawyer"/"attorney" only as a target career, not as a regulatory framework to design. No named statute, compliance architecture, or multi-jurisdictional strategy in play for the user's own decision.)
