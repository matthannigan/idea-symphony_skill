### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST frames the feature-pruning decision as a reconciliation of four structurally linked constraint systems — user base (8% 30-day retention, 2% DAU/MAU, risk of "triggering churn" from simplification), team (6 engineers / 1 designer / 1 PM, "burned out from maintaining 15 half-built features"), runway (8 months of funding remaining), and codebase ("technical debt implications of removing deeply integrated features"). This matches the positive swap example "Multi-stakeholder product systems where user needs, team capacity, runway, and codebase are structurally linked constraints" under Step 2 criterion (b). Effort-invariance check: the swap would hold at medium as well — the topic-structure is identical regardless of effort.
