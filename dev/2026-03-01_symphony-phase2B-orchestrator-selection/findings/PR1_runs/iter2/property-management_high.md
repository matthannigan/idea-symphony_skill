### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single STR management business navigating its operating environment — municipal STR ordinance, state preemption risk, HOA restrictions, community politics, and its own unit economics are facets of one hospitality operation, not distinct institutional systems being reconciled. Criterion (b) fails: there are multiple stakeholders and constraint layers, but only one decision-making system (the company) is being designed. The strategic cross-system dimension is already captured by the Lawyer/Politician Tier 3 selections; Analogist keeps creative metaphorical reframing for the single-domain hospitality operation. Effort-invariance: same decision would hold at medium effort — this is a topic-structure call.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow"; "$80K combined savings for startup costs"; "percentage of booking revenue" fee model; "Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting"
- Decision: exclude (deferred — see Notes)
- Q1b gating: satisfied — removing financial content (fee structure, break-even, $80K capital, unit economics per property) would fundamentally change the brainstorming need. Financial architecture is a core decision axis of the company design, not a background bullet. Specific dollar/percentage mechanisms named; clears Q2 depth bar. Not a product-strategy bullet-list trap.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "city recently passed new STR regulations with registration requirements and occupancy taxes"; "New city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time; state legislature considering statewide preemption of local STR bans; homeowner associations in several target neighborhoods have their own STR restrictions"; "Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence"; "Contract design with property owners (liability allocation, termination clauses, insurance requirements)"
- Decision: include
- Rationale: Named STR ordinance (statutory regime) plus state preemption dynamics plus HOA layer plus liability/contract/insurance architecture = multi-jurisdiction regulatory strategy to DESIGN, not routine operating-environment permits for a single small business. Clears the operating-environment-permits trap by a wide margin.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Significant tension between STR advocates (property owners, tourism businesses) and opponents (long-term renters concerned about housing costs, neighbors concerned about noise/parking); city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue"; "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)"; "Positioning the company as a 'good neighbor' operation vs. absentee management stereotype"
- Decision: include
- Rationale: Central stakeholder politics with named veto players (city council, HOAs, state legislature), active coalitions (STR advocates vs opponents), and an imminent election that will shape ordinance trajectory. Not "team morale" — genuine civic coalition politics.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "—"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, or ML/AI architecture in the REQUEST. Noise monitoring is a compliance device, not a systems-architecture brainstorming need.

**Notes:** High-effort ceiling of 2 was binding — three strong triggers qualified (Accountant, Lawyer, Politician). The stated goal leads with "navigates the complex regulatory and community landscape," making Lawyer (regulatory architecture across city/state/HOA/contract) and Politician (community politics, election, coalition work) the most central two. Accountant is the deferred alternative: if the session is re-scoped toward unit-economics and fee-structure modeling as the primary brainstorming need, substitute Accountant for either Lawyer or Politician.
