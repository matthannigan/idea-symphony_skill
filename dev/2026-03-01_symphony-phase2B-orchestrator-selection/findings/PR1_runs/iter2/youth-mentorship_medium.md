### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The topic spans distinct institutional systems that must interoperate — a faith-based community organization, school counselors, juvenile justice referrals, volunteer management, and parental/guardian consent frameworks. Referrals, safeguards, mandatory-reporting pathways, and consent structures must be designed *across* these systems, which matches Step 2 criterion (b) (multi-institutional social program — the canonical positive swap example in the prompt). Effort-invariance check: the same swap applies at high effort — this is a topic-structure decision independent of effort.
