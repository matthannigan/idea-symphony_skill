### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Habit-tracker is a single-product UX design topic — one product, one user-facing system. The stakeholders mentioned (users, solo dev/small team) are facets of one product rather than distinct institutional or stakeholder systems that must be structurally reconciled. Criterion (b) does not apply because there is no multi-institutional or multi-stakeholder-system interoperation to design; criterion (a) does not apply because there are no distinct constraining systems whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need. Explicitly listed as a negative swap example ("single-product UX design"). Effort-invariance check: same decision would hold at medium — topic structure is unchanged by effort level.
