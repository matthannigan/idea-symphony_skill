### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is a career transition across two distinct professional systems (ICU nursing and public interest immigration law) bridged by one person, with the reconciliation itself as the brainstorming need — see "whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law" and "alternative paths that combine healthcare and immigrant advocacy without a full career change." Matches the positive swap example "Career transitions across fields (two career systems bridged by one person)." Effort-invariance check: same decision would hold at high effort — this is a topic-structure property, not an effort-level property.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year ... Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary ... Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
  - Decision: exclude
  - (Rationale: Q1b gating test — if financial content is removed, the central question materially changes: the user explicitly frames the decision as "financially, professionally, and personally," and survivability of the 3-year income gap + debt trajectory vs public-interest salary is a core decision axis, not a background bullet. Named dollar figures, runway math, and LRAP mechanism clear Q2's depth bar above consumer-spending. Not strong because this is personal-finance architecture rather than business-model/unit-economics design, so it does not clear the "strong" bar — moderate only.)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "leaving my career as an ICU nurse to become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees"
  - Decision: exclude
  - (Rationale: "Attorney"/"lawyer" appears as the target career, not as a regulatory framework the brainstorm must architect. This is the explicit R6 career-change false-positive trap — Lawyer here would produce an immigration-law regulatory implementation guide rather than a career-decision exploration.)

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "supportive family but they think this is risky"
  - Decision: exclude
  - (Rationale: No coalition building, governance design, institutional change, or multi-stakeholder decision process. Family concerns are personal, not stakeholder politics.)

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: (no technical architecture content in REQUEST)
  - Decision: exclude
  - (Rationale: No engineering, hardware/software, ML, or systems-architecture dimension. Career-change topics are a named Technical Expert false-positive pattern per R6 — "legacy code"/"system rewrite" framings on career topics are a clear FP.)
