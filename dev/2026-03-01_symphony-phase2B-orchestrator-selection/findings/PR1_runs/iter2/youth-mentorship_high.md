### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The program must design referrals, consent flow, and safeguards *across* distinct institutional systems — a faith-based organization, public schools, the juvenile justice system, and a volunteer framework — each with its own logic, data rules, and stakeholder accountability. This matches Step 2 criterion (b) and the "Multi-institutional social programs (mentorship spanning faith org + schools + juvenile justice + volunteer management)" positive swap example directly. Effort-invariance check: the multi-institutional structure is a topic property, not an effort property — the decision would be identical at medium.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)"
  - Decision: exclude (funding is fixed bounded context, not a financial-design axis; Q1b gating test — removing all financial content from the REQUEST leaves the safety/matching/training/outcomes brainstorming fully intact, so financial structure is a background concern not a core decision axis; no unit economics, revenue model, pricing, or capital-allocation content)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers; Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors; Liability insurance and organizational legal protections; Parental/guardian consent frameworks and ongoing communication; Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
  - Decision: include (multiple named regulatory regimes — mandatory reporting statutes, juvenile-justice data-privacy protections, minor-consent frameworks, background-check compliance — with the stated goal explicitly including "meeting legal requirements"; this is compliance architecture to *design* across institutions, not routine operating-environment permits for a single small business, so the Q3 operating-environment-permits trap does not apply)

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teens ages 13-17 identified through school counselors and juvenile justice referrals" / "~40 interested adults from the congregation and partner organizations"
  - Decision: exclude (multi-stakeholder program operations, but the stakeholders are operational partners in a referral/volunteer pipeline, not a coalition-building, governance-design, or institutional-change political arena; the multi-institutional coordination is captured structurally by the Connector swap, not by Politician)

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: [no technical-architecture content in REQUEST]
  - Decision: exclude (no engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software, ML/AI, or systems-architecture decisions present; program is human-services design)
