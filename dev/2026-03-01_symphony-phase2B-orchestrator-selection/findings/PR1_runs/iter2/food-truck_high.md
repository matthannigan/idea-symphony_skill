### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck is a single business navigating its operating environment (permits, pricing, competition, local ordinance). It is listed explicitly as a negative swap example — one operation with multiple facets, not distinct institutional systems being reconciled. Criterion (b) does not apply: the city government, food truck association, and customer base are stakeholders around one business, not separate institutional logics the user must design linkages across. Effort-invariance check: the same decision holds at medium effort — the swap criteria are topic-structure-only and do not shift with effort level.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)"; "$60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan"; "Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs"; Goals: "clear pricing strategy, unit economics, and a realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation"
  - Decision: include
  - Q1b gating satisfied: financial architecture is the core decision axis, not a background bullet. If financial content were removed from the REQUEST, the central brainstorming question (how to build a sustainable unit-economics-sound food truck with a growth path) would collapse. Named dollar figures, specific financing instruments, and unit-economics vocabulary clear the depth bar.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Rationale: Operating-environment-permits trap applies directly. Food truck permits, commissary kitchen requirements, vending zones, and municipal licensing fees are routine check-the-box operational constraints for a single small business, not a compliance architecture the founder must design. No named statutory regime (HIPAA/FDA/STR), no multi-jurisdiction regulatory strategy. The pending ordinance is a political/stakeholder matter (Politician territory) and a business-risk matter (Accountant + Devil's Advocate territory), not compliance-design work.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances"; "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process"; "Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
  - Decision: include
  - Rationale: Stakeholder politics is a genuine decision axis, not ambient. The REQUEST explicitly frames ordinance engagement and coalition-building with the food truck association and city officials as part of the brainstorming need, and the outcome of the ordinance debate materially shapes the business. This is governance-structure and coalition-sequencing territory, not team morale.

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: — (no engineering/ML/hardware-software-codesign content; food truck equipment is operational, not architectural)
  - Decision: exclude
