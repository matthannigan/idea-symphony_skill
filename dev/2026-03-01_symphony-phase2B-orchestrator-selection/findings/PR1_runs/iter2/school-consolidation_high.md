### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST requires reconciling distinct institutional systems — three school districts, three separate teachers' unions, parent groups, town-identity advocates, fiscal conservatives, and state education officials — whose constraints interact and whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need. Matches the prompt's positive swap example ("school consolidation — districts + unions + communities + state"). Criterion (b) satisfied.
- Effort-invariance check: This decision is a topic-structure judgment and would be identical at medium effort.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%... consolidated district could reduce administrative duplication... saving an estimated $1.2M annually... true cost savings vs. transition costs, facility decisions, debt consolidation... property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
  - Decision: include
  - Q1b gating test: satisfied — financial structure is a core decision axis explicitly named in the Goal ("financial... dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed"). Removing financial content from the REQUEST would fundamentally change the central question, since solvency (2-year fund balance before insolvency) and funding-formula arithmetic are why consolidation is on the table. Specific dollar mechanisms (per-pupil formula, tax equalization, bond consolidation) clear the strong bar.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)... any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Rationale: No named regulatory framework or compliance regime to design. Union contract reconciliation is labor relations, and voter approval is a governance/political process — both absent a statutory compliance architecture. Politician covers the stakeholder/governance dimension; Devil's Advocate covers ambient contract risk.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts... Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)... town identity advocates ('our school IS our town')"
  - Decision: include
  - Rationale: The decision literally hinges on a tri-district vote plus new governance design across merging institutions — coalition sequencing, veto players, governance architecture, and facility-closure politics are all central. The Goal explicitly names "political... dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed."

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Transportation logistics and costs for a larger geographic district"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Rationale: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware/ML architecture, or systems-design decisions. Transportation routing is operations, not technical architecture.
