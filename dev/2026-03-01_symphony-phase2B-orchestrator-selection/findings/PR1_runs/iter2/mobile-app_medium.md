### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST frames a multi-stakeholder product system where user base (180K downloads, 8% retention, churn risk from simplification), team capacity (6 engineers burned out maintaining 15 features), runway (8 months of funding), and codebase (technical debt from deeply integrated features) are structurally linked constraints whose reconciliation IS the brainstorming need.
