### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single business (one STR management company) navigating its operating environment — city STR ordinance, HOA rules, community politics, owner contracts, and internal finance are facets of one hospitality operation, not distinct institutional systems being reconciled. Criterion (b) does not apply because the company is the single decision-making system even though it interacts with many stakeholders.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow ... $80K combined savings for startup costs ... percentage of booking revenue"
- Decision: exclude (deferred — medium ceiling is 1; Lawyer selected as most central). Q1b gating is satisfied: removing financial content would change the central question because the unit economics of the fee model and break-even per property are load-bearing design decisions, not background bullets.

- Persona: Lawyer

HOAs ... have their own STR restrictions ... Liability structure ... Contract design with property owners ... Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting"

- Persona: Politician

**Notes:** Two additional strong Tier 3 triggers (Accountant and Politician) were deferred by the medium-effort ceiling. Lawyer was chosen as most central because the stated goal frames regulatory/liability/contract architecture as the spine the company must navigate (city STR ordinance, state preemption, HOA restrictions, permit zone caps, multi-tier tax compliance, owner contract and liability allocation). Accountant (fee structure, break-even, seasonal cash flow modeling) and Politician (city council election, HOA board engagement, coalition positioning) are strong deferred alternatives and would be the first additions at high effort.
