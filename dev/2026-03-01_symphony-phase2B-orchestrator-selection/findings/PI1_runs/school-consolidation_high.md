### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST requires reconciling distinct institutional systems whose constraints interact — three separate districts with their own boards, three separate teacher-union contracts, three town communities with distinct identities, plus the state funding formula and county board process. The brainstorming need IS this cross-institutional reconciliation (criterion b clearly met; this is the canonical positive example named in the prompt).

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula... would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%... saving an estimated $1.2M annually... structural deficits... 2-year fund balance remaining before insolvency"
- Decision: include
- Q1b gating: removing the financial content would eliminate the central decision axis — the consolidation question is driven by structural insolvency, per-pupil formula mechanics, transition cost vs. savings modeling, debt consolidation, property tax equalization, and long-term fiscal sustainability under enrollment decline. Financial architecture is a core axis, not a background bullet; the REQUEST names specific dollar mechanisms and percentages throughout.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)... bond obligations... any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No named statutory regime (HIPAA/FDA/STR/zoning) where the need is to design regulatory strategy. Union contract reconciliation is labor-relations and political-coalition work (covered by Politician), not compliance architecture. Bond/voter process is governance procedure, not legal design.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts... Teachers' unions... parent groups, town identity advocates ('our school IS our town'), fiscal conservatives, and state education officials... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures"
- Decision: include
- Rationale: Stakeholder politics, coalition building, institutional merger, governance-structure design, and voter-approval mechanics are CENTRAL — the decision hinges on assembling a winning coalition across three communities with competing identities and interests. This is exactly the design-for-institutional-change pattern Politician targets.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Transportation logistics and costs for a larger geographic district"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No engineering/ML/hardware/signals architecture. Transportation is operational/logistical, not technical architecture.
