### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The habit-tracker REQUEST concerns a single-product UX design problem — one product whose users, behavioral-science principles, monetization, and technical stack are facets of one system, not distinct institutional/stakeholder systems that must interoperate. Criterion (b) does not apply: there are no multiple institutional systems (schools/courts/agencies, or hardware+ML+regulatory) whose constraints must be reconciled across boundaries. This matches the stated negative swap example ("Single-product UX design — habit-tracker").
