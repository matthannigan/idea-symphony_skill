### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: REQUEST presents a multi-stakeholder product system where user needs (180K downloads, 8% retention, overwhelmed feedback), team capacity (6 engineers, 1 designer, 1 PM, burned out), runway (8 months), and a deeply integrated codebase (technical debt from 15 half-built features) function as structurally linked constraints that must be reconciled — this matches Step 2 criterion (b) and the positive swap example for multi-stakeholder product systems.
- Effort-invariance check: Confirmed — the same decision would hold at medium; the Connector trigger is a topic-structure property independent of effort.
