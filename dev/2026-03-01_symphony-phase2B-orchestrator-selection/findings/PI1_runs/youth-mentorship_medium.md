### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Program design spans distinct institutional systems that must interoperate — a faith-based community organization, local schools (counselor referrals), juvenile justice (referrals and data-privacy obligations), and a volunteer pool drawn from congregation plus partner organizations. Referral pathways, consent frameworks, and safeguard protocols must be designed *across* these institutional systems, matching Step 2 criterion (b) and the positive example of multi-institutional social programs. Effort-invariance check: this decision would be identical at high effort (topic structure is unchanged).

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none

This is the design-regulatory-strategy case Step 1.2 Q2 calls out as "strong," and it is distinct from the operating-environment-permits trap.
