### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck is a single-domain business strategy — one founder navigating one operating environment (permits, pricing, competition, financing) with no distinct institutional systems requiring reconciliation. Criterion (b) does not apply: the city regulatory process, the food truck association, and the capital stack are facets of one business's operating environment, not separate institutional systems whose logics must be bridged.
- Effort-invariance check: This decision would be the same at high effort — the topic structure (single business, single jurisdiction, single product line) does not change with effort level.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)… $60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan… Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations… Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs… Whether to pursue the SBA loan or bootstrap more conservatively"
  - Decision: include
  - Q1b gating test: satisfied — removing all financial content from the REQUEST would eliminate the stated goal itself ("clear pricing strategy, unit economics, and a realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation"). Unit economics, financing structure, and cash-flow modeling are the core decision axis, not background bullets. Named mechanism (dollar amounts, food cost ratios, break-even, specific financing vehicles) clears the depth bar decisively.

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "city requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Operating-environment-permits trap applies: food truck permits, commissary requirements, and vending-zone rules are routine operating-environment constraints for a single small business to comply with, not a multi-jurisdiction compliance architecture to design. The pending ordinance is a political/coalition engagement question (Politician territory), not a statutory-regime design need. No named statutory framework (HIPAA, FDA, STR ordinance, etc.) and no multi-institutional compliance architecture.

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process… Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
  - Decision: exclude
  - Central stakeholder-politics/coalition-building content (active ordinance fight, association coalition, city-council engagement) is a genuine strong trigger. Excluded solely because medium ceiling is 1 and Accountant is more central to the explicit stated goal (unit economics and sustainable business path). Carried as deferred alternative in Notes.

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "[no hardware/ML/systems-architecture content in REQUEST]"
  - Decision: exclude
  - No engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, or architectural decisions named. Food-truck operations are not a technical-architecture brainstorming need.

**Notes:** Politician (strong trigger) deferred by medium-effort ceiling of 1. If this session is later re-run at high effort, Politician should be included alongside Accountant.
