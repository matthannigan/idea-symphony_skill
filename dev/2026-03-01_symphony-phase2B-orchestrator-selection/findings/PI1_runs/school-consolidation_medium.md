### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: REQUEST requires reconciling three distinct school districts plus three separate teachers' unions, state education officials, parent groups, town-identity advocates, and fiscal conservatives — multiple institutional systems (districts + unions + state + community governance) with their own logic whose structural linkage (unified governance, contract reconciliation, facility allocation, voter coalitions across jurisdictions) IS the brainstorming need. This matches the canonical multi-institutional-change positive example. Effort-invariance check: same decision would apply at high effort — the multi-institutional structure is topic-intrinsic, not effort-dependent.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula ... would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12% ... saving an estimated $1.2M annually ... property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
- Decision: exclude
- Q1b gating test satisfied: financial structure (consolidated budget modeling, tax equalization, bond consolidation, long-term fiscal sustainability under enrollment decline) is a core decision axis; removing financial content would collapse the central "whether and how to consolidate" question since insolvency timing and per-pupil formula gains are primary drivers. Excluded under medium ceiling (1) in favor of Politician; deferred to Notes.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile) ... any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No named statutory regime or compliance architecture to design. Union-contract reconciliation is labor/political negotiation (Politician territory), not regulatory strategy. Voter-approval requirement is governance process, not compliance design.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts ... Building a coalition that can win voter approval ... Governance structure for the new unified board ... Managing the politics of facility closures ... 'our school IS our town' resistance"
- Decision: include
- Rationale: Coalition building, multi-district voter approval, governance-structure design, institutional-merger politics, and community-identity resistance are ALL central decision mechanisms; the stated goal explicitly names "whether a consolidation vote can succeed" as the determining question.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "(no technical-architecture content in REQUEST)"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No hardware/software/ML/systems-architecture content. Transportation logistics and facility decisions are operational planning, not technical architecture.

**Notes:** Accountant (strong) deferred per medium-effort ceiling of 1. The stated goal elevates the coalition/vote/identity dimension as the determining factor, so Politician was selected; financial architecture is covered at depth by Tier 1 Analyst and Systems Thinker for this session. Consider including Accountant if the effort level is raised to high.
