### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST bridges two distinct professional systems — ICU nursing (clinical healthcare, BSN credentialing, hospital labor economics) and public interest immigration law (JD/bar credentialing, nonprofit legal-services economics, LRAP structures) — connected by one person's transition. This matches criterion (a): distinct constraining systems whose reconciliation (credential pathway, skill transfer, financial bridge, identity continuity) IS the brainstorming need. Matches the "Career transitions across fields" positive swap example.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year ... 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary ... Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
- Decision: include
- Q1b gating test satisfied: the REQUEST names specific dollar amounts, a named financial mechanism (LRAP), and explicit runway math (3 years without income). If all financial content were removed from the REQUEST, the central question ("does this transition make sense") would materially change — the financial survivability of the 3-year income gap plus debt load is a core decision axis the user is actively trying to resolve, not a background bullet. Personal-finance decision architecture (not business unit economics), but clears the moderate bar.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: Career-change topic names "lawyer/attorney" as a target career — explicit Step 1.2 Q3 trap. No named regulatory framework to design; the brainstorming need is career-decision exploration, not compliance architecture.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "supportive family but they think this is risky"
- Decision: exclude
- Rationale: No coalition-building, governance design, or institutional-change politics. Family skepticism is a personal relationship dimension covered by Empath/Storyteller, not stakeholder politics.

- Persona: Technical Expert
