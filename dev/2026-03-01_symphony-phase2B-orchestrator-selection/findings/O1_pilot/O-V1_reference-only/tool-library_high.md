### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The tool-library topic has a clear single primary domain (a neighborhood nonprofit service design) and does not require bridging or reconciling distinct systems that constrain each other. The value of the Perspective persona here is creative metaphorical reframing (e.g., library-of-things analogies, commons-management analogies), which is Analogist's strength. No inter-domain reconciliation trigger is present.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Small neighborhood nonprofit with modest budget"; "Tool maintenance and replacement costs"; "Sustainability beyond initial enthusiasm"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Liability and safety concerns" (explicitly called out as a Key Consideration); tool lending inherently creates injury/damage exposure requiring waivers, insurance categories, and user-safety frameworks
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: REQUEST describes an internal nonprofit operating in one storefront with ~8-10 volunteers; no governance, coalition-sequencing, regulatory-politics, or institutional-change dynamics are present. "Building community buy-in" is a stakeholder-engagement concern that Audience Advocate and Appreciative Inquirer already cover.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI architecture in the REQUEST. "Tools" refers to physical hand/power tools, not technical systems architecture. Clear false-positive risk if included.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Both Accountant and Lawyer register as moderate (not strong) triggers. At high effort, the 0-2 ceiling allows both to be included, and they address distinct, non-overlapping dimensions central to tool-library sustainability: unit economics / reserve funds / depreciation (Accountant) and waiver design / insurance / statutory product-liability considerations (Lawyer). Neither rises to "strong" because the REQUEST does not center the session on financial modeling or regulatory navigation, but both are prominent enough in the Key Considerations to justify inclusion under the moderate threshold.
