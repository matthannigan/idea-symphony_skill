### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic has a clear single primary domain (short-term rental property management business). While it touches multiple stakeholder groups (owners, guests, neighbors, regulators), it does not require bridging or reconciling distinct systems that constrain each other in the Connector's sense — there is no cross-disciplinary career transition, institutional merger, or multi-domain technical design. The Analogist's metaphorical reframing of a single business domain is the safer, higher-value choice.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow" and "Capital: $80K combined savings for startup costs"; business model is "a percentage of booking revenue"; Key Considerations include "Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting" and "Insurance requirements (general liability, property damage, errors & omissions)."
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence"; "Contract design with property owners (liability allocation, termination clauses, insurance requirements)"; "new city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time"; "state legislature considering statewide preemption of local STR bans"; "homeowner associations in several target neighborhoods have their own STR restrictions"; "Zoning compliance and permit management across multiple neighborhood zones."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)"; "city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue"; "Significant tension between STR advocates (property owners, tourism businesses) and opponents (long-term renters concerned about housing costs, neighbors concerned about noise/parking)"; "Positioning the company as a 'good neighbor' operation vs. absentee management stereotype."
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: The REQUEST contains no engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems content. "Noise monitoring" is a compliance mechanism, not a genuine technical-architecture concern.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three strong triggers (Accountant, Lawyer, Politician) compete for the 0-1 medium-effort slot. Selected Lawyer because regulatory/compliance exposure is both the densest and most specialized territory in the REQUEST — multi-layered regulation (municipal STR ordinance, state preemption, HOA covenants, zoning), explicit contract/liability design, and insurance frameworks touch 5 of the 9 Key Considerations and appear across Context, Regulatory Landscape, and Community Dynamics. Tier 1 coverage here is thin: Devil's Advocate flags high-level risk but not specific regulatory mechanisms; Analyst reaches financial-structural depth the Accountant would extend but does not reach the statutory/contractual specificity only the Lawyer provides. Politician's stakeholder-politics contribution, while genuine, is partially addressable by Audience Advocate's structural human-centeredness and Systems Thinker's governance/feedback view, making Lawyer the least-replaceable specialist for this REQUEST.
