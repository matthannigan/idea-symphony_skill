### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The topic's core operation is "consolidate three small school districts into a single unified district" — literal merging of distinct institutional systems (three separate teachers' union contracts, three superintendents, three HR departments, three tax bases, three boards, seven school buildings) that currently constrain each other. This is a paradigmatic inter-domain reconciliation / multi-system institutional change scenario where Connector's isomorphism and recombinant modes activate, matching the guide's explicit example "multi-system institutional change."

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%" and "property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: The REQUEST mentions "union contract reconciliation" and "voter approval" but frames these as political/negotiation challenges rather than regulatory/compliance exposure. No liability, zoning, data privacy, or regulatory framework exposure is central. Devil's Advocate can flag high-level contract/bond risks.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Community identity and the 'our school is our town' resistance," "Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts," "Governance structure for the new unified board," "Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)," plus stakeholders explicitly include "teachers' unions, parent groups, town identity advocates, fiscal conservatives, and state education officials." This is textbook stakeholder politics / institutional governance change.
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI architecture content. Transportation logistics and facility decisions are operational/logistical, not technical architecture in the guide's sense.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Both included Tier 3 personas fit cleanly within the high-effort 0-2 ceiling. Politician is a strong trigger (coalition-centric voter approval across three districts with town-identity politics); Accountant is a moderate trigger (financial structure is central, with specific dollar figures and a funding-formula change, though not a business-model topic per se). No competition for slots — both fit.
