### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-product, single-domain strategic reset ("Radically simplifying a feature-bloated mobile app by stripping it down to its essential core"). There is no inter-domain reconciliation, institutional merger, or bridging of distinct systems that constrain each other. Metaphorical reframing (e.g., keystone-feature analogies, pruning-from-biology) is the kind of divergence that serves this topic, so the Analogist default holds.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Runway: 8 months of funding remaining" and "Team: 6 engineers, 1 designer, 1 PM"
- Decision: exclude
  (Runway is a constraint that shapes the decision, but the topic is not fundamentally about financial structure, pricing, revenue, or unit economics. No business-model question, no monetization redesign called out. Analyst's financial-framework coverage is sufficient at medium. At medium, only strong triggers justify Tier 3 inclusion; this is moderate at best.)

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: REQUEST contains no mention of regulatory exposure, liability, data privacy frameworks, app-store compliance issues, or contractual/IP risk from feature removal.
- Decision: exclude
  (Devil's Advocate can flag any high-level churn/communication risk. No regulatory/compliance gating factor is present.)

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "How to communicate the simplification to existing users without triggering churn" and "Team morale and the psychology of killing your darlings"
- Decision: exclude
  (Stakeholder management here is product-communication and internal-team-morale, not governance, coalition politics, veto players, or institutional change. Audience Advocate covers user-facing stakeholder framing; the political-analytical vocabulary Politician specializes in would be ill-fitted to this topic.)

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Technical debt implications of removing deeply integrated features"
- Decision: exclude
  (One genuine engineering concern is present — deintegration of deeply coupled features — but the core task is product-strategy reprioritization ("Identify the 3-4 features that represent the app's true core value"), not engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems architecture. Technical Expert's strong-trigger profile is wearable-device-class depth; here the technical dimension is a secondary consideration handled adequately by Analyst and Systems Thinker. At medium, only strong triggers justify inclusion.)

**Notes:** Accountant and Technical Expert both register as moderate triggers on this topic; at medium effort (strong-only threshold, 0-1 slot), neither clears the bar. This would be a natural topic to revisit at high effort, where both could plausibly clear a moderate-trigger threshold and compete for the 0-2 Tier 3 slots.
