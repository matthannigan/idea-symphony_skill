### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single primary domain (launching and scaling a food truck business). There is no inter-domain reconciliation, cross-field bridging, or institutional merger at play — the cuisine fusion (Filipino-Mexican) is a product concept, not a structural system-merging problem. Analogist is the safer choice; metaphorical reframing (e.g., restaurant-industry, mobile-retail, or biological analogies) is well-suited to a single-domain business strategy topic.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)"; "$60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan"; "Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs"; "Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations"; "Whether to pursue the SBA loan or bootstrap more conservatively"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process"; "Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"; "city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems present in the REQUEST. Equipment-financing choices are commercial/operational, not technical-architecture.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant is a clean strong trigger — financial structure is explicitly central to the user's goals (pricing strategy, unit economics, break-even, cash flow, SBA financing). Second slot at the 0-2 high-effort ceiling was a contest between Politician and Lawyer, both moderate. Selected Politician because the REQUEST frames the regulatory environment primarily as a political/engagement problem ("engage with the regulatory process," "building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association," city council election-adjacent ordinance debate) rather than as a liability/compliance-heavy legal problem. Politician's distinctive vocabulary (coalition sequencing, political capital, veto players, association-building) is directly on-topic; Lawyer's core value — specific statutes and compliance frameworks for liability-heavy domains — is a less precise fit for a permits-and-ordinance context that Devil's Advocate and Accountant will already cover at the regulatory-risk and licensing-fee level.
