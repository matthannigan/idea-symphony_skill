### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The topic is explicitly about merging three distinct K-12 districts — each with its own board, superintendent, union contract, tax base, and town identity — into a single unified district. This is inter-domain/inter-institutional reconciliation (three systems that currently constrain each other via separate governance, finance, and facilities), not single-domain metaphorical reframing, so Connector's structural isomorphism and recombinant innovation modes outperform Analogist's import mode.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts... consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%"; "Financial analysis: true cost savings vs. transition costs, facility decisions, debt consolidation"; "property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"; "Union contract reconciliation" across 3 separate teachers' union contracts; bond/debt consolidation obligations. These are governance/procedural rather than liability-heavy compliance exposure; no zoning, privacy, or regulated-industry frameworks where a Lawyer's 4-cluster regulatory output would uniquely add value beyond what Devil's Advocate and Politician capture.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "addressing the political, financial, and community identity dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed"; "Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts"; "Governance structure for the new unified board"; "Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)"; "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile), parent groups, town identity advocates..., fiscal conservatives, and state education officials."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI architecture in the REQUEST. Transportation logistics and facility decisions are operational/financial, not a technical architecture domain.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Two strong triggers compete for the 0-1 medium slot (Accountant and Politician). Selected Politician because the REQUEST frames political feasibility — voter approval in all three districts, coalition-building across unions/parent groups/town-identity advocates/fiscal conservatives, facility-closure politics, and new governance structure — as the determinant of whether consolidation can happen at all. The Analyst (10-15 questions) plus the topic's explicit financial frame will carry substantial financial-analysis scaffolding, whereas Politician's distinctive political-analytical vocabulary (veto players, coalition sequencing, political capital) is not substitutable from any Tier 1 persona and is append-only in Phase 2C, so the loss from excluding it would be larger.
