### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain social program (youth mentorship design within one faith-based organization). It does not require bridging or reconciling distinct systems that constrain each other — school referrals, juvenile justice referrals, and congregational volunteer pools are partner inputs into one program, not separate domains being merged. Analogist's metaphorical reframing is the safer, higher-value choice.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)"
- Decision: exclude
- (Funding is a fixed modest seed grant with in-kind support, not a business model, revenue design, or budget-structuring question. None of the guide's trigger keywords — "business," "startup," "pricing," "revenue," "budget" as a design problem — are central. Tier 1 Analyst coverage is sufficient at this funding scale.)

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers," "Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors," "Liability insurance and organizational legal protections," "Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
- Decision: include
- (Multiple concrete regulatory exposures: child-protection background check law, mandatory reporter statutes, liability/insurance frameworks, minor data privacy intersecting juvenile justice confidentiality. This is precisely the "regulatory/compliance exposure" trigger the guide describes — trigger on need, not keywords — and Devil's Advocate alone cannot surface specific statutory mechanisms. Strong trigger justifies inclusion at medium effort.)

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Teens ages 13-17 identified through school counselors and juvenile justice referrals" and "Faith-based community organization with 20-year presence"
- Decision: exclude
- (Stakeholder complexity exists across church, schools, and juvenile justice, but the topic is programmatic partnership coordination rather than governance, coalition-building for institutional change, or political-capital management. No "governance structure," "veto players," or "institutional change" framing is central. At medium effort, moderate is below threshold.)

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: REQUEST contains no engineering, hardware-software, or ML/AI architecture content.
- Decision: exclude
- (No genuine technical architecture. Matching criteria and outcome measurement are operational/analytical questions handled by Analyst and Systems Thinker.)

**Notes:** The Lawyer selection is clean — it is the single strongest Tier 3 trigger and fits the medium-effort 0-1 ceiling. Per the guide, Lawyer's heavy negative framing should be balanced in Phase 2C synthesis by Appreciative Inquirer and Visionary output to avoid defensive ideation on what is fundamentally a relationship-building program.
