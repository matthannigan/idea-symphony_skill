### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is explicitly about reconciling two distinct professional domains that constrain each other — "Whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law" and "Alternative paths that combine healthcare and immigrant advocacy." This is the guide's canonical Connector trigger: career transitions across fields / cross-disciplinary reconciliation, where isomorphism ("how are ICU triage and immigration-case triage the same problem?") and recombination ("what if we combined healthcare + legal advocacy?") modes activate and outperform the Analogist's single-domain metaphorical reframing.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year… Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary… Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees" — the topic is about deciding to enter the legal profession, not navigating regulatory/compliance exposure in a project. The guide explicitly flags career-change as "a partial false positive — the persona responds to the keyword 'lawyer' and produces a regulatory implementation guide rather than decision-exploration questions."
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Think through whether this career transition makes sense — financially, professionally, and personally" — this is a personal decision, not a civic/institutional/organizational change topic. No stakeholder coalitions, governance structures, or veto players involved.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: The REQUEST involves no engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems. The guide explicitly calls out career-change as a "clear false positive… engineering metaphors become a cognitive cage on non-technical topics (e.g., career-change 'legacy code,' 'system rewrites')."
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant is the only Tier 3 inclusion; the 0-2 ceiling is not pressured because the other three Tier 3 candidates each have explicit false-positive or no-trigger disqualifications for this topic class in the guide.
