### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain program design problem (one-on-one youth mentorship inside a single faith-based organization). It is not an inter-domain reconciliation problem — there are no distinct systems that constrain each other in the way the Connector's isomorphism/recombination modes require. Metaphorical reframing (Analogist) is more useful than cross-domain bridging here.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)"
- Decision: exclude
- Reasoning: The REQUEST mentions a small seed grant and in-kind support, and the organization is a nonprofit, but the brainstorm's stated Goals and Key Considerations do not center financial structure, unit economics, pricing, or budget modeling. Funding is a background constraint, not the thing to be designed. The Analyst can cover the modest financial framing needed. Signal is present but shallow — does not clear the bar even at high effort when a clearly stronger Tier 3 trigger is competing for the slot budget.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers… Mandatory reporting obligations… Liability insurance and organizational legal protections… Parental/guardian consent frameworks… Boundaries and safety protocols for one-on-one interactions with minors… Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
- Decision: include
- Reasoning: Six of the nine listed Key Considerations are direct regulatory/compliance/liability items touching distinct legal regimes (child protection, mandatory reporting statutes, insurance, consent law, juvenile-justice data privacy). This is exactly the "regulatory exposure" trigger the guide describes — not a keyword match but genuine multi-regime compliance complexity where Devil's Advocate alone cannot surface specific mechanisms. Strong trigger; inclusion is warranted regardless of effort level.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Faith-based community organization with 20-year presence in the neighborhood… Core group of 8-10 committed volunteers"
- Decision: exclude
- Reasoning: No governance restructuring, coalition building, institutional change, or regulatory-politics dynamic is in play. The organization is established; the program is internal. Stakeholder complexity exists (teens, parents, schools, juvenile justice referrals, congregation) but is handled adequately by Audience Advocate without needing political-analytical vocabulary like veto players or coalition sequencing.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (no applicable citation — topic is a human-services program, not a technical system)
- Decision: exclude
- Reasoning: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI architecture in the REQUEST. Including Technical Expert would produce the documented "cognitive cage" false-positive pattern where engineering metaphors are misapplied to a non-technical topic.

**Notes:** Tier 3 slot budget at high effort is 0-2. Lawyer is a clean strong trigger and consumes one slot. Accountant was considered for the second slot but does not meet the "moderate or better" bar once the brainstorm's Goals/Key Considerations are weighed — the REQUEST treats the $15K grant and in-kind support as a context constraint, not as the object of design. Slot left unfilled rather than padding with a weak trigger.
