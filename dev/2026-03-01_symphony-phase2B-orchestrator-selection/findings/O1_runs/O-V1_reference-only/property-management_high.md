### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is centered on a single primary domain (short-term rental property management as a service business). There is no cross-system reconciliation or inter-domain bridging in the REQUEST — no merging of distinct institutional systems that constrain each other. The value of the Perspective persona here is creative metaphorical reframing (hospitality, real estate, neighborhood-relations analogies), which is exactly Analogist territory. Default Analogist is retained.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow... Financial modeling... $80K combined savings for startup costs"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence; Contract design with property owners (liability allocation, termination clauses, insurance requirements); Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting; Zoning compliance and permit management... new city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring... state legislature considering statewide preemption... HOA... restrictions"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Significant tension between STR advocates... and opponents... city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue... Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)... Positioning the company as a 'good neighbor' operation"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No reference in REQUEST to engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems. The "noise monitoring" mention is a regulatory requirement, not a technical architecture problem being brainstormed.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three Tier 3 personas met the "strong" threshold (Accountant, Lawyer, Politician), but the high-effort ceiling is 0-2. Accountant was dropped because the Analyst is already in Tier 1 and this topic's financial questions (fee structure, break-even, seasonal cash flow, unit economics) sit in the framework-level territory the Analyst reliably covers — the Accountant's quantified-benchmarks layer is useful but less irreplaceable than Lawyer's specific regulatory mechanisms (STR ordinances, occupancy tax, HOA covenants, insurance lines) or Politician's political-analytical vocabulary for the live city-council / election / HOA-board dynamics. Lawyer and Politician each address distinct, high-salience concerns in the REQUEST that no Tier 1 or Tier 2 persona would generate with comparable specificity.
