### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The food-truck topic has a clear single primary domain (launching and scaling a single-cuisine food truck business). There is no inter-domain reconciliation — no bridging of distinct systems that constrain each other. The Filipino-Mexican fusion cuisine is a creative recombination within a single domain (food/restaurant), not a cross-institutional reconciliation. Metaphorical reframing from adjacent domains (biology, other industries) is more valuable here than structural isomorphism between two systems.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis) ... Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs ... Whether to pursue the SBA loan or bootstrap more conservatively" plus "$60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process ... Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems in the REQUEST — culinary/operational rather than technical-architecture topic.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant is an unambiguous strong trigger (financial structure is central: unit economics, pricing, SBA loan decision, equipment financing, break-even analysis). Lawyer and Politician register as moderate triggers — regulatory context and city council dynamics are present but framed as strategic engagement rather than deep compliance exposure or institutional change, and at medium effort only strong triggers justify inclusion. Devil's Advocate (Tier 1) will cover high-level regulatory and political risk.
