### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST frames the topic as a transition between two distinct professional domains that constrain each other ("leaving my career as an ICU nurse to become a public interest attorney"; "Whether nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law"). The guide explicitly cites "career transitions across fields" as a case where the Connector's isomorphism and recombinant-innovation modes outperform the Analogist; the question is about reconciling two systems, not reframing a single domain metaphorically.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year"; "Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary"; "Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees" — the REQUEST is a personal career-decision exploration, not a regulatory/compliance design problem. Guide flags career-change as a known partial false positive: the Lawyer "responds to the keyword 'lawyer' and produces a regulatory implementation guide rather than decision-exploration questions."
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No institutional change, governance, or coalition dynamics in the REQUEST; the decision is personal ("32 years old, unmarried, no children; supportive family but they think this is risky") with no stakeholder-politics layer.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware-software co-design, or ML/AI systems. Guide explicitly names career-change as a clear false positive where engineering metaphors ("legacy code," "system rewrites") become a cognitive cage.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** The Accountant registers as a moderate trigger (personal financial structure is genuinely central — loan balance, savings runway, opportunity cost, LRAP programs), but the medium-effort threshold is "strong only (0-1)." The financial coverage at medium effort will rest on the Analyst, supplemented by the Questioner, which is adequate given the Analyst carries ~61% of Tier 1 financial questions. If the effort level were high, Accountant would become a defensible inclusion.
