### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST centers on a single primary domain (short-term rental property management) with multiple facets (regulatory, financial, community relations). While these facets interact, they do not constitute distinct mutually-constraining systems in the sense required for a swap (cross-disciplinary technical co-design, multi-institutional merger, or cross-field career transition). Analogist is the correct default — drawing parallels from hospitality, real estate, and regulated-service businesses will serve the ideation better than forced bridging.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Financial modeling: management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow"; "$80K combined savings for startup costs"; "management fee structure" as percentage-of-booking-revenue model.
- Decision: exclude (displaced by ceiling — see Notes)

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "New city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time; state legislature considering statewide preemption of local STR bans; homeowner associations in several target neighborhoods have their own STR restrictions"; "Liability structure: guest injuries, property damage, contractor negligence"; "Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Significant tension between STR advocates (property owners, tourism businesses) and opponents (long-term renters concerned about housing costs, neighbors concerned about noise/parking); city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue"; "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)"; "Positioning the company as a 'good neighbor' operation vs. absentee management stereotype."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering architecture, ML/sensor, or hardware-software co-design decisions present in REQUEST.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three strong Tier 3 triggers (Accountant, Lawyer, Politician) compete for the 0-2 high-effort ceiling. Selected Lawyer + Politician because the regulatory and political dimensions are uniquely foundational and deeply layered (municipal ordinance + state preemption + HOA restrictions; active city council election with STR as a key issue; multi-stakeholder coalition tensions) — this topic cannot be responsibly explored without dedicated Lawyer and Politician viewpoints. The Accountant trigger, while genuinely strong (management fee structure, unit economics, seasonal cash flow), covers financial mechanics that Tier 1 Analyst and Tier 2 First Principles Thinker can meaningfully address in the question set, whereas Lawyer and Politician represent domain expertise that no other roster persona substitutes for.
