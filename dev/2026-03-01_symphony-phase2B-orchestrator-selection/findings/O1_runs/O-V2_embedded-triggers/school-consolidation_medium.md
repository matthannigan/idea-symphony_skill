### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is explicitly a multi-institutional consolidation of three distinct school districts, each with its own union contract, governance structure, funding base, town identity, and community politics — these are distinct systems that mutually constrain the consolidation (e.g., reconciling "3 separate [teacher union] contracts," harmonizing different property tax bases, and winning "voter approval in all three districts"). The selection guide explicitly names "school district consolidation" as a canonical Connector swap case.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%" and "property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: Union contract reconciliation is noted ("3 separate contracts to reconcile"), but the REQUEST does not name specific regulatory frameworks, licensing regimes, statutes, or compliance architectures that would anchor a Lawyer's contribution. State consolidation procedure is implied but not framed as a regulatory-architecture problem.
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"; stakeholders include "Teachers' unions... parent groups, town identity advocates... fiscal conservatives, and state education officials"; goal is to address "the political, financial, and community identity dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed"; "Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts"; "Governance structure for the new unified board"; "Managing the politics of facility closures."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering, hardware/software, ML, or deep-tech architecture content in the REQUEST.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Two strong Tier 3 triggers compete for the single medium-effort slot (Accountant and Politician). Selected Politician because the REQUEST frames voter approval in all three districts as the gating success condition ("any consolidation requires voter approval"), and the stated goal leads with "political, financial, and community identity dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed." The financial dimension is genuinely primary too, but Tier 1 Analyst and Systems Thinker can cover the budget-modeling, funding-formula, and tax-equalization questions, whereas coalition-building, governance design, and town-identity politics have no Tier 1 counterpart of equivalent depth. Under the high-effort ceiling (0-2) both would be included.
