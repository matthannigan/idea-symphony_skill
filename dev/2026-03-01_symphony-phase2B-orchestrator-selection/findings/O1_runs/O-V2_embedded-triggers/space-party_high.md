### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single-domain topic (planning a children's birthday party) with several facets (activities, decorations, logistics, safety, budget) but no distinct, mutually-constraining systems that must be bridged. The swap criteria (career transitions, multi-institutional change, cross-disciplinary technical design) are not met, so Analogist is retained.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Budget-friendly options" (listed under Key Considerations)
- Decision: exclude. This is an explicit false-positive trap — "budget-friendly" here is a consumer-spending concern for a children's party, not financial-structure-central content (no unit economics, revenue modeling, or organizational finance). No moderate or strong trigger.

- Persona: Lawyer

- Persona: Politician

- Persona: Technical Expert

**Notes:** Space-themed 5th birthday party for 11 children is a clean case — a single-domain, low-stakes consumer/family topic. None of the four Tier 3 domains (financial architecture, regulatory compliance, political/governance dynamics, deep technical engineering) are even secondary dimensions of the REQUEST. The Tier 1 + Tier 2 roster (14 personas) already provides strong coverage for age-appropriateness (Audience Advocate, Empath), safety (Devil's Advocate), creativity (Provocateur, Visionary, Storyteller), logistics (Analyst, Systems Thinker), and budget-consciousness within normal scope (First Principles Thinker, Constraint Flipper).
