### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic sits within a single primary domain (short-term rental property management as a hospitality/real-estate service business). Although it has regulatory, financial, and community facets, these are facets of one business, not distinct mutually-constraining systems being bridged or merged. The swap criteria (career transitions across fields, multi-institutional change, cross-disciplinary technical design) are not met.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "management fee structure, break-even per property, seasonal cash flow"; "percentage of booking revenue"; "$80K combined savings for startup costs"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "city recently passed new STR regulations with registration requirements and occupancy taxes"; "city STR ordinance limits permits per neighborhood zone, requires noise monitoring, and mandates local contact person within 30-minute response time"; "state legislature considering statewide preemption"; "homeowner associations ... have their own STR restrictions"; "Tax compliance across municipal occupancy tax, state sales tax, and federal income reporting"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "city council election in 6 months with STR regulation as a key issue"; "Engaging with the political process around STR regulation (city council, HOA boards)"; "Significant tension between STR advocates ... and opponents"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, ML/AI architecture, or similar deep-tech decisions named. Listing optimization and pricing are operational, not architectural.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three strong Tier 3 triggers compete for the single medium-effort slot. Selected Lawyer because the regulatory landscape is the most foundational and multi-layered constraint named in the REQUEST — a new municipal STR ordinance with specific compliance mechanisms (permits, noise monitoring, response-time mandates), potential state preemption, HOA restrictions, and multi-jurisdictional tax compliance. The company's viability is gated on navigating this stacked regulatory architecture before financial modeling or political engagement become decisive. Accountant and Politician concerns will be partially covered by Analyst and Audience Advocate / Systems Thinker respectively at Tier 1.
