### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain program design (one-on-one youth mentorship within one faith-based organization). There are no distinct, mutually constraining systems that need bridging or reconciliation (no cross-institutional merger, no cross-disciplinary technical design, no career-transition-style domain crossing). Multiple facets (safety, training, outcomes, recruitment) all sit within the youth-mentorship domain, which per the rules is NOT a swap case.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)"
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: The REQUEST mentions funding amount but does not name unit economics, revenue models, pricing strategy, cash-flow modeling, or capital allocation as primary dimensions. This is a small-nonprofit budget context, not financial-structure-central content.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers"; "Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors"; "Liability insurance and organizational legal protections"; "Parental/guardian consent frameworks"; "Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
- Decision: include
- Reason: The REQUEST explicitly names multiple specific regulatory/compliance frameworks — mandatory reporting statutes, background check disqualification criteria, liability architecture, minor consent law, and juvenile justice data privacy. Regulatory/compliance exposure is a primary dimension of safely operating a mentorship program for minors, not a peripheral concern. This is regulatory need (not a false-positive legal keyword).

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "Faith-based community organization with 20-year presence in the neighborhood"
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: No coalition building, voter approval, governance restructuring, institutional merger, or multi-stakeholder political conflict is named. The organization operates independently; there is no political-dynamics-driven change at stake.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (no technical architecture content in REQUEST)
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: The REQUEST names no engineering tradeoffs, system architecture, ML/hardware design, or deep-tech decisions. "Measuring program outcomes" is program evaluation, not engineering architecture.

**Notes:** Clean call — Lawyer is the only Tier 3 persona with a strong trigger, and the medium-effort rules permit up to one strong-trigger Tier 3 inclusion. The concentration of specific regulatory-framework citations (mandatory reporting, background checks, minor consent, juvenile justice data privacy, liability insurance) makes this one of the clearest Lawyer triggers possible for a non-commercial topic.
