### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is a textbook multi-institutional change scenario — three separate K-12 districts, each with its own union contract, tax base, town identity, and governance structure, are being merged into a single unified district. These are distinct systems that mutually constrain each other (e.g., "3 separate teachers' union contracts to reconcile," "property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases," "which towns lose 'their' school"), which matches the guide's explicit example ("school district consolidation") for the Connector swap.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Budget modeling for the consolidated entity (staffing, transportation, facilities maintenance)... revenue impact: state funding formula changes, property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond obligations... true cost savings vs. transition costs, facility decisions, debt consolidation."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Union contract reconciliation and staffing transition plans... Governance structure for the new unified board... any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts."
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts... Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)... Stakeholders: Teachers' unions, parent groups, town identity advocates ('our school IS our town'), fiscal conservatives, and state education officials encouraging consolidation."
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering/architecture tradeoffs named in the REQUEST; transportation logistics and facility decisions are operational, not deep-tech.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Three Tier 3 candidates had at-or-above-moderate triggers (Accountant strong, Politician strong, Lawyer moderate) competing for the 0-2 ceiling. Selected the two STRONG triggers (Accountant + Politician) because the consolidation literally cannot move forward without both (1) a defensible financial model showing the consolidated district is solvent under declining-enrollment scenarios and (2) a political coalition strategy that can win voter approval in all three districts. Lawyer's moderate trigger (union contract reconciliation, governance statutes) is real but secondary — the REQUEST frames union/governance issues as political and fiscal problems more than regulatory-architecture problems, and excluded Tier 3 territory is partially absorbed by Systems Thinker and Devil's Advocate.
