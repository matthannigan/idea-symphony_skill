### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain undertaking — a neighborhood nonprofit operating a tool lending service. There are no distinct, mutually-constraining systems to bridge (no cross-institutional merger, no cross-disciplinary technical stack, no career transition). The facets named (sustainability, equity, maintenance, liability, community buy-in) are all facets of one coherent operational domain, which per the embedded rule is NOT a swap case. Analogist is well-suited to drawing comparisons to libraries, co-ops, seed banks, and other sharing institutions.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "Small neighborhood nonprofit with modest budget"; "Tool maintenance and replacement costs"; "Sustainability beyond initial enthusiasm"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer

- Decision: exclude (REQUEST names liability as a generic concern without specifying any regulatory framework, statute, licensing regime, or compliance architecture. Per the false-positive trap, "safety concerns" or "liability" without specific regulatory frameworks is Devil's Advocate territory, not Lawyer.)

- Persona: Politician

- Decision: exclude (Community engagement and buy-in are present, but the REQUEST does not name coalition building, voter approval, governance structure, institutional change, or multi-stakeholder political conflict. This is community outreach, not stakeholder politics hinging on political dynamics.)

- Persona: Technical Expert

**Notes:** Accountant included at moderate strength because operational financial sustainability — maintenance/replacement costs, modest nonprofit budget, long-run viability after initial grant enthusiasm — is a meaningful secondary dimension of the REQUEST, though not a primary one (no unit economics, revenue model, or capital-allocation architecture is specified). At high effort the moderate threshold permits inclusion, well within the 0-2 ceiling. No other Tier 3 persona reaches moderate.
