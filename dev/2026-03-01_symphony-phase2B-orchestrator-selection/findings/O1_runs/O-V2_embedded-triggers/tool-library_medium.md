### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain initiative (a neighborhood nonprofit tool lending library). While it has several facets (sustainability, equity, maintenance, liability, community buy-in), they all sit within one organizational/community domain. There are no distinct, mutually-constraining systems requiring bridging (no cross-institutional merger, no career transition across fields, no cross-disciplinary technical design). Default Analogist is the right fit for drawing comparisons to other lending/sharing models.
