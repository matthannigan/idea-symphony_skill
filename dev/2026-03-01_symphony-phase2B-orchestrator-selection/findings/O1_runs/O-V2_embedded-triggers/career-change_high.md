### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST explicitly asks the requester to bridge two distinct and mutually constraining professional systems — "leaving my career as an ICU nurse to become a public interest attorney" — and to evaluate whether "nursing experience is an asset or irrelevant in immigration law." This is a canonical career-transition-across-fields swap case where clinical healthcare and public interest law must be reconciled, not a single-domain topic with multiple facets.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year ... Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary ... Loan repayment assistance programs for public interest lawyers"
- Decision: exclude
- Note: This is personal-finance decision-exploration (opportunity cost at an individual level), not the unit economics, revenue models, pricing strategy, or organizational financial architecture that a STRONG Accountant trigger requires. Analyst and First Principles Thinker adequately cover the personal financial math.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "become a public interest attorney that helps immigrants and refugees ... complete an LSAT, 3-year JD program, and pass the bar"
- Decision: exclude
- Note: Explicit false-positive trap — "lawyer/attorney" here is the TARGET CAREER being considered, not a regulatory/compliance exposure in the REQUEST itself. The brainstorming need is personal decision-exploration about entering the legal profession, not regulatory implementation of a venture. No specific regulatory framework, statute, licensing regime, or compliance architecture governs the requester's decision.

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (none — no coalition building, voter approval, governance, or institutional-change content in the REQUEST)
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (none — no engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, or deep-tech architecture in the REQUEST)
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** The dominant axes here — calling vs. burnout, opportunity cost, emotional sustainability, identity transition, integrating prior experience — are handled by the Tier 1 + Tier 2 roster (Empath, Storyteller, Connector, First Principles Thinker, Futurist, Devil's Advocate). None of the four Tier 3 candidates clear the moderate threshold once the career-change and legal-career false-positive traps are applied.
