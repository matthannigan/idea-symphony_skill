### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: The topic is a single-domain product strategy problem (simplifying one mobile app to its core). There are no distinct, mutually-constraining systems to bridge — feature prioritization, user research, team morale, and technical debt are all facets of the same product-strategy domain. Analogist is the better fit for cross-industry analogies about simplification (e.g., 37signals, In-N-Out's focused menu).
