### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck startup is a single-domain topic (food-service venture) with multiple facets (pricing, regulation, operations, expansion), but these facets do not constitute distinct, mutually constraining systems. There is no cross-disciplinary bridging requirement (e.g., no ML + privacy + regulatory + hardware intersection, no multi-institutional merger). Default Analogist is the correct choice.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "clear pricing strategy, unit economics, and a realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation"; "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)"; "Cash flow management through seasonal fluctuations"; "Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
- Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: moderate
- Topic citation: "the pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process"; "Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
- Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: No engineering-tradeoff content in REQUEST.
- Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant is a clean strong trigger (central goal explicitly names unit economics, pricing strategy, and break-even analysis as core deliverables). With the 0-2 high-effort ceiling and Accountant consuming one slot, the second slot was contested between Lawyer (moderate) and Politician (moderate) — both triggered by the same pending-ordinance situation. Politician selected over Lawyer because the REQUEST frames the regulatory situation primarily as an engagement/coalition problem ("engage with the regulatory process," "building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association") rather than as a compliance-architecture problem; the legal requirements (permits, commissary, vending zones) are relatively standard food-truck regulatory surface and Devil's Advocate + Systems Thinker can adequately probe compliance edges, whereas the stakeholder/political engagement dimension benefits from dedicated Politician perspective.
