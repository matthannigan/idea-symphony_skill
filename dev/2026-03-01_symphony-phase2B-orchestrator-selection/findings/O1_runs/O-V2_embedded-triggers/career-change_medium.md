### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is explicitly a career transition across two distinct professional systems (ICU bedside nursing and public-interest immigration law) that mutually constrain each other — nursing experience, loan-repayment pathways, and healthcare-adjacent immigrant advocacy need to be bridged into the legal-career decision. This matches the embedded swap criterion ("career transitions across fields") and goes beyond a single-domain topic with multiple facets, so Connector replaces Analogist.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "$22K remaining in nursing school loans; $45K in savings; current salary $82K/year" and "Financial reality of 3 years without income + law school debt vs. public interest salary"
- Decision: exclude
  - The financial content is personal budgeting and opportunity-cost analysis for an individual, not unit economics, revenue models, pricing strategy, or organizational capital allocation. Per the embedded false-positive trap, consumer/personal-finance framing is not an Accountant trigger. Analyst and Systems Thinker (Tier 1) can handle the personal financial modeling.

The REQUEST asks about personal decision-exploration (calling vs. burnout, timeline, loan mechanics), not regulatory architecture, statutes, or compliance design. No Lawyer trigger.
