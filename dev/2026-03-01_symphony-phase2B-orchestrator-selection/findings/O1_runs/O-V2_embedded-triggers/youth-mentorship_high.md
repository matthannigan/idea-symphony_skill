### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This topic is a single-domain program-design problem (youth mentorship program launch). While it has multiple facets (legal compliance, volunteer training, matching, safety protocols), these are not distinct mutually-constraining systems in the sense that would warrant Connector. There is one primary domain (youth-serving program design), so Analogist remains the right fit for drawing on analogous programs (Big Brothers Big Sisters, AmeriCorps mentorship, school-based mentoring, faith-based youth programs, etc.).

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church (meeting space, administrative staff)"
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: The REQUEST mentions a funding amount but does not name unit economics, revenue model, pricing strategy, or financial-structure-central content. Budget sustainability is a concern but not a primary financial-modeling dimension.

- Persona: Lawyer
- Trigger strength: strong
- Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers; Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors; Liability insurance and organizational legal protections; Parental/guardian consent frameworks; Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
- Decision: include
- Reason: The REQUEST explicitly names multiple specific regulatory frameworks and compliance architectures — mandatory reporting (a statutory obligation), liability insurance structure, parental consent frameworks, data privacy for minors (with juvenile-justice-referral implications), and safety protocols for adult–minor interactions. This is regulatory/compliance as a primary dimension, not a keyword. The stated goal explicitly includes "meeting legal requirements."

- Persona: Politician
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (no stakeholder-politics / governance / coalition-building / voter-approval / institutional-change content in REQUEST)
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: The program is internal to a single faith-based organization. No coalition building, governance structure, institutional merger, or multi-stakeholder political conflict is named.

- Persona: Technical Expert
- Trigger strength: none
- Topic citation: (no technical architecture content in REQUEST)
- Decision: exclude
- Reason: No engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software co-design, ML architecture, or deep-tech decisions are named. A case-management system might eventually be needed but is not a stated dimension.

**Notes:** Only one Tier 3 trigger fires at or above moderate (Lawyer, strong). The 0-2 ceiling is not binding here — Lawyer is the clean single inclusion. At high effort the regulatory dimension of youth-serving programs with juvenile-justice referrals is substantial enough on its own to warrant a dedicated Tier 3 slot.
