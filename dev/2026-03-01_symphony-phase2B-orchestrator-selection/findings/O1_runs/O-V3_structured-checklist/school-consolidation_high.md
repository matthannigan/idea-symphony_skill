### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST explicitly frames the brainstorming need as reconciling three distinct school districts plus teachers' unions, parent groups, town identity advocates, fiscal conservatives, and state education officials — multi-institutional change with mutually constraining systems. This matches the prompt's own Connector-swap example ("school-district consolidation — districts + unions + communities + state").

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%... consolidated district could reduce administrative duplication... saving an estimated $1.2M annually"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)... any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts"
  - Decision: exclude

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts... Building a coalition that can win voter approval... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures (which towns lose 'their' school?)"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Transportation logistics and costs for a larger geographic district"
  - Decision: exclude
