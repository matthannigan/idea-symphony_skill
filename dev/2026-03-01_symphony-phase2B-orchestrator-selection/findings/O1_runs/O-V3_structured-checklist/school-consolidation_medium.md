### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: Rural school-district consolidation is the canonical multi-institutional change case — three districts, three teachers' unions, three town-identity communities, county board, and state education officials are distinct systems that structurally constrain each other, and reconciling them IS the brainstorming need (explicitly: "political, financial, and community identity dimensions that will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed").

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "combined annual operating budgets total $28M with $4.2M in deferred building maintenance; state per-pupil funding formula penalizes small districts, meaning consolidation would increase per-pupil allocation by ~12%... saving an estimated $1.2M annually"
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — medium-effort ceiling forces a single pick)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teachers' unions (3 separate contracts to reconcile)" / "any consolidation requires voter approval"
  - Decision: exclude (no named regulatory framework or compliance regime; union contract reconciliation is framed as political negotiation, and voter approval is governance rather than regulatory compliance)

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "any consolidation requires voter approval in all three districts... Building a coalition that can win voter approval in all three districts... Governance structure for the new unified board... Managing the politics of facility closures"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: (no relevant content — no engineering, ML, hardware, or systems-architecture dimensions)
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Accountant deferred despite a strong trigger. Medium-effort ceiling of 1 Tier 3 persona forced a pick between Accountant and Politician. Politician selected as most central because the REQUEST's goal statement foregrounds voter approval as the binding constraint ("will determine whether a consolidation vote can succeed") — without political coalition success, the financial analysis cannot be realized. If effort were raised to high, Accountant should be added (financial structure is unusually rich: per-pupil funding formula, property tax equalization across districts with different tax bases, bond/debt consolidation, structural deficits, transition-cost modeling).
