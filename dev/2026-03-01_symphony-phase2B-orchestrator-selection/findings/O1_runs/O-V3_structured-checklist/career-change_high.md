### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST is a career transition across two distinct professional systems (ICU nursing / clinical healthcare and public interest immigration law) bridged by one person; reconciling whether nursing experience is "an asset or irrelevant in immigration law" and finding "alternative paths that combine healthcare and immigrant advocacy" is the central brainstorming need, matching the checklist's "career transitions across fields" swap example.
