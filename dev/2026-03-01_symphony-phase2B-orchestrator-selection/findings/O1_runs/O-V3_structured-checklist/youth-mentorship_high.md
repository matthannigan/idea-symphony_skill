### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single-community, single-program initiative (one faith-based org launching one mentorship program in one neighborhood). Multi-stakeholder coordination (schools, juvenile justice referrals, parents, congregation) does not equal multi-system reconciliation — there are no distinct systems that structurally constrain each other and require bridging. Analogist's default cross-domain analogy-making is the better fit.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church"
  - Decision: exclude (funding is bounded context — fixed seed grant + in-kind support — not a financial-structure design problem; no unit economics, revenue model, pricing, or capital allocation to brainstorm)

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Background check requirements and disqualifying criteria for volunteers; Mandatory reporting obligations and training for mentors; Liability insurance and organizational legal protections; Parental/guardian consent frameworks; Data privacy for minors, especially those referred through juvenile justice"
  - Decision: include (multiple named regulatory regimes — mandatory reporting statutes, juvenile-justice data-privacy protections, minor-consent frameworks, background-check compliance — and the stated goal explicitly includes "meeting legal requirements," making compliance architecture central, not ambient)

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: "Teens ages 13-17 identified through school counselors and juvenile justice referrals" / "~40 interested adults from the congregation and partner organizations"
  - Decision: exclude (multi-stakeholder program operations, but no coalition-building, voter approval, governance design, or institutional-change politics — stakeholders are operational partners, not a political decision arena)

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: [no technical-architecture content in REQUEST]
  - Decision: exclude (no engineering tradeoffs, hardware/software, ML, or systems-architecture decisions present)
