### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Connector (swap)
- Swap rationale: The REQUEST frames the feature-pruning decision as a reconciliation of four structurally linked systems that constrain each other — user base (retention/churn risk from simplification), team (6 engineers/1 designer/1 PM burned out from maintaining 15 half-built features), runway (8 months of funding), and codebase (technical debt from deeply integrated features). Per the checklist's explicit swap example ("Feature-pruning where user/team/runway/codebase are structurally linked problems"), the brainstorming need is to bridge these distinct systems, so the default Analogist is swapped for the Connector.
