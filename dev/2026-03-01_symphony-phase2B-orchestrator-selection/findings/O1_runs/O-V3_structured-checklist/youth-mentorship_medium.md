### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: This is a single-community initiative (one faith-based org launching a program in one urban neighborhood). Multiple stakeholders (church, schools, juvenile justice, foundation) are present, but they are not distinct systems that constrain each other in a way that requires cross-system reconciliation as the brainstorming need. Per the guidance, single-community multi-stakeholder situations keep Analogist.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant

  - Topic citation: "$15K seed grant from a regional foundation, plus in-kind support from the church"

  - Rationale: Budget figure is a context detail, not a financial-mechanism design need. No unit economics, revenue models, pricing strategy, or business-model design dimensions are present in the brainstorming goal.
