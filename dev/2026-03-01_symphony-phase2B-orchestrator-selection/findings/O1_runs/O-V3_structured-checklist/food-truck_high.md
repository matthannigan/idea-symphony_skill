### Selection Rationale

**Connector/Analogist decision:** Analogist (default)
- Swap rationale: Food-truck is a single-domain business strategy topic — no distinct constraining systems requiring reconciliation. Listed explicitly as an Analogist-keeper example in the swap criteria.

**Tier 3 selections — structured rationale per candidate persona:**

- Persona: Accountant
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "Pricing strategy and unit economics (food cost ratios, break-even analysis)"; "$60K savings + potential $40K SBA microloan"; "Equipment financing vs. leasing tradeoffs"; Goals call for "clear pricing strategy, unit economics, and a realistic path from single truck to multi-revenue-stream operation"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Lawyer
  - Trigger strength: strong
  - Topic citation: "City requires food truck permits, commissary kitchen use, and designated vending zones; city council is currently debating new food truck ordinances that would restrict downtown vending hours and add annual licensing fees"
  - Decision: include

- Persona: Politician
  - Trigger strength: moderate
  - Topic citation: "The pending city ordinance changes and how to engage with the regulatory process"; "Building relationships with city officials and the local food truck association"
  - Decision: exclude (deferred — ceiling reached)

- Persona: Technical Expert
  - Trigger strength: none
  - Topic citation: — (no architectural/engineering/ML content; food-truck equipment is operational, not architectural)
  - Decision: exclude

**Notes:** Politician (moderate) deferred as alternative. Three Tier 3 personas qualified at strong/moderate under the high-effort rule; ceiling of 2 forced a pick. Accountant and Lawyer chosen as most central — the stated Goals emphasize unit economics/pricing/revenue paths (Accountant) and the pending ordinance + named permit/commissary/zoning regime (Lawyer) is an active business risk, not ambient context. Politician's stakeholder-engagement angle is real but supports regulatory navigation rather than being the central decision; swap in if Lawyer coverage proves redundant with Analyst/Systems Thinker on the ordinance question.
