## 1. Headline judgment

**Yes — the incremental Phase 2, Phase 3, and orchestration gains aggregate to strong end-to-end outputs.** Across four topics drawn from very different registers (life-decision, business plan, kid's birthday, B2C app PRD), the four runs at each level produce a *consistent* shape: 10–11 persistent through-lines preserved from `min` to `high`, ~3–4× question growth versus ~15–19× persona-response growth (Phase 3 carrying the marginal investment exactly as designed), executive-summary thesis evolving meaningfully across levels without contradiction, and convergence-count phrasing (`"six of seven personas converged…"`, `"six of twelve clusters reframe…"`) appearing as native Phase-3 → Phase-4 → Phase-5 propagation in three of four `high` runs. The architecture is doing what it was supposed to do.

The honest qualifier: the four comparisons evaluate Phase 5's **output** (BRAINSTORM.md) and infer Phase 2/3 quality through it. They do not directly inspect SUMMARIES.md or SYNTHESIS.md, so Phase 4 and the Phase 4 → Phase 5 hop remain unobserved. That gap is the biggest reason to focus next research investment on Phase 4 and Phase 5.

## 2. Evidence the gains aggregate

**Through-line preservation is unusually clean.** Each of the four comparisons identifies 10–11 through-lines that appear in *every* BRAINSTORM.md from `min` through `high`. Cross-topic specifics:

- career-change: 11 through-lines, including "ICU and immigration-law burnout are structurally similar" and "calling can't be assessed from inside burnout."
- food-truck: 11 through-lines, including "Filipino-Mexican fusion is not recipe-defensible" and "single-channel discipline before diversification."
- space-party: 10 through-lines, including "one engineered peak beats distributed spectacle" and "transitions are the failure site, not the activities."
- habit-tracker: 11 through-lines, including "anti-gamification as brand promise" and "real competitor is paper, not named apps."

The fidelity verdicts in §3 of each document are unanimous: a `min` reader gets the operational backbone; higher levels add scaffolding, not different conclusions. That is the strongest single signal that Phase 2 (clustering) and Phase 3 (independent persona generation) are producing genuinely overlapping insights from independent perspectives — not artefacts of shared scaffolding.

**Effort scaling matches architectural intent.** Question growth lands at 3.4–4.3× from `min` to `high` across topics; persona-response growth lands at 15–19×. The cost is overwhelmingly in Phase 3, exactly as the design predicted. None of the four comparisons reports the Phase 2 expansion outpacing or eclipsing Phase 3 — clustering is doing structural work (4–5 clusters at `min`, 9–10 at `low`/`med`, 11–12 at `high`) that the per-cluster persona work then expands into.

**Productive drift lands in the right zones.** Each topic's comparison identifies 3–6 zones where higher-effort runs *sharpen* recommendations. The zones are topic-shaped rather than mechanical: career-change drifts on financial planning, age-timing, alternative-paths, and emotional framing; food-truck drifts on pricing, SBA loan timing, revenue mix sequencing, and ordinance engagement; space-party drifts on peak placement, decoration, food/cake, adult capacity, and take-home; habit-tracker drifts on pricing, MVP scope, streak/grace, and data architecture. Phase 2's clustering and Phase 3's persona spread are landing on the axes where the underlying question actually has tension.

**Convergence-count phrasing is the cleanest single propagation signal.** `"Six of seven personas independently converge…"` appears at `high` in food-truck, habit-tracker, and space-party. Career-change's `high` run does the same with `"6/7 personas: same OS, different uniform"` and `"Six of seven personas... independently arrive at this diagnosis"`. This phrasing is generated by Phase 3 (parallel personas), preserved by Phase 4's attributed/synthesis split, and surfaced in Phase 5's executive summary. The fact that it propagates intact in four of four `high` runs means the attribution channel from `synthesis/attributed/{cluster}.md` through to BRAINSTORM.md is working — at least at `high`.

**Orchestration durability is visible but not load-tested.** The model audit trail (added recently) and effort-conditional prompt branching (`phase4_full-synthesis.md` vs. `phase4_summary-only_low-effort.md` vs. `phase4_summary-only_min-effort.md`) both show up across runs. The pattern is repeatable across the four topics, which is mild evidence that the orchestration is durable rather than brittle.

## 3. Where the seams show

**Methodological blind spot: Phase 4 → Phase 5 is unobserved.** The four comparisons read PLAN/QUESTIONS/BRAINSTORM only. They infer Phase 4 and Phase 5 quality from the BRAINSTORM at face value. Concretely: if Phase 4 dropped a through-line that personas raised in Phase 3, or if Phase 5 hallucinated a convergence count not present in `synthesis/attributed/`, the four comparisons would not catch it. The clean propagation signal in §2 above is necessary but not sufficient.

**BRAINSTORM section structure varies more than it should.** The food-truck and space-party agents both reported having to flex the comparison's table density to match topic shape; the habit-tracker run reports executive-summary structure shifting meaningfully across levels (single-thesis → tension-foregrounded → architectural-unlock → five-reframe). Some of this is healthy adaptation to topic register, but it suggests `templates/brainstorm.md` plus the SKILL.md inline Phase 5 instructions may not be specifying the contract tightly enough — the orchestrator has too much latitude on what an executive summary contains, and it shows.

**Stale PLAN.md text leaks into downstream analyses.** Two of the agents flagged operational issues from PLAN.md text that are already-fixed in the current pipeline. PLAN.md is meant to record session state, not be a permanent record; either it should be regenerated when a session is re-run, or the comparison prompt should warn agents that PLAN.md from old sessions may reference resolved issues. This is a Phase 1 / orchestration housekeeping issue worth noting because it leaks into every comparison.

**Comparison-prompt self-pruning.** Two of four prose word counts (food-truck, space-party) ran above the 2,500-word target despite the agents being told the bound. This is a prompt-engineering issue on the comparison prompt itself, not a Symphony pipeline issue — lower priority.

## 4. Phase 4: recommended research and test track

Phase 4 has had three recent commits (`d0d477f Split SUMMARIES and SYNTHESIS`, `e44601c Enhance Phase 4 summary prompts and outline Phase 5 investigation`, `00a6da1 model audit trail`) but no structured investigation series comparable to the Phase 2B (PR1/PI1/O1, GT1) and Phase 2C (BL1/SP1/PC1/TDA1) tracks. The four comparisons surface Phase 4 only by inference; a focused investigation should make Phase 4 quality directly observable.

Concrete test items, patterned on the Phase 2 GT/SP/PC/PI/O/PR series:

- **F4-FA1 (Faithfulness Audit, full-synthesis path).** Use `synthesis/attributed/{cluster}.md` as ground truth. For a fixed `med` and `high` run, score whether each persona's distinctive contribution survives into `{cluster}_synthesis.md` and `{cluster}_summary.md`. Targets: zero dropped persona names, zero hallucinated quotes, all convergence counts traceable.
- **F4-LB1 (Loss-Budget, summary-only path).** Run `phase4_summary-only_min-effort.md` and `phase4_summary-only_low-effort.md` against responses where the underlying through-line set is known (use a `med` run's attributed file as reference). Quantify the fraction of through-lines visible in responses that survive into the summary.
- **F4-PP1 (Three-Prompt Parity).** `phase4_full-synthesis.md`, `phase4_summary-only_low-effort.md`, and `phase4_summary-only_min-effort.md` are evolving independently. Document the contract surface they share (output schema, attribution rules, blind-spot detection requirements) versus where they may legitimately diverge by effort level. Catch drift before the three prompts diverge in ways that break downstream Phase 5 assumptions.
- **F4-MA1 (Model Assignment Review).** Now that the model audit trail is wired in, query it across the four topics × four levels and assess synthesis quality by which model wrote which artefact. Specifically test whether `med` Haiku synthesis matches `med` Sonnet synthesis on a controlled re-run of the same Phase-3 outputs.
- **F4-RG1 (Cross-Effort Regression).** Freeze a Phase 2/3 output set, run Phase 4 across all four effort branches against it, diff the outputs. Characterize variance and identify which differences are intentional (effort branching) vs. drift.

The faithfulness and loss-budget audits are the two highest-leverage items because they directly answer the question the four comparisons cannot: does Phase 4 actually preserve what Phase 3 produced?

## 5. Phase 5: recommended research and test track

Phase 5 is the most under-investigated stage in the pipeline. There is no dedicated subagent prompt — `BRAINSTORM.md` is generated by the orchestrator inline from `templates/brainstorm.md` plus instructions in SKILL.md, against `QUESTIONS.md` + `SUMMARIES.md` (and `SYNTHESIS.md` at `med`/`high`) as input. The only Phase-5-specific memo is `dev/2026-04-23_symphony-phase5-investigation.md`. Recent commits include zero dedicated Phase 5 work beyond the outline note in e44601c.

The four comparisons make a positive case that Phase 5 is *doing real synthesis work* — executive-summary thesis evolves meaningfully across effort levels, convergence counts propagate, through-lines are preserved. But the structural variability noted in §3 and the inability to verify Phase 4 → Phase 5 fidelity from outside both point to the same intervention.

Concrete test items:

- **F5-EX1 (Extraction).** Convert Phase 5 from an inline orchestrator step to a dedicated subagent prompt at `idea-symphony/prompts/phase5_brainstorm-generation.md`. Until Phase 5 lives in its own prompt file with explicit input/output contracts, it cannot be tested in isolation, A/B-tested, or model-swapped. This is the highest-leverage single intervention in the pipeline.
- **F5-FA1 (Faithfulness Audit).** Once F5-EX1 is done, run a claim-by-claim audit: every assertion in BRAINSTORM.md must trace back to a source in `SYNTHESIS.md` (or `SUMMARIES.md` at `min`/`low`). Catch hallucinations, dropped through-lines, misattributed convergence counts, and synthesis claims that evaporate into the executive summary. The four comparisons make Phase 5 inputs (BRAINSTORM) easy to score; the missing piece is the source document.
- **F5-RP1 (Reproducibility).** The habit-tracker `effort-comparison_original.md` × `effort-comparison.md` pair is an accidental proof-of-concept: same source data, same prompt, regenerated — strong convergence on every load-bearing claim. Repeat this deliberately. Run the same effort level twice on the same `REQUEST.md`+`SUMMARIES.md` and diff the BRAINSTORM exec-summaries. Quantify the variance budget.
- **F5-TC1 (Template Contract).** The structural variability across topics suggests `templates/brainstorm.md` is either over-constraining (forcing structure where content doesn't warrant it) or under-constraining (allowing the orchestrator to drift). Audit BRAINSTORM section presence/absence/length across the four topics × four levels and decide whether the template needs tightening or whether topic-flex is intentional.
- **F5-EB1 (Effort-Conditional Branching Validation).** At `min`/`low` the BRAINSTORM links to `_summary.md`; at `med`/`high` to `_synthesis.md`. Verify the link choice matches the effort level on every emitted run. This is a cheap script-level check but currently not wired in anywhere.
- **F5-RB1 (Roster-Bias Check).** Phase 5 reads `QUESTIONS.md` for cluster organization. Does the BRAINSTORM exec summary disproportionately reflect early-listed clusters? Cheap check: shuffle cluster order in `QUESTIONS.md` between two re-runs, diff exec summaries.

Of these, F5-EX1 is a near-prerequisite for the rest — once Phase 5 is a testable artefact rather than inline orchestrator code, the other items become straightforward.

## 6. Final summary

The aggregate signal across the four comparisons is positive: Phase 2 clustering is producing structurally sensible expansion, Phase 3 persona generation is producing genuinely overlapping insights from independent perspectives, and the orchestration scaffolding (model audit trail, effort-conditional branching, cluster→persona attribution) is intact end-to-end. A user reading any of the four `BRAINSTORM.md` outputs at any effort level gets a coherent product — and reading the same topic at higher effort gets meaningful sharpening, not contradiction. The Phase 2 and Phase 3 investments have compounded.

The next investment should sit on Phase 4 and Phase 5. Phase 4 needs a structured investigation series modelled on the Phase 2B/2C tracks — faithfulness and loss-budget audits are the highest-leverage starting points because they directly close the inference gap the four comparisons cannot close from outside. Phase 5 needs to be **extracted from SKILL.md into a dedicated subagent prompt** before any other Phase 5 work; without that, the entire stage remains untestable in isolation, and the structural variability already visible in BRAINSTORM exec summaries cannot be diagnosed or fixed.
