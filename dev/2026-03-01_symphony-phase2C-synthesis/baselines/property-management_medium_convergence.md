# property-management — medium — Convergence Baseline

**Input file:** test-data/property-management_medium.md
**Synthesize pool:** 92 questions across 7 personas (6 Tier 1 + Lawyer as selected Tier 3)
**Convergence pairs/groups:** 15
**Questions participating in convergence:** 65 (of 92 total, 71%)
**Convergence density:** 71%

Convergence density of 71% is high — the topic's spine concerns (regulatory risk, HOA navigation, liability allocation, good-neighbor positioning, owner trust) are recognized similarly across multiple persona lenses. This is consistent with property-management being a well-structured real-world business problem where the major concerns are visible from every analytical angle, even when the proposed interventions differ.

## Convergence Group 1: "Regulatory scenarios & political flip risk"

**Theme:** Scenario-level planning for worst-case regulatory outcomes (election flip, preemption, bans) and how the business pivots. Multiple personas independently identify scenario-diversity and pivot-readiness as critical strategic questions.

**Cluster:** B1 Cluster 1 (Regulatory Scenarios)

**Questions:**
- [Analyst Q1] "Regulatory scenario modeling — (a) status quo, (b) stricter limits, (c) state preemption, (d) complete ban"
- [Devil's Advocate Q1] "Permit Caps as an Existential Threat — alternative strategies (subleasing, permitted-only, pivot to LTR)"
- [Devil's Advocate Q2] "Election Volatility Risk — contingency plans for worst-case scenarios"
- [Questioner Q1] "Assumption-challenge on regulatory stability — preemption AFTER election"
- [Systems Thinker Q1] "City council election creating a 'worse before better' dynamic"
- [Storyteller Q3] "Envision the day a city council election changes everything"

**Merge recommendation:** Preserve all distinct. Each persona attacks scenario planning from a meaningfully different angle: Analyst quantifies scenarios as a decision matrix; DA Q1 names existential risk and pivot options; DA Q2 names contingency triggers; Questioner asks about timing interactions; Systems Thinker names the feedback-loop dynamic; Storyteller walks through the post-election cascade as lived experience. Cluster-level merging at synthesis output is appropriate; per-question merging would lose the distinct cognitive operations (scenario-enumeration, pivot-option design, timing analysis, system-dynamics, narrative-walkthrough).
