## Cluster 1: Financial Analysis, Break-Even & Transition Costs

**Description:** The dollars-and-cents viability question — true break-even analysis (administrative savings vs. one-time transition expenses), bond/debt reconciliation across districts, tax-base equalization, unit economics at different enrollment levels, and the fiscal floor for a viable rural district. Covers the "does the financial case actually pencil out?" layer.
**Question count in cluster:** 10 (Synthesize 9 + Append cross-ref 1)
**Contributing personas:** Analyst (5: Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, Q13), Devil's Advocate (2: Q1, Q2), Questioner (2: Q5, Q7) | Append cross-ref: Provocateur (1: Q4 deliberate inefficiency as rescue-framing)
**Primary dimension:** Strategic
**Sample questions (up to 5):**
- "What is the true break-even analysis for consolidation?" — one-time transition costs (legal, systems integration, contract reconciliation, facility closure) vs $1.2M annual admin savings [Analyst Q1]
- "How does the unit economics change at different enrollment levels?" — fiscal floor for a viable rural district at projected 15% further decline [Analyst Q2]
- "How do debt and bond obligations differ across the three districts?" — pooling vs pre-consolidation voter responsibility [Analyst Q4]
- "The financial case assumes $1.2M annual savings, but what one-time/first-year costs might consume years of savings?" [Devil's Advocate Q1]
- "Has anyone verified whether the 12% per-pupil funding increase would actually materialize?" — state funding trap scenarios [Devil's Advocate Q2]

## Orphan Questions (Not Cluster-Forming)

Per D1, the 7 Provocateur questions are treated as orphans from a thematic-clustering standpoint — their value is the provocation itself (absurd inversion + "real insight"), not the underlying topic they happen to probe. Each cross-pollinates multiple clusters (e.g., Q1 "school districts as wrong unit of analysis" touches Cluster 5 Governance + Cluster 13 Alternative Models; Q5 "declining enrollment as asset" touches Cluster 2 Enrollment + Cluster 8 Educational Quality), but assigning them to a single cluster destroys their distinctive framing.

**Orphan list (all Provocateur, 7):**
1. What if "school districts" were the wrong unit of analysis entirely? (Q1)
2. What if the goal was to make consolidation fail spectacularly? (Q2)
3. What if every student attended a different "school" every week? (Q3)
4. What if districts deliberately made themselves LESS efficient before consolidating? (Q4)
5. What if declining enrollment was framed as an educational asset rather than a crisis? (Q5)
6. What if we planned for the district to be dissolved in 20 years? (Q6)
7. What if consolidation required every student to change schools, not just some? (Q7)

For B4 round-robin, Provocateur's *internal* clusters are used — not the B1 thematic clusters.
