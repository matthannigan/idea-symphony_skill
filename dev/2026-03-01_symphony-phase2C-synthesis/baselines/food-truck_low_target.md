### Cluster C1 — Financial Model, Capital & Unit Economics

**Must include (3):**
1. **[Merged from CG3: Anal Q5, DA Q2, DA Q3, Quest Q6, Quest Q8]** Is the $100K capital stack sufficient to survive a realistic valley of death (12-18 months to profitability, 30% revenue undershoot, seasonal dips) — and what decision framework (covenants, personal guarantees, timeline pressure, opportunity cost of delay) determines whether to take the SBA debt, how much reserve to hold, and at what metrics to shift from growth investment to survival mode?

**Should include (1):**
4. **[Anal Q3 unique]** Revenue mix and seasonality patterns — month-over-month mix across daily vending, catering, events, and expansions that maintains positive cash flow through seasonal dips; which stream provides the most predictable planning foundation.

**Optional:**
5. **[ST Q6 unique]** Fixes-that-Fail pattern in financing choices — the SBA loan provides immediate capital acceleration but fixed monthly payments reduce the ability to weather seasonal fluctuations; what's the minimum viable startup reaching positive cash flow without loan payments, and what advantages does the loan actually buy that creativity/time cannot replicate?
