## Round-Robin Selection Walkthrough

**Important:** Each Append persona is round-robin'd *within its own clusters*. Personas are not interleaved across each other.

**At high effort, A_quota = A_available for every Append persona.** Round-robin selection collapses to "include all questions in cluster order." There is no selection pressure. The walkthrough below is therefore trivial — included for SP1 to verify the *order* and the *cluster attribution*, not the selection.

## Integration Notes

The Append questions are intentionally preserved verbatim (no synthesis compaction). Each preserves its distinctive framing:

- **Provocateur** preserves the "absurd inversion + real insight" format. Q1's "banned from any licensed profession" and Q3's "law school as camouflage" land the provocations that any synthesis step would domesticate.
- **Storyteller** preserves the narrative vignettes with specific sensory detail. Q2's "ICU sounds, patient's face, conversation" and Q3's "2 a.m., rice and beans" are concrete vignettes that synthesis into abstract questions would destroy.
- **Visionary** preserves the paradigm-challenging reframings. Q1's "eliminate boundary between healthcare and legal advocacy entirely" and Q2's "entirely new field of practice" propose reimaginations that synthesis would flatten into strategic-positioning language.
- **Constraint Flipper** preserves the explicit inversion operation. Q1's "3 years as differentiator rather than gap" and Q5's "burnout as a sustainability advantage" each flip a constraint; merging with Synthesize questions on the same topics would collapse the inversion.
- **Connector** preserves the cross-domain + isomorphism + recombination moves. Q4's "translator's advantage," Q6's "VC investment-round principle applied to career transition," and Q8's "ecological mid-succession species" are Connector's three-mode signature; synthesizing them with Analyst or Systems Thinker questions would strip the bridging move that is the Connector's distinctive value.

**Quality check for SP1:** After synthesis, each Append question should be copy-identifiable as its persona's voice. If SP1 output contains Append questions whose framing has been softened, paraphrased, or collapsed into Synthesize-style analytical questions, the Append integrity has failed.
