## C1 Comparison (Persona-Level Clustering vs. Cross-Persona Clustering)

**Per-persona clustering** (the pre-synthesis state after Phase 2B):
- Each of the 10 Synthesize personas organizes their 10-20 questions into 3-5 internal clusters
- Analyst: ~5 clusters (financial viability, UVP, decision criteria, structural realities, sustainability)
- Appreciative Inquirer: ~4 phases (Discovery, Dream, Design, Destiny)
- Audience Advocate: ~5 clusters (community experience, power/access, authentic partnership, accessibility, sustainability)
- Devil's Advocate: ~4 clusters (financial risk, motivation/burnout challenge, skill-transfer skepticism, Plan B)
- Questioner: ~6 clusters (escape/calling, skill transfer, financial reality, burnout transfer, non-JD paths, values)
- Systems Thinker: ~4 clusters (identity/competence loops, burnout/shift-the-burden, leverage points, reversibility)
- Empath: ~3 clusters (motivation/body, identity/meaning, trauma/witnessing)
- First Principles Thinker: ~3 clusters (fundamental motivation, hybrid/alternative, value maximization)
- Futurist: ~3 clusters (market trends, medical-legal intersection, cross-sector mobility)
- Accountant: ~3 clusters (viability/runway, hybrid credential paths, risk-adjusted returns)
- Total per-persona clusters: ~40

**Cross-persona clustering** (this B1 baseline):
- 12 thematic clusters drawn across all 137 Synthesize questions
- Compaction at cluster level: ~40 per-persona → 12 cross-persona = 3.3x meta-compaction

**C1 recommendation:** Cross-persona clustering is the correct level for Phase 2C synthesis. The per-persona view is valuable at Phase 2B (each persona organizes its own output coherently) but the synthesis step operates across personas, so cluster identification must too. The 12 cross-persona clusters here map cleanly to the underlying decision architecture of the topic (financial, motivational, skill-transfer, burnout, hybrid, testing, identity, community, market, systems, niche, values) rather than to persona voices. Per-persona cluster headings (e.g., "AI's Discovery phase" or "Analyst's decision-criteria ranking") would fragment the output and fail to surface the dominant cross-persona convergences (e.g., Cluster 2 motivation-diagnosis merges 3+ persona voices asking the same underlying question). C1 outcome: Phase 2C synthesis prompt should explicitly instruct cross-persona clustering.
