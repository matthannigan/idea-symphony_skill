# career-change — high — Convergence Baseline

**Input file:** test-data/career-change_high.md
**Total questions in input:** 172
**Synthesize-group questions:** 137 (this analysis operates on Synthesize universe only)
**Convergence groups (P):** 22

**Convergence density:** 62.8%
**Unique (non-convergent) Synthesize questions (U):** 51 of 137 = 37.2%

**Convention:** A convergence group = 2+ questions from *different personas* addressing the same underlying concern. Within-persona thematic repetition is not counted. The Append-group personas (Provocateur, Storyteller, Visionary, Constraint Flipper, Connector) are excluded from convergence counting because their value is framing, not topic-convergence — they are appended distinctively per D1.

**Convergence density 62.8% is modestly above other high-effort cells** (property-management/high 60.9%, youth-mentorship/high 59.3%, space-party/high 58.9%, mobile-app/high 57.8%, tool-library/high ~55%). Career-change's slightly elevated density reflects the topic's high thematic concentration — a single individual's career decision has tight dimensional overlap between financial, motivational, skill-transfer, burnout, and values questions, because every persona ultimately frames the same underlying decision from their native register. Four personas (Empath, FPT, DA, Questioner) independently ask variants of "push or pull / calling or escape" — the highest convergence multiplicity observed in any single group across the 10-topic test set.
