Synthesis cannot produce this inversion; only Append preserves it. Same pattern holds for CF Q1 (restricted hours → niche) vs CG-1 (ordinance impact modeling).

4. **Visionary Q4 (never-become-chain) overlaps FPT Q3 (parallel-options) but is not redundant.** FPT Q3 asks "why treat truck → fleet → B&M as progression rather than parallel options" — a reductive-analytical question. Visionary Q4 asks "design from ground up to NEVER become a traditional restaurant chain even while achieving goals — networked federation, guild-style collective, platform business" — an aspirational reimagining with specific alternative forms. Both should appear: FPT Q3 in Cluster 12 (Synthesize), Visionary Q4 in Append. SP1 should check this distinction.

Both appear; the Analogist is Append per D1, ST is Synthesize per D1. SP1 variants that attempt to merge them into a single question fail SQ4 by dropping Analogist.

SP1 may find Analogist Q4 (craft brewery) convergent with Cluster 3 (B&M growth) or Analogist Q5 (food cart pods) convergent with Cluster 8 (ecosystem) — keep appended per D1.

7. **Provocateur Q3 (self-destruct as success goal) is genuinely weird and possibly disruptive to Phase 3.** The question argues explicit goal is forced closure within 18 months because "runaway success is actually failure." SP1 variants might categorize this as a quality-control problem rather than a valid provocation.
