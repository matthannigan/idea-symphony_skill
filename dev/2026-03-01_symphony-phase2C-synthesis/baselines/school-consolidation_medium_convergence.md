## Convergence Group 1 — Break-Even Analysis & Transition Costs

**Theme:** True cost of consolidation — one-time transition expenses (legal, systems integration, staff severance, contract reconciliation, facility closure) vs the $1.2M projected annual admin savings, and the years-to-recover calculation.
**Cluster:** B1 Cluster 1 (Financial Analysis, Break-Even & Transition Costs)
**Questions:**
- [Analyst Q1] What is the true break-even analysis for consolidation? — one-time transition costs vs years to recover.
- [Devil's Advocate Q1] Transition costs vs. long-term savings — one-time and first-year costs that might consume years of savings; map transition expenses, calculate true break-even.
- [Questioner Q5] What if the projected cost savings are optimistic or temporary? — one-time transition expenses, salary harmonization above the unified schedule.

**Merge recommendation:** **Merge all three** into:
> "What is the true break-even analysis for consolidation — mapping all one-time transition expenses (legal fees, systems integration, staff severance, contract buyouts, facility closure costs, referendum campaigns) against the projected $1.2M annual administrative savings, and calculating how many years of operations are needed to recover those costs before any net savings accrue — and what happens if salary harmonization pushes veteran teachers above the unified schedule faster than projected?"

Three-persona convergence on the foundational fiscal-viability question. Merged question captures analytical framing (Analyst), adversarial break-even concern (DA), and harmonization-surprise angle (Questioner).

## Convergence Group 2 — State Funding Reliability & Policy Risk

Clean two-question convergence; merged question covers the verification/durability concern (DA) and the conditional-strings concern (Questioner).

## Convergence Density Interpretation

- **53.0% convergence density** — slightly above the tool-library/medium and space-party/medium 50.4% norm.
