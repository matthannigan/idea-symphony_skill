# tool-library — high — Convergence Baseline

**Universe:** 132 Synthesize-group questions (Append-group convergence is not in scope per D1 — Append voices are preserved, not merged).
**Convergence groups:** 20
**Questions in convergence:** 82 of 132 (~62%)
**Convergence density:** 62%
**Unique (non-convergent) questions:** 50

**Convention:** A convergence group contains 2 or more questions from *different personas* addressing the same underlying concern. Within-persona repetition (e.g., Questioner asking adjacent questions twice) is not "convergence" and is handled via per-persona deduplication, not merge.

Each group is anchored to a B1 cluster. Merge recommendations are either:
- **Merge:** a single synthesized question that captures the shared concern; merge text is anchored in the source questions and persona attribution is preserved in a "drawn from" tag.
- **Preserve all distinct:** voices differ in framing or angle enough that a merge would lose meaningful content.

## Convergence Group 1 — Access Barriers Masquerading as Safeguards

**Theme:** Policies intended to prevent abuse (deposits, ID, residency verification, membership fees) excluding the most vulnerable.
**Cluster:** 1 (Access, Equity, Barrier Design)
**Questions:**
- [Devil's Advocate Q7] Access barriers masquerading as safeguards — deposits/ID/residency excluding undocumented, unhoused, cash-poor.
- [Audience Advocate Q2] Invisible hurdles — requirements assuming literacy, credit cards, smartphones, documentation.
- [Questioner Q10] Balancing safety with accessibility — requirements that reduce risk while creating barriers.
- [Empath Q4] Emotional messages of trust/distrust through policies; who feels surveilled or unwelcome.
- [Accountant Q11] Deposit structures — hold on credit card, sliding scale, community vouchering, deposit-free for qualifying members.

**Merge recommendation: Merge.** Five-way convergence on the same tradeoff — and the alternative designs proposed (vouchering, sliding scale, trust-based with accountability) are compatible.

**Merge text:** *Which access-control policies (deposits, ID/residency verification, membership fees, safety training requirements) inevitably exclude the community members the library most aims to serve — and what alternative designs (sliding-scale and deposit-free qualifying pathways, community vouchering, trust-based systems with accountability mechanisms, hold-on-card rather than cash deposits) protect the asset base without signaling distrust or erecting barriers?* (drawn from Devil's Advocate, Audience Advocate, Questioner, Empath, Accountant)

## Convergence Density Notes for B3

- **~62% convergence is high** — slightly higher than habit-tracker/high (59%) and expected for a topic with 10 Synthesize personas and tightly-coupled concerns (access / finance / liability / community). Tool-library's concerns cluster densely around a few cross-cutting themes (barriers, sustainability, trust) that multiple personas naturally address.
- **17 mergeable groups and 3 preserve-distinct groups** — preserve-distinct rate is ~15%, lower than habit-tracker/high's ~39%. The lower preserve-distinct rate reflects: (a) Accountant's role as data-filler for Analyst's scaffolding means most Accountant+Analyst convergences are clean merges; (b) the topic's concerns (logistics, finance, liability) are more operational than philosophically-layered, making multi-angle distinctions less common.
- **Preserve-distinct groups are concentrated in Cluster 4 (liability) and Cluster 8 (belonging)** — the cells where equity/dignity framings differ meaningfully from operational/cost framings.
- **Accountant has the highest convergence rate (100% of questions in convergence)** — this is consistent with Accountant's role as an Analyst-complement. SP1 should not misinterpret Accountant's 0 unique-count as a representation problem; Accountant's voice should be preserved *within* mergers by its distinctive quantitative-specificity vocabulary (dollar ranges, percentages, break-even points, depreciation mechanics).
- **Appreciative Inquirer has the highest unique rate (~83%)** and AI's strengths-based framing is the most orthogonal voice in this cell. AI's questions cluster heavily in Cluster 8 (Community Building) and form several orphans in Cluster 9 (Trust) via flat-pool clustering.
- **Variants that merge any of the 3 preserve-distinct groups over-merge.** Variants that fail to merge ~14+ of the 17 mergeable groups under-utilize convergence. Variants that merge fewer than 10 but preserve all 50 unique questions may still be acceptable if the failed merges are on borderline cases (CG-7, CG-12, CG-14, CG-16 are two-way convergences that could defensibly stay unmerged).
