## Convergence Group 1 — Screening Architecture & Background-Check Adequacy

**Theme:** Multi-dimensional screening calibration — background checks alone are insufficient, but adding behavioral interviews, reference interviews, probationary pairing, and renewal cycles raises both false-positive (excluding qualified mentors from affected communities) and operational cost concerns.
**Cluster link:** B1 Cluster 1 (Screening/Mentor Qualification)
**Questions:**
- [Analyst Q2] Volunteer screening calibration — multi-dimension; false-positive/false-negative trade-off without disproportionately excluding qualified mentors from community
- [Devil's Advocate Q1] Background check limitations — reference interviews from multiple contexts, behavioral interview questions probing boundary awareness, probationary pairing periods
- [Lawyer Q2] Background check scope and renewal cycles — state law variation; annual/biennial/event-triggered re-checks

**Merge recommendation:** **Merge all three** into:
> "What multi-dimensional screening architecture — criminal background checks across relevant registries, structured reference interviews from multiple contexts, behavioral interviews probing boundary awareness, and probationary pairing periods — balances thorough vetting with access preservation for qualified mentors from the affected community, and what renewal cycle (annual, biennial, event-triggered) keeps the screening current without disproportionate administrative burden?"

Three questions converging tightly on screening-architecture from three angles: Analyst quantitative trade-off framing, DA structural-supplement framing, Lawyer regulatory-mechanism framing. Merger captures all three while preserving the operational-legal-equity tension.
