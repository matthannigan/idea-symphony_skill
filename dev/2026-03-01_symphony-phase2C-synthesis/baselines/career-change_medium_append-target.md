## Per-Persona Internal Cluster Identification

Inspection of the 4 Append personas' source questions yields the following internal cluster structures. None of the four personas supply explicit `##` cluster headings in the test data, so cluster labels here are inferred from question content and the universal-3-cluster heuristic noted in the persona-selection guide (Provocateur: "universal 3-cluster structure"; Connector: "exactly 3 clusters, zero filler — inherits Analogist's append mechanics cleanly"; Visionary: "universal 3-cluster structure; self-contained questions ideal for append"; Storyteller: "universal 3-cluster structure").

### Provocateur (7 questions → 3 internal clusters)

- **Cluster P-A: Credential / permission inversions** (2 questions) — inverting the premise that professional credentials are required or intended for practice
  - Q1: What if the question isn't "ICU nursing or law" but "why do you assume helping people requires a professional credential"? (banned-from-professions thought experiment)
  - Q2: What if you pursued law school with zero intention of ever practicing law? (JD as reconnaissance, not enlistment; transformation vs. credentialing)
- **Cluster P-B: Hidden-agenda / absurd-simultaneity inversions** (2 questions) — inverting the narrative the career change presents to the world
  - Q3: What if public interest law isn't the destination but the camouflage? (respectable cover story for something more radical)
  - Q4: What if you did both — simultaneously, badly, on purpose? (friction of living between worlds as generative)
- **Cluster P-C: Diagnostic / agency inversions on identity and signal** (3 questions) — inverting the frame that burnout and career-change are personal/intentional
  - Q5: What if you treated your ICU nursing experience not as background but as a weapon? (data that lawyers don't know exists; stop fitting expertise into legal containers)
  - Q6: What if the burnout you're experiencing isn't a problem to solve but information about the system? (burnout as structural probe; read it before choosing your next machine)
  - Q7: What if you're not escaping nursing but nursing is escaping you? (careers as agents; redirected by the field, not abandoning it)
