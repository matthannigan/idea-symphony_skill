## Scoring Convention

Each baseline-expected output question is weighted equally at 1.0 for preservation scoring. A synthesized output question counts as "preserving" a baseline-expected question when either:

- It reproduces the baseline question verbatim or with only stylistic edits (≥80% content overlap by anchor-phrase match), OR
- It is a merge-anchored question that explicitly addresses the convergence-group theme identified in B2 (e.g., CV-01 "LRAP/PSLF/IDR mechanics with dependency-loop risk"), with preservation credit split evenly across the convergence group's members.

**Merge credit:** Each convergence group (CV-01 through CV-13) is worth 1.0 preservation point; the merged output question receives full credit when it captures all distinct sub-angles flagged in the B2 merge recommendation. Partial credit (0.5) when only the surface theme is captured and distinct sub-angles are dropped.

**Persona-attribution preservation:** Each baseline-expected question carries persona attribution (a list of 1-4 contributing personas). Synthesis output should retain attribution at the question level (e.g., "via Analyst + Devil's Advocate + Systems Thinker"). Attribution-preservation is scored separately at 1.0/0.0 per question.

**Anchor phrases:** Merge text should be anchored on the specific vocabulary that makes the question identifiable — "Shifting the Burden," "break-even crossover," "golden handcuff," "80% paperwork," "fixed stock / constant flow," "Fixes that Fail." Stripping these anchors in favor of generic phrasing loses persona distinctiveness and should be penalized.
