**Cluster:** C2 (Regulatory Environment & Ordinance Response)

- [Questioner Q1] Anticipatory compliance — how ordinance passage reshapes the model and which alternative revenue streams activate immediately.
- [Devil's Advocate Q4] Ordinance vulnerability blind spot — modeling worst-case ordinance passage as existential threat; pre-developed adaptive pivots.
