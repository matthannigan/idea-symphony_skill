## Integration Notes

Per D1's implementation description in the persona-selection-guide: "Append selected questions to the Phase 2C output under a persona-labeled section." The final Phase 2C output structure should be:

1. **Synthesized clusters** (42 questions across 12 clusters per B3 Target) — each cluster labeled with its theme.
2. **Append section** (44 questions) — tagged by persona, in a distinct section of the output (or interleaved with clusters if the synthesis prompt design elects interleaving).

**Tagging style:** Each Append question should carry the `[Perspective: persona-name]` tag so the reader can distinguish Append-group distinctive voice from synthesized Q&A. D1 is silent on interleaved-vs-segregated presentation; the default assumed here is a **segregated Append section at the end** (per the persona-selection guide's "directly appended" language), with clear persona-labels. SP1 may test variants of this integration.

**Cross-cluster tagging (optional):** Append questions can be secondarily tagged with the B1 thematic cluster they most closely relate to (column 2 in the table above), so synthesized clusters have a "see also" pointer to relevant Append content.

**Provocateur treatment:** Provocateur questions inherently don't map cleanly to B1 thematic clusters. They are tagged as `[Perspective: Provocateur]` with no thematic-cluster cross-reference; the provocation itself is the payload. This is consistent with the guide's note that "Absurd inversion + 'real insight' format is the entire value. Synthesizing strips the provocation."

**Constraint Flipper placement:** CF's 7 questions map to multiple clusters with the heaviest concentration in Cluster 8 (Economics) + Cluster 12 (Reimagination) cross-mapping. CF-A regulatory-inversions map differently than on property-management — here the "regulatory" constraints are about volunteer-pool structure, budget, and faith-based context rather than permit-and-zoning. Interleaved presentation for this cell would distribute CF across 5-6 clusters.

**Storyteller placement:** Storyteller's 6 questions span Clusters 2 (trauma disclosure), 5 (climate/belonging), 6 (first meeting), 7 (mentor training), 10 (program narrative), 11 (graduation/closure) — 6 different clusters. In segregated-Append presentation, Storyteller reads as a coherent "narrative collection." In interleaved presentation, Storyteller questions scatter across 6 clusters.

**Visionary placement:** Visionary's 7 questions cluster heavily in Cluster 12 (Reimagination) — 6 of 7 are pure reimagination questions. This is the tightest Visionary-to-Cluster mapping observed (even tighter than property-management/high's 6/8). In segregated presentation, Visionary reads as a coherent "what if we reimagined this entire model?" section. In interleaved presentation, Visionary's thread is highly concentrated near Cluster 12.

**Connector placement:** Connector's 7 questions operate in structural-isomorphism and recombination modes. Each question bridges a specific cross-domain pattern to a specific mentorship concern: peer-support loop → mentor reciprocity (Cluster 7), matching paradox → matching design (Cluster 4), safety-trust wilderness analogy → safety climate (Cluster 5), third-place architecture → meeting spaces (Cluster 5/10), intervention decay → duration/scaffolding (Cluster 11), referral feedback loops → ecosystem (Cluster 10), volunteer life-cycle leak → mentor retention (Cluster 7/11). Interleaved presentation produces "what if we analogized this problem structurally to X" moments near each relevant Synthesized cluster. This is distinctive of Connector's isomorphism mode — unlike Analogist's single-domain reframing, Connector crosses sectors.

**Politician placement:** Politician's 10 questions concentrate in Cluster 10 (Ecosystem — 6+ references), Cluster 6 (Family/Consent — 3 references), and Cluster 8 (Economics — 4 references). Politician's political-analytical vocabulary (gatekeepers, coalition sequencing, decision-rights frameworks, veto players, political economy, crisis-reputation dynamics, stakeholder-segmented success metrics) is unique — no Synthesize persona uses this vocabulary. Politician's 10 questions form a coherent "organizational-political architecture" reading when segregated; when interleaved by cluster, they cluster primarily in Cluster 10 with bridges to 6 and 8. Politician is the heaviest Append contribution per persona in the evaluation set so far (10 questions vs. Provocateur/Storyteller at 6-7).

**PC1 implication:** The youth-mentorship/high cell is a good case for testing interleaved vs. segregated integration because:
- Append stream is very large (44 — the largest in the evaluation set), exceeding the Synthesized stream (42).
- Append questions map to clusters with varying levels of fidelity (Politician → Cluster 10 heavy; Visionary → Cluster 12 heavy; Storyteller wide; Connector moderate; CF moderate with concentration in 8+12; Provocateur orphan).
- Six distinct Append-voice registers compete for reader attention: narrative (Storyteller), reimagination (Visionary), constraint-inversion (CF), structural-isomorphism (Connector), political-analytical (Politician), and inversion/orphan (Provocateur).

With 6 Append personas producing 44 questions across 3-6 clusters each, segregated presentation produces 6 coherent persona sections at the end. Interleaved presentation would scatter 44 Append questions across 8-10 of the 12 clusters, producing roughly 3-5 Append questions adjacent to each cluster's 3-5 Synthesized questions. For this cell, interleaved presentation risks visually dominating each cluster with Append content.
