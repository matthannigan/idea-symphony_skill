# tool-library — medium — Convergence Baseline

**Input file:** test-data/tool-library_medium.md
**Total questions in input:** 123
**Convergence groups:** 15
**Questions participating in convergence:** 62 (of 123, 50.4%)
**Convergence density:** 50.4%

**Convention:** A convergence group = 2+ questions from *different personas* addressing the same underlying concern. Within-persona thematic repetition is not counted. The Provocateur's 7 orphan questions are excluded from convergence counting because their value is provocation-framing, not topic-convergence — they are appended distinctively per D1. Politician questions are Append-routed per D1 but several do share thematic territory with Synthesize-group questions; these are noted but **not merged into synthesis** (convergence is documented for cross-reference only — the Politician questions flow intact to Append per D1).

## Convergence Group 1 — Volunteer Burnout & Succession

**Theme:** The 8-10 committed volunteer base is a single point of failure; what sustains operations when the founding energy fades?
**Cluster:** B1 Cluster 8 (Volunteer Sustainability, Governance & Stewardship)
**Questions:**
- [Devil's Advocate Q1] What happens to the tools when the volunteer core inevitably burns out? Succession planning and rotation systems.
- [Questioner Q13] What if volunteer burnout hits year two? Structures sustaining operations when enthusiasm fades.
- [Storyteller Q7] A year in the life of Maria, a core volunteer — moments of joy, moments of logistical/emotional labor, burnout prevention.

**Merge recommendation:** **Merge Devil's Advocate Q1 + Questioner Q13** into:
> "When founding-volunteer enthusiasm fades in year two or three, what succession planning, rotation systems, and volunteer pipeline structures would let operations continue uninterrupted — and what early warning signals (meeting attendance, energy levels, task-drop rate) would indicate burnout is forming before it collapses the core?"

Storyteller Q7 **preserved distinct** — the character-driven year-in-the-life narrative is Storyteller's signature register; merging strips the narrative vignette. Appears in Append per D1.

## Convergence Group 4 — Membership Model vs Access

**Theme:** Membership requirements (ID, proof of address, fees) as a funding mechanism that may exclude those who most need free tool access.
**Cluster:** B1 Cluster 5 (Access, Equity & Participation Barriers) + B1 Cluster 2 (Unit Economics)
**Questions:**
- [Analyst Q12] Membership vs open-access trade-offs — free-with-deposit, tiered fees, sponsorship-based, volunteer-run.
- [Devil's Advocate Q10] What if the people who most need free tool access are exactly those who can't meet membership requirements?
- [Questioner Q8] Why do we assume individual lending is the default model? Group memberships, household accounts, organization-level access.
- [Analogist Q4] Layered trust architecture from car-sharing — deposit structures, telematics, user tiers, insurance pools, peer reviews.

**Merge recommendation:** **Merge Analyst Q12 + Devil's Advocate Q10** into:
> "What membership and access architecture (free-with-deposit, tiered fees, sponsorship, organization-level accounts) balances the sustainability need for accountability with the equity imperative of reaching members who lack standard documentation, stable housing, or credit history — so the people most in need of free tools aren't screened out by the very requirements designed to protect the library?"

Questioner Q8 **preserved distinct** — the "why individual lending at all?" reframing challenges a more fundamental assumption than membership-terms debate; the group-membership idea is a structural alternative, not a membership-fee variant.

Analogist Q4 **preserved distinct (Append)** — its value is the car-sharing import; synthesis would strip the metaphor. Routes to Append per D1.

## Convergence Density Interpretation

- **50.4% convergence density** — roughly half the questions participate in cross-persona convergence. This matches the space-party medium baseline (also 50.4%) almost exactly, suggesting this may be a stable convergence-density norm for medium-effort cells with 6 Synthesize-group personas.
- **Politician's Append routing preserves its content while not contributing to synthesis mergers.** Groups 2, 6, 14, 15 each have a Politician question whose topic overlaps with the group but whose voice (40-80 word verbose governance questions) routes to Append. This is D1-expected.
- **Highest-convergence clusters:** Cluster 5 (Access/Equity — 5 personas converge), Cluster 9 (Commons/Trust — 2 personas with 4 questions), Cluster 8 (Governance — Politician + Devil's Advocate + Questioner + AI converge on volunteer sustainability and governance).
- **Lowest-convergence cluster:** Cluster 7 (Community/Connection) — mostly Appreciative Inquirer uniques (5 of 11), with Audience Advocate reciprocity-framing partially distinct. AI's strengths-based contributions largely resist convergence by design.
- **Append-group uniqueness:** Visionary's 8 are 100% unique; Storyteller's 8 are ~75% unique (2 have Group-11 topic overlap); Analogist's 8 are ~87% unique (one has Group-4 topic overlap); Provocateur's 7 are all orphans. This supports D1's Append treatment — these voices would be stripped by synthesis.
- **Politician (Append-routed, Tier 3):** 3 of 10 are fully unique (Q3, Q5, Q6 — distinctly governance/power-sequencing questions). The other 7 have topical convergence with Synthesize-group but are preserved intact in Append per D1. This is D1-expected and exactly why Politician is Append rather than Synthesize.
