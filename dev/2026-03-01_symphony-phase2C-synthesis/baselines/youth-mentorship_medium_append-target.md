## Per-Persona Internal Cluster Identification

### Provocateur (7 questions → 3 internal clusters)

- **Cluster P-A: Relationship/role inversion** (3 questions) — inverting who is helper and who is helped
  - Q1: What if teens mentored adults instead of the reverse? (teens as sources of knowledge, not recipients)
  - Q4: What if we designed for maximum danger instead of maximum safety? (shared-risk growth vs. cocooned protection)
  - Q7: What if we matched pairs based on maximum incompatibility? (mirrors vs. windows into unfamiliar worlds)
- **Cluster P-B: System/protocol inversion** (2 questions) — inverting screening, reporting, and institutional architecture

- **Cluster P-C: Success/structure inversion** (2 questions) — inverting outcomes and commitment architecture
  - Q3: What if the program succeeded perfectly—and then we shut it down? (building capacity vs. permanent dependency)
  - Q6: What if mentorship lasted exactly one day instead of one year? (depth vs. breadth; what sustained relationships assume)

Cluster sizes: P-A=3, P-B=2, P-C=2. Largest cluster: P-A (3). Tied-for-smallest: P-B and P-C (2 each); source-order tiebreak for refill priority → P-B before P-C.

**Tagging style:** Each Append question carries the `[Perspective: persona-name]` tag so the reader can distinguish Append-group distinctive voice from synthesized Q&A. SP1 may test variants of this integration.
