## 4. C1 Recommendation (Per-Cell)

**Recommendation for this cell: flat pooling with persona tags preserved as metadata** (full rationale in B1 clusters file).

**Does per-persona vs. flat matter for this cell?** Yes, very substantially. With 10 Synthesize personas, per-persona grouping creates 10 separate thematic maps the synthesizer must mentally align — a quadratic cross-referencing task. Flat pooling surfaces the actual cross-persona convergences:
- Cluster 4 (Matching) draws 8 personas
- Cluster 5 (Safety/Climate) draws 5+ personas with Empath at 38% of cluster (6/16) — per-persona view would treat this as "Empath's section"
- Cluster 6 (Family/Consent) draws 5 personas with AA at 38% of cluster (5/13) — per-persona view fragments the family-trust theme
- Cluster 10 (Ecosystem) draws 7 personas with even distribution — per-persona view fragments into organizational/trend/systems sub-discussions
- Cluster 11 (Relationship Arc) has 4 distinct-register treatments (Empath emotional-interior, AA process-design, DA overcommitment, ST structural) that per-persona view obscures

Cross-persona bridges that flat-pooling surfaces but per-persona view hides:
- Cluster 2 (Mandatory Reporting): Lawyer regulatory + DA operational failure + Questioner paradigm all converge on the trust-vs-legal-obligation tension
- Cluster 6 (Family/Consent): AA equity + Lawyer regulatory + DA operational + Empath felt-context all converge on family trust
- Cluster 9 (Measurement): Analyst framework + AA participant-burden + DA relationship-undermining + AI strengths-based + FPT helping-vs-harming + ST self-fulfilling-prophecies + Futurist surveillance-trajectory — 7 registers on shared multi-stakeholder measurement concerns

**Confidence:** **very high** (matching property-management/high's confidence). At 10 Synthesize personas with 59.3% convergence density and 27% orphan/bridging rate, per-persona clustering actively obscures the primary thematic structure.

**Effort-level pattern (cross-effort prediction):**
- Low (6 Synthesize personas): per-persona view may work as convergences are less dense
- Medium (7-8 Synthesize personas — Tier 1 + 0-1 Tier 3): flat pooling preferred, moderate-high confidence
- High (9-10 Synthesize personas): flat pooling essential, very high confidence

Youth-mentorship/high at 10 Synthesize personas sits just below property-management/high's 11. Per-persona view becomes increasingly costly as persona count grows, and youth-mentorship/high is in the "essential-to-flat-pool" zone.

**Caveats:**
- Cluster 12 (Reimagination) is FPT+Futurist 10/10 = 100% — genuine 2-persona cluster. Flat pooling doesn't mislead but also doesn't substantially help (there's nothing to cross-reference across other personas).
- Cluster 2 (Mandatory Reporting) is small (8 questions) but 4-persona-convergent — per-persona view would fragment its tight thematic convergence.
- Cluster 3 (Liability/Boundaries) is Lawyer-dominant (3 of 7) — per-persona view would treat as "Lawyer section" when the cross-persona convergence (DA structural + Analyst activity-tiering) is real.
