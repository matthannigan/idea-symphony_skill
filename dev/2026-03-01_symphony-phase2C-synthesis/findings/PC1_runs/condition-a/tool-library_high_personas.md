# tool-library — high — Brainstorming Persona Recommendations (Condition A)

## Topic Cluster Identification

Scanning the raw questions, four distinct topic clusters emerge in the tool library material:

1. **Operations & Logistics** — inventory tracking across 50-100 tools, maintenance burden, 500 sq ft space utilization, volunteer capacity with 8-10 core volunteers, replacement cycles, checkout workflows
2. **Community Engagement & Access** — walk-up experience, equity across renters/homeowners/undocumented residents, language/cultural barriers, trust, belonging, first-time-borrower dignity
3. **Financial Sustainability** — membership fee structures, grant funding during ramp-up, replacement reserves at 20-25% annually, insurance premiums, 3-8% loss rates, deposit economics
4. **Governance & Political Structure** — board composition, informal power mapping, landlord tenure security for donated space, coalition-building, liability policy tensions, opposition from hardware stores

High effort = 7 personas per cluster. Core (DA + Pragmatist) repeats across all four clusters.

## Selection Summary

- **Total personas (persona-cluster assignments):** 28 across 4 clusters (7 each).
- **Unique personas invoked:** 15 — Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Technical Expert, Simplifier, Systems Thinker, Constraint Flipper, Analogist, Audience Advocate, Empath, Storyteller, Provocateur, Politician, Accountant, First Principles Thinker, Futurist, Lawyer, Visionary. (Core pair repeats across all 4 clusters.)
- **Circle distribution (per-assignment):** Inner 8 (including core pair × 4 = 8 core + 8 inner-ring-specific = 16 combined core/inner), Middle 9, Outer 3. Adjusting to guide's categories: Core 8 (DA+Pragmatist × 4), Inner 8, Middle 9, Outer 3.
- **Dominant topic drivers:**
  - **Human-centered content is heavy** — three personas from that triad (AA, Empath, Storyteller) all fit the engagement cluster because raw data explicitly centers dignity, named-character journeys, and stakeholder segmentation.
  - **Genuine outer-ring applicability** — Accountant (financial modeling with specific percentages and premiums), Politician (explicit coalition and opposition mapping), and Lawyer (injury liability, tenure risk, dissolution asset questions) each have concrete topic hooks rather than trigger-word matches.
  - **Cross-domain reasoning is latent** — Analogist pulls in naturally because raw data already draws on library circulation, car-sharing (Zipcar/Turo), seed libraries, guilds, and open-source maintenance.
  - **Challenge Triad repeatedly productive** — DA (core) + Provocateur + Constraint Flipper appear across engagement and operations clusters because the material has both entrenched assumptions (surveillance, ownership) and constraint-reframing opportunities (500 sq ft, volunteer dependence, budget).
