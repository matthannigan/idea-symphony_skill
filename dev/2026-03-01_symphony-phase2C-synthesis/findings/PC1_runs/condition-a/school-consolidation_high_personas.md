# school-consolidation — high — Brainstorming Persona Recommendations (Condition A)

The raw question set spans four distinguishable topic clusters that emerge from the Phase 2 questions:

1. **Fiscal & Financial Structure** — break-even analysis, $1.2M administrative savings, $4.2M deferred maintenance, bond obligations across districts, property tax equalization, union contract reconciliation costs, per-pupil funding at 15% further enrollment decline.
2. **Governance, Coalition-Building & Voter Approval** — unanimous approval required across three districts, unified board representation formulas, stakeholder coalition sequencing, union leverage dynamics, "local control" backlash.
3. **Community Identity, Student Experience & Human Impact** — "our school" as town identity, 45-minute bus rides, belonging in merged buildings, grief of closure, special education continuity, working families' childcare, invisible losses.
4. **Strategic Alternatives & Long-Horizon Restructuring** — alternative models (shared services, confederation, "lead district"), 50-year horizon, remote-work migration patterns, demographic headwinds beyond 2035, adaptive reuse of buildings, phased/pilot consolidation.

Per the guide, high effort = 7 personas per cluster = Core (2) + Inner Ring topic-matched (2) + Middle Ring cluster completers (2-3), with outer ring added where genuinely relevant. Four clusters × 7 = 28 persona-slots. Outer ring specialists (Accountant, Politician, Storyteller) are genuinely warranted here — the topic has real financial-planning, multi-stakeholder-governance, and community-narrative dimensions. Lawyer is not included — though the topic has contract/regulatory touchpoints, they are secondary to the core governance/fiscal/identity tensions; the guide warns against over-applying Lawyer outside genuinely regulated domains.

## Selection Summary

- **Total persona-slots:** 28 (4 clusters × 7 personas per cluster)
- **Unique personas used:** 14 — Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Accountant, First Principles Thinker, Systems Thinker, Futurist, Constraint Flipper, Politician, Audience Advocate, Provocateur, Empath, Storyteller, Connector, Visionary, Analogist (plus Empath counted once)
- **Circle distribution (per-slot):** Inner 6 · Middle 13 · Outer 3 · Core 8 (Core personas DA + Pragmatist repeat across all 4 clusters as required)
- **Outer ring used:** Accountant (Fiscal), Politician (Governance), Storyteller (Community) — all passing the "genuinely relevant" test per guide anti-patterns. Lawyer deliberately excluded: contract/regulatory touchpoints exist (bond obligations, union contracts, IEP compliance) but are secondary to the dominant fiscal/governance/identity tensions, and the guide cautions against reflexive Lawyer inclusion.
- **Clusters completed:**
  - Challenge Triad (DA + Provocateur + Constraint Flipper) — completed across Governance and Fiscal clusters given entrenched "we've always done it this way" energy around district boundaries.
  - Human-Centered Triad (Empath + Audience Advocate + Storyteller) — completed on the Community cluster, the strongest human-centered fit.
  - External Perspectives Triad (Systems Thinker + Connector + Analogist) — completed on the Strategic Alternatives cluster because the topic is genuinely novel/multi-domain (the guide's explicit Analogist trigger).
  - Forward-Looking Pair (Visionary + Futurist) — completed on the Strategic Alternatives cluster per high-effort guidance.
- **Dominant topic drivers:**
  1. Fiscal sustainability under demographic decline (drives Accountant, FPT, Futurist, Systems Thinker selections)
  2. Three-way coalition veto dynamics and unanimous voter-approval requirement (drives Politician, Audience Advocate, Provocateur)
  3. "Our school is our town" identity grief and service-continuity for vulnerable students (drives Empath, Storyteller, Audience Advocate, Connector)
  4. Irreversibility of consolidation and whether alternative restructuring models exist (drives Visionary, Futurist, Analogist, Constraint Flipper)
- **Exclusions:** Lawyer (regulatory dimensions secondary, not dominant), Technical Expert (no technical-product dimension beyond routine systems integration), Simplifier (topic is about restructuring necessity, not feature creep), Momentum Builder (implementation sequencing is covered by Politician + Pragmatist without forcing MB onto a cluster where it would duplicate).
