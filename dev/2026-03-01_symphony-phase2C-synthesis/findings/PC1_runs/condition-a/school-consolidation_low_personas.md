## Shadow Selection (if escalated to medium effort — 4 personas per cluster)

The test content decomposes into four topic clusters. Under medium-effort rules (Core 2 + Inner Ring 2), the rubric would select:

### Cluster 1: Financial & Fiscal Analysis

| Persona | Circle | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Always included. Directly relevant: the $1.2M administrative savings claim, the 12% per-pupil state funding increase, and the 5-year break-even projections all need adversarial pressure-testing — the test content itself already surfaces concerns like "state funding trap" and "transition costs vs. long-term savings." |
| Pragmatist | Core | Always included. The smallest district's 2-year fund balance creates a hard feasibility clock; Pragmatist asks what minimum viable consolidation looks like given that constraint. |
| Accountant | Outer (topic-gated, warranted) | Financial/Business topic affinity. Genuinely relevant: $28M combined operating budget, $4.2M deferred maintenance, salary harmonization across three union contracts, per-student cost curves across instruction/administration/transportation/facilities. The scale is institutional, not personal — no anti-pattern risk. |
| Systems Thinker | Middle | Good Fit for Financial/Business on a multi-district system. Cost-per-student curves, economies of scale vs. fixed costs, and "where might a larger district actually introduce new cost inefficiencies" are explicit system-dynamics questions in the source content. |

### Cluster 2: Community Impact & Stakeholder Experience

| Persona | Circle | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Always included. Needed to pressure-test optimistic framings of "community benefit" against the bused student with the 60+ minute commute and the town that loses its school building. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Always included. Grounds community mitigation ideas (advisory councils, community hubs, phased facility closures) in what can actually be staffed and funded. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner (User-Facing/Community: Strong Fit) | Test content is saturated with stakeholder-segmentation questions: single parents like Emma without cars, eighth-grader Javier who has changed schools three times, students with disabilities facing longer commutes, grandparents who walked to school plays. The persona's job — mapping differentiated stakeholder needs — is exactly the analytical work the raw questions demand. |
| Empath | Inner (User-Facing/Community: Strong Fit) | The source content repeatedly frames consolidation as grief, identity disruption, and "the school IS our town." Empath handles the emotional-resonance layer that the Audience Advocate's segmentation work does not fully cover (complete Human-Centered pair without requiring the full triad at medium effort). |

### Cluster 3: Governance, Political Process & Voter Approval

| Persona | Circle | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Always included. The "voter approval paradox" (all three districts must approve simultaneously with asymmetric incentives), union contract reconciliation landmines, and the "coordination trap" all demand explicit critical analysis. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Always included. Needed for realistic referendum mechanics, phase-in guarantees, and which mitigations can actually be executed before the vote. |
| Politician | Outer (topic-gated, warranted) | Governance/Political: Strong Fit. Genuinely relevant — not an anti-pattern here. The test content explicitly raises weighted board representation, geographic voting requirements, rotating seats, supermajority thresholds, coalition building across three districts, and teacher union dynamics. This is textbook multi-stakeholder governance, not a technical topic where Politician over-applies. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner (Governance/Political: Strong Fit) | Voter approval requires mapping distinct constituencies — fiscal conservatives, teachers' unions, parents, state officials, the town losing its school vs. the town keeping it — which the source content (Devil's Advocate Q5, Analyst Q4) names directly. |

### Cluster 4: Alternatives & Strategic Reinvention

| Persona | Circle | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Always included. Needed to test whether "shared services only" or "hybrid/federated" models deliver claimed 60-70% of savings with 20% of controversy (Devil's Advocate Q11 in source) — or whether that's wishful thinking. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Always included. Source content raises intermediate options (shared superintendent, joint purchasing, consolidated special ed, regional high school partnerships); Pragmatist identifies the minimum-viable version that could be executed before a full referendum. |
| Visionary | Inner (Strategic/Vision: Strong Fit) | The test content includes explicit paradigm-shift questions: distributed learning cooperatives, rural education as an R&D lab, eliminating school districts as the unit of analysis, designing for contraction rather than growth.
