# school-consolidation — medium — Brainstorming Persona Recommendations (Condition A)

The REQUEST describes a three-district rural school consolidation feasibility study. Questions cluster into four topic areas that each warrant their own medium-effort persona set (4 personas per cluster: Core + 2 Inner Ring topic-matched).

Topic clusters identified from the REQUEST content:
1. **Financial Viability & Break-Even** — $1.2M administrative savings, $4.2M deferred maintenance, $8M outstanding bonds in District A, 12% per-pupil funding increase, tax base equalization, transition costs
2. **Governance & Political Feasibility** — voter approval required in all three districts, unified board representation, three union contract reconciliation, coalition building across towns
3. **Community & Student Experience** — town identity loss when schools close, 15-min to hour-plus bus rides, belonging across merging student bodies, special needs/IEP continuity, working family logistics
4. **Strategic Alternatives & System Dynamics** — shared services vs. full consolidation, 22% enrollment decline as symptom vs. problem, 2035 trajectory, post-consolidation feedback loops

## Recommended Personas

| Persona | Circle | Topic Cluster | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Financial Viability & Break-Even | Core persona. Warranted here: the REQUEST's financial case rests on contested assumptions ($1.2M savings vs. unquantified transition costs, 12% funding increase that may not materialize, $4.2M deferred maintenance that consolidation doesn't fund). DA stress-tests these before commitment. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Financial Viability & Break-Even | Core persona. Warranted here: asks what the minimum-viable financial arrangement looks like given three districts with wildly different balance sheets (District A's $8M in bonds vs. debt-free District B; smallest district's 2-year fund balance). |
| Accountant | Outer (topic-gated) | Financial Viability & Break-Even | The REQUEST is explicitly a fiscal-crisis-driven consolidation with concrete dollar figures across bonds, deferred maintenance, tax base equalization, union salary harmonization, and state funding formulas. This is not personal-scale — it is meaningful multi-million-dollar public finance. Accountant's anti-pattern (over-financializing small purchases) does not apply. |
| Systems Thinker | Inner | Financial Viability & Break-Even | The financial question is entangled with enrollment decline dynamics: administrative savings eroded by transportation costs, bond pooling across unequal tax bases, union contract reconciliation creating ongoing cost pressure. ST maps how fiscal flows interact rather than treating $1.2M savings as a static number. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Governance & Political Feasibility | Core. Warranted: "voter approval required in all three districts" means any one district vetoes the whole proposal — DA identifies the irreducible political failure modes (fiscal-conservative vs. town-identity coalitions in each of the three districts). |
| Pragmatist | Core | Governance & Political Feasibility | Core. Warranted: asks what the minimum-viable governance structure is that can actually pass three separate votes, not the theoretically ideal board composition. |
| Politician | Outer (topic-gated) | Governance & Political Feasibility | The REQUEST has unambiguous multi-stakeholder governance: three separate voter approvals, three teachers' unions with different salary schedules/seniority rules, unified board composition balancing town populations, facility closure decisions that trigger community vetoes. This is public-policy coalition building at its most literal — Politician's "Include When" criteria are fully met. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner | Governance & Political Feasibility | Governance topic with stakeholder segmentation: fiscal conservatives, parents, teachers' unions, town-identity voters, state officials. AA maps what each segment needs to say yes, distinct from Politician's coalition-sequencing lens. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Community & Student Experience | Core. Warranted: challenges assumptions that consolidation will "attract qualified teachers" or "improve educational outcomes" — both explicitly made by the proposal but contested by the research on small schools. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Community & Student Experience | Core. Warranted: grounds community-impact discussion in what's actually implementable — e.g., which transition supports (before/after care, translation services, IEP continuity) are minimum viable for day-one operation. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner | Community & Student Experience | "User-Facing/Community" is a Strong Fit. The REQUEST surfaces many distinct stakeholder segments with divergent needs: students facing 1.5-2 hours of added daily commute, single parents who relied on walking-distance schools, IEP families losing continuity with specialized staff, non-English-speaking parents losing cultural liaison staff, rural families without reliable transportation. AA systematically maps these. |
| Empath | Inner | Community & Student Experience | Strong Fit for user-facing/community. The REQUEST is saturated with emotional stakes that rarely surface in financial feasibility studies: driving past an empty school building where your grandmother attended, being "those kids from [other town]" in the merged cafeteria, losing teachers who knew your family history. Empath accesses what people actually feel, complementing AA's structural mapping. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Strategic Alternatives & System Dynamics | Core. Warranted: the REQUEST frames consolidation as inevitable, but several questions surface that consolidation may be a symptom-treater rather than a structural fix if enrollment continues its 22% decline trajectory through 2035. DA pressure-tests the "consolidation is the answer" framing. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Strategic Alternatives & System Dynamics | Core. Warranted: asks whether a "soft consolidation" (shared superintendent, joint purchasing, combined special ed) captures 60-70% of savings with 20% of the political lift — the minimum-viable strategic move. |
| Systems Thinker | Inner | Strategic Alternatives & System Dynamics | Strong Fit for strategic/vision topics with complex dynamics. The REQUEST's questions about self-reinforcing loops (school closure → property values fall → tax base erodes → more closures), delay effects (financial benefits immediate, social costs delayed 5-10 years), and shifting-the-burden (consolidation masks enrollment decline as root cause) are explicit systems-thinking territory. |
| Analogist | Inner | Strategic Alternatives & System Dynamics | The REQUEST is a genuinely novel/ambiguous problem space — rural school consolidation doesn't map cleanly to one established playbook. The existing Connector material in the raw data already draws parallels to hospital mergers (federated branding with centralized back-office), watershed districts (local representation + resource efficiency), Scandinavian community hubs (co-located services), and rural healthcare's traveling-specialist model. Analogist imports concrete solutions from these adjacent domains. |

## Selection Summary

- Total personas: 16 assignments across 4 clusters (4 unique core/inner personas: DA, Pragmatist, AA, Empath, Systems Thinker, Analogist; 2 outer-ring: Accountant, Politician)
- Circle distribution: Inner 8 (AA x2, Empath, Systems Thinker x2, Analogist), Core 8 (DA x4, Pragmatist x4), Outer 2 (Accountant, Politician)
- Composition per cluster: Core (2) + Inner Ring topic-matched (2), with outer-ring substitution in two clusters where specialized lenses are genuinely warranted (Accountant for Financial, Politician for Governance)
- Dominant topic drivers:
  - **Multi-stakeholder public-finance governance** — drives Accountant + Politician outer-ring inclusion. The REQUEST's explicit dollar figures, three-district voter mechanics, and three-union contract reconciliation are textbook "Include When" triggers, not anti-pattern cases.
  - **Human-centered impact at community scale** — drives AA + Empath pairing. The REQUEST's questions about IEP continuity, working-family logistics, town identity loss, and student belonging are irreducibly human-experience questions.
  - **Novel / multi-domain problem space** — drives Analogist inclusion. Rural school consolidation has no single established playbook; the raw data itself reaches for hospital-merger, watershed-district, and Scandinavian-hub analogies.
  - **Entangled system dynamics** — drives Systems Thinker in two clusters. Financial flows, demographic trends, and community vitality are coupled via explicit feedback loops the REQUEST surfaces (success-to-the-successful between remaining and closed-school towns; fixes-that-fail in facility closures).
