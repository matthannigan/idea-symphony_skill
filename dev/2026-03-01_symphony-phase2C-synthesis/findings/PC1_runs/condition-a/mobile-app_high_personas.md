# mobile-app — high — Brainstorming Persona Recommendations (Condition A)

The REQUEST concerns a social fitness app with 15 features, 180K downloads, 8% 30-day retention, 2% DAU/MAU, 47-second average sessions, 8 months of runway, a burned-out team of 8, and the need to cut to 3-4 core features and relaunch. The problem space spans product/feature decisions, user experience, team health, financial survival, and strategic repositioning — so topic clusters below are drawn from those natural divisions in the source material.

## Recommended Personas

| Persona | Circle | Topic Cluster | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Core persona, always included. Directly relevant: challenges assumptions like "simplification automatically means better" and "users even want this app in any form" — exactly the assumptions being tested by the decision to cut from 15 features to 3-4. |
| Pragmatist | Core | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Core persona, always included. The 8-month runway and need for a shippable MVP forces feasibility-first thinking: "what's the simplest version that solves the core problem better than the alternatives users already have?" |
| First Principles Thinker | Inner | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Strong fit for technical/product topics. The central question — "which 3-4 features should we keep" — is fundamentally about deconstructing "social fitness" to atomic units of value (workout logged, meal planned, friend found) and distinguishing causation from correlation in retention. |
| Simplifier | Inner | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Strong fit for technical/product and implementation topics. The entire REQUEST is a subtractive exercise: removing 11 of 15 features. Simplifier's "what if we removed this?" lens is the operating mode of the whole decision. |
| Provocateur | Middle | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Completes the Challenge Triad with Devil's Advocate. The REQUEST has entrenched assumptions ("we need 3-4 features", "bloat is the problem") that benefit from absurdist reframes like "keep ONE feature and make it absurdly worse" or "delete features for 8 months with no replacement." |
| Constraint Flipper | Middle | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Completes the Challenge Triad. The REQUEST is stuffed with apparent constraints (8-month runway, 47-second sessions, burned-out team) that Constraint Flipper can reframe as design assets — e.g., "what if 47 seconds is the target experience, not a failure metric?" |
| Systems Thinker | Middle | Feature Selection & Prioritization | Strong analytical fit because the 15 features are described as "deeply integrated." Feedback loops are explicit in the REQUEST: low retention → feature cutting → loss of power users → more retention loss. Dependency mapping and "keystone features" analysis is exactly this lens. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | User Experience & Emotional Impact | Core. Challenges the framing that users will accept simplification — e.g., passionate minorities of the meditation guide, supplement store, friend finder may churn en masse and leave negative reviews. |
| Pragmatist | Core | User Experience & Emotional Impact | Core. Keeps the emotional conversation tied to shippable communication strategies, migration paths, and realistic onboarding within 8 months. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner | User Experience & Emotional Impact | Strong fit for user-facing topics. The REQUEST names specific user tensions: "too many notifications," "overwhelming," and the gap between 180K downloads and 8% retention. AA maps stakeholder segments — 25-year-old fitness enthusiast vs. 55-year-old beginner — as named in the source. |
| Empath | Inner | User Experience & Emotional Impact | Strong fit. The REQUEST explicitly frames users as having "built habits" around removed features and experiencing "betrayal." | Connector | Middle | User Experience & Emotional Impact | The REQUEST's core dynamic — users attached to features they barely use — has structural parallels (IKEA effect, endowment effect, museum curation of lesser works). Connector surfaces these to help the team see the user-attachment problem through other-domain frames. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Team & Organizational Dynamics | Core. Challenges the assumption that a burned-out team can execute a relaunch within 8 months, and that engineers will willingly delete features they spent years building. |

| Empath | Inner | Team & Organizational Dynamics | Strong fit. The REQUEST names team burnout and the emotional weight of "killing darlings" as real risks. Empath handles "the grief of killed darlings" for the engineer who built the community forum and the designer who crafted 50 meditation screen flows. |

| Systems Thinker | Middle | Team & Organizational Dynamics | The REQUEST describes a reinforcing loop: team feels ownership → resists cuts → bloat persists → maintenance burden rises → morale drops. Systems Thinker maps this team-bloat feedback loop and locates leverage points. |
| Visionary | Middle | Team & Organizational Dynamics | The REQUEST asks what product would let the team do their best work — a vision question. Visionary asks what liberating work culture looks like when the team isn't "maintaining a feature graveyard." |
| Constraint Flipper | Middle | Team & Organizational Dynamics | The REQUEST treats burnout and team-of-8 as threats. Constraint Flipper reframes: "a burned-out team of 8 is actually the perfect size for a reset" — turning the staffing constraint into a rationale for speed and cohesion. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Business & Financial Survival | Core. Challenges the financial assumption set: that 8 months is enough time, that a simplified product will retain better, that removing features cuts costs rather than creating extraction costs. |

| Systems Thinker | Middle | Business & Financial Survival | Retention, acquisition, burn, and feature bloat form an interconnected system with delays (3-6 month lag to know if simplification worked, 8 months until money runs out). Systems Thinker maps the time-delay structure that makes naive financial modeling dangerous. |
| Constraint Flipper | Middle | Business & Financial Survival | The REQUEST frames 8 months as a threat. Constraint Flipper reframes it: "scarcity forces brutal prioritization that abundance never does" — treating the runway as the tool that finally forces the team past feature-creep addiction. |
| Devil's Advocate | Core | Strategic Repositioning & Relaunch | Core. | Futurist | Inner | Strategic Repositioning & Relaunch | Strong fit. ## Selection Summary

- Total personas: 35 persona-cluster assignments across 5 topic clusters (7 personas per cluster, as required at high effort)
- Unique personas used: 13 (Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, First Principles Thinker, Simplifier, Provocateur, Constraint Flipper, Systems Thinker, Audience Advocate, Empath, Storyteller, Visionary, Connector, Analogist, Accountant, Futurist — 15 total once Accountant and Futurist are counted)
- Circle distribution (per-slot counts across all clusters): Core 10 (DA×5 + Pragmatist×5), Inner 10, Middle 14, Outer 1
- Dominant topic drivers:
  - **Challenge Triad** completed on Feature Selection cluster (DA + Provocateur + Constraint Flipper) — justified by the "kill your darlings" energy and entrenched "more features is better" assumption in the REQUEST
  - **Human-Centered Triad** completed on User Experience cluster (Audience Advocate + Empath + Storyteller) — justified by the explicit emotional stakes (betrayal, grief, loss of identity) and named user scenarios in the source
  - **External Perspectives Triad** partially completed on Strategic Repositioning (Systems Thinker + Connector + Analogist) — justified by the novel positioning problem ("simplification as innovation") where cross-domain metaphor is the core need
  - **Forward-Looking Pair** (Visionary + Futurist) appears on Strategic Repositioning because the REQUEST is explicitly a strategic rebirth decision, not just a product-tweak
  - **Accountant** (outer ring) selected only for Business & Financial Survival cluster — genuinely warranted by the explicit 8-month runway, burn rate, LTV:CAC modeling, and funding-scenario content in the REQUEST; not over-applied
  - **Lawyer and Politician deliberately excluded** — the REQUEST has no regulatory dimension (privacy-first is a positioning choice, not a compliance obligation here) and the team of 8 has no multi-stakeholder governance dynamics worth Politician's lens
