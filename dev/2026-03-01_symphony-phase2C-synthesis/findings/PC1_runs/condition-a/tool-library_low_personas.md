| Persona | Circle | Topic Cluster | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Devil's Advocate | Core | (core — always included) | Required at every effort level >= Medium. Topic genuinely rewards critique: the questions surface real failure modes (volunteer burnout with "8-10 core volunteers," donated 500 sq ft storefront that "becomes unavailable after the first year," tool replacement when grant money runs out, theft of donated-space inventory). |
| Pragmatist | Core | (core — always included) | Required at every effort level >= Medium. Topic is heavily operational: 500 sq ft constraint, 8-10 volunteer capacity, checkout/return mechanics, tool stocking decisions, and membership fee structures all demand minimum-viable framing. |
| Audience Advocate | Inner Ring | Human-Centered Triad | Topic is strongly user-facing and equity-laden. The REQUEST (as surfaced by questions) centers on "a diverse neighborhood of 2,000 households," serves "homeowners versus renters, longtime residents versus newcomers, different income levels," and raises concrete access questions: "someone who doesn't speak English fluently, someone with mobility limitations," "undocumented residents, transient populations, or those without stable housing." Audience Advocate is the Strong Fit for mapping these stakeholder segments. |
| Analogist | Inner Ring | External Perspectives Triad | Topic benefits substantially from cross-domain import. The questions explicitly invoke parallels the selection rubric highlights as Analogist territory — "community seed libraries," "car-sharing models" (Zipcar/Turo), "historic tool cooperatives and guild systems," "open-source software maintenance," "library book circulation patterns." Tool libraries sit at a novel-but-mappable intersection (lending + nonprofit ops + community space), and Analogist is the Strong Fit for importing concrete solutions (tiered trust architecture from car share, collection-development/weeding from libraries, module-maintainer models from OSS). |

**Contingent Selection Summary (Medium):**

- Total personas: 4
- Circle distribution: Inner 2, Middle 0, Outer 0 (plus 2 core)
- Dominant topic drivers:
  1. Human-centered equity dimension ("diverse neighborhood of 2,000 households," renters/homeowners split, undocumented residents, mobility/language barriers) → Audience Advocate.
  2. Rich cross-domain parallels (seed libraries, Zipcar, guilds, OSS, book libraries) → Analogist.
  3. Real operational fragility (volunteer burnout, donated-space impermanence, theft, funding cliff) → Devil's Advocate (core, but highly load-bearing here).
  4. Tight resource constraints (500 sq ft, 8-10 volunteers, grant-funded) → Pragmatist (core, load-bearing).

**Cluster-overlap check:** Audience Advocate (Human-Centered) and Analogist (External Perspectives) sit in different clusters — good diversity. Devil's Advocate + Pragmatist (core) sit in Challenge and Analytical Core respectively. No cluster stacking.

**Outer-ring check (not triggered at Medium, but noted for completeness):** Accountant would be a defensible High-effort add (genuine nonprofit budgeting: "membership fee structure," "replacement reserve fund," "fixed costs — space insurance, core equipment," "break-even point at 50, 200, or 500 active users"). Politician is borderline — governance dimension exists ("built *by* and *with* the community," "who controls the library") but does not reach multi-stakeholder-coalition scale. Lawyer is weak — liability is mentioned but is not the dominant frame. Storyteller would be a strong High-effort add to complete the Human-Centered Triad given the institutional/community-service nature of the topic (per the guide's own example selections for this exact topic).
