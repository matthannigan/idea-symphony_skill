**What's distinctive about this cell:**

Career-change/high is an individual life-decision brainstorm at deep roster depth (10 Synthesize + 5 Append = 15 personas). Topic character:
- **Decision-theoretic with high human-centered density:** The topic is fundamentally a life-decision brainstorm — whether and how to transition from ICU nursing to public interest immigration law at age 32. Unlike other topics in the test set which are product/service/community-design decisions, career-change has a single decision-maker facing consequential irreversible choices with financial, emotional, identity, social, and purpose dimensions. - **Highest convergence density in test set (62.8%):** vs. property-management/high 60.9%, youth-mentorship/high 59.3%, space-party/high 58.9%, mobile-app/high 57.8%, tool-library/high ~55%. The topic's thematic concentration is unusual: a single person's career decision produces tight convergence across financial, motivational, skill-transfer, burnout-transfer, and hybrid-alternative dimensions because every persona frames the same underlying decision. **Convergence Group 4 (motivation diagnosis) has 6-way convergence** — four personas independently ask variants of "push or pull / calling or escape" (Analyst Q9, Questioner Q1, DA Q6, AA Q6, Empath Q1, FPT Q3) — this is the single highest convergence multiplicity observed in any group across the 10-topic test set.
- **Significant persona concentration in thematic clusters:** Audience Advocate's 15 questions concentrate 8 in Cluster 8 (community service) = 53% single-cluster concentration. Accountant's 12 questions concentrate 8 in Cluster 1 (financial) = 67% single-cluster concentration. Empath's 11 questions concentrate 4 in Cluster 7 (identity) = 36% single-cluster concentration. These concentrations are cell-distinctive and drive load-bearing preservation requirements.
- **Deep multi-cluster participation for Systems Thinker and First Principles Thinker:** ST spans 7 clusters (meta-lens role); FPT spans 6 clusters (definitional role). These personas are not concentrated but distributed as framing personas across the decision.
- **High orphan/bridging rate (~12%):** Not as high as mobile-app/high (35%) but above tool-library/high (10%). The specific bridging pattern (e.g., Empath Q11 bridging identity and community; ST Q11 bridging hybrid paths and systems framing) reflects the topic's genuinely multi-dimensional decision character.

Convergence density (62.8%) sits modestly above the high-effort band (55-61%). Career-change's topic character explains this: thematic concentration is tighter because all personas operate on the same decision, not a multi-faceted product/service/community design.
