This is the richest inter-domain-reconciliation pattern in the test set — school-consolidation's multi-institutional-sector span (3 districts + state government + teachers' unions + county infrastructure + community organizing + school boards) is the richest Connector trigger observed.

### Awkwardness flag for PC1 (interleaving vs. segregation)

This cell is another strong test case for interleaving vs. segregated integration (similar to youth-mentorship/high):
- Append stream at 45 is the largest in the evaluation set, exceeding Synthesized stream (40) — Append stream is 53% of total output.
- Six distinct Append-voice registers (narrative=Storyteller, reimagination=Visionary, constraint-inversion=CF, structural-isomorphism=Connector, political-analytical=Politician, orphan/inversion=Provocateur) compete for reader attention.
- Cluster 9 (Political Feasibility) would have 4 synthesized + ~15 Append cross-references under interleaving — the cluster becomes 19 questions long with Politician dominating.
