## Recommendations for RP1 (topic-level)

Sorted by drift severity × cross-link strength.

1. **[FA1 cross-linked, sub-pattern 2C unique to youth-mentorship]** Adopt RP1 item #2 in expanded form: convergence-count discipline must explicitly handle the **same-persona-multi-framing case** (count distinct personas, not distinct framings within one persona). Sub-pattern 2C is the inverse of mobile-app's sub-pattern 2B, and the RP1 wording must hold both in mind — fixing one without the other risks introducing the inverse drift. Per FA1: "(b) count distinct personas, not distinct framings — if one persona offers two angles, that is one persona, not two; (c) count personas who engage the underlying claim, even with different framing." This RP1 fix is at the **synthesis prompt** per FA1 attribution (the synthesis derives the wrong counts; the summary inherits).

2. **[FA1 cross-linked at high-B; pattern-matched at med-B]** Adopt RP1 item #6 (categorical-reframe explicit-staging requirement) for `phase4_full-synthesis.md`. The substance of the reframe survives in both med-B and high-B `_summary.md`, but neither lifts it to a declarative sentence at top of the Themes section. Concrete language per FA1: *"If the cluster contains a categorical reframe — a sentence-level claim that reframes the question itself ('we are not running X; we are running Y') — name it as a single declarative sentence at the top of the Themes section, separate from the individual Themes."* This is the highest-leverage prompt addition for youth-mentorship — it would lift Drift-2 and Drift-3 simultaneously and likely improve high-A as well (which has a comparable implicit compliance-vs-relational reframe).

3. **[PP1 cross-linked, saturated]** Adopt RP1 item #5: add `## Central Tension` field at top of `_summary.md` for med/high. low/06 already emits the explicit header; med/high silently drop it. Restoring the field unifies the four regimes around the strongest Phase-5 hand-off signal. youth-mentorship's three med/high samples all carry the central tension substance in ES — the field is a low-risk wrapper.

**Topic-specific note for RP1.** youth-mentorship is the only topic in FA1's n=16 to exhibit sub-pattern 2C (overcount-when-one-persona-multiple-framings). The mechanism — synthesizer reads two framings from the same persona as evidence of two convergent personas — is the inverse of mobile-app's mechanism. RP1's first iteration on the convergence-count rule should be regression-tested against both youth-mentorship/high/11 and mobile-app/high/05 to ensure neither sub-pattern reintroduces the other.

**Drift / total ratio (excluding regime-mandated from denominator):** 3 drift / (3 drift + 14 intentional) = **17.6%**. Above the RP1 stop criterion ceiling of 5%. RP1 iteration is required to bring youth-mentorship under the threshold; the three RP1 items above target all 3 drift cases.
