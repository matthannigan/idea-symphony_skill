All 8 use prose count language ("all four personas converge", "three of four", "every persona"). This is consistent across med and high, across topics, and across documents.
- **Convergence-count accuracy (the prose-substrate quality that Phase 5 must lift):** **1 of 8 clean** (food-truck_high_08). The other 7 samples have ≥1 count miscount (overcount or undercount), with several samples having multiple. Phase 5 cannot trust prose counts as Phase-4-substrate; Phase 5 must re-derive counts from `attributed/` if it wants accuracy.
- **Phase 5 readiness assessment from n=8: PARTIALLY READY.** The substrate Phase 5 needs (Central Tension content; convergence labels; categorical reframe; foundational reversal at high) is present in some form in all 8 samples but is not formatted for ingestion. Specifically:
  - Central Tension: substance is there 8/8 but only 2/8 are crisp enough that a downstream consumer can extract a single sentence; the prompt has no `## Central Tension` field at med/high (PP1 P0).
  - Confidence tags: 0/8 formal emission means Phase 5 cannot key off them; this is a hard prompt gap, not a quality gap.
  - Categorical reframe: 7/8 surface partial-or-strong in summary (career-change_med_01, career-change_high_06, food-truck_med_01, food-truck_med_03 partial; career-change_med_04, career-change_high_11, food-truck_high_02, food-truck_high_08 strong/PASS).
  - Foundational reversal (4 high samples): 3/4 PASS in synthesis (career-change_high_06, career-change_high_11, food-truck_high_08); 2/4 PASS in summary; 1/4 partial in summary (food-truck_high_02 at 2.5/4).
- **Caveat — n=8 may not generalize to all 10 topics:** This early-look spans 2 topics (career-change, food-truck) of 10. Phase-5-readiness assessment for topics with very different structural shapes (e.g., space-party, tool-library, school-consolidation) may differ. Confidence-tag absence and prose-substrate quality are likely **prompt-driven** properties (will replicate across topics); Central Tension clarity is plausibly **cluster-driven** (may vary by topic genre). Treat tag-emission rate as firm, Central Tension match rate as directional.
