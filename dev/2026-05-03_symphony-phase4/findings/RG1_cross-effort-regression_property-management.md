## Recommendations for RP1 (topic-level)

Sorted by priority. All cross-link to FA1 / PP1 where the drift originates.

1. **[saturated, cross-topic — already FA1 RP1 priority #4]** Persona-name suppression rule for `_summary.md` (and bullet-label form for `_synthesis.md`). Property-management's high/07 is the **single most severe Mode-4 violation in the n=16 set** — 6 distinct sentences across Exec + 4 of 5 Themes leak persona names, including character-name leaks via persona-coupled possessives (Marcus, Maria, Margaret, Elena). Property-management's brainstorming clusters generate dense persona-narrative material (Storyteller's Marcus is a 6-year-embedded liaison; Audience Advocate's Maria is a community housing partnership director), which is exactly what tempts the synthesizer. **RP1 fix via summary prompt (`phase4_full-synthesis.md` Document 3)** — the FA1 sample's recommendation language is the right wording, with explicit prohibition of character-name leaks via possessive constructions.

2. **[saturated, cross-topic — FA1 RP1 priority #5]** Add `## Central Tension` field at top of med/high `_summary.md`. All four property-management med/high samples bury Central Tension substance in Exec prose; only low/02 emits an explicit field. Property-management has unusually rich Central Tensions (med/03's contraction-vs-current-window; med/05's visibility-loss-vs-financial-loss; high/07's "cost is the credibility"; high/02's visibility paradox) that would be load-bearing for Phase 5 ingestion if surfaced explicitly. **RP1 fix via summary block of `phase4_full-synthesis.md`** — mirror the low-prompt's Central Tension field.

3. **[saturated, cross-topic — FA1 RP1 priority #6]** Categorical-reframe explicit-staging requirement. Property-management's med/03 surfaces three reframes in Exec but does not stage any as a single declarative top-of-Themes sentence; high/02 lands the reframe ("Compliance is not a cost center — it is the product") cleanly; high/07 buries it in Exec ¶4. Property-management is reframe-rich (memo identifies 4+ categorical reframes paywalled at med/high) and would benefit disproportionately from the explicit-staging rule. **RP1 fix via synthesis prompt** (categorical reframes are synthesizer-stage moves, not summary-compression).

4. **[saturated, cross-topic — FA1 RP1 priority #1, NOT applicable to property-management's sampled cells]** Single-persona-reframe-floor rule for `_summary.md`. Property-management's sampled high cells **pass** A3-content (high/07 at 75%, high/02 likely passes at synthesis-only review since not sampled by FA1) — Mode 1 is the only saturated FA1 mode that property-management high samples mostly escape. Med samples are not FA1-scored. Cross-link to other topics where this saturates; do not block on property-management evidence alone, but the rule should apply uniformly to all topics.

5. **[topic-distinctive nuance, not in FA1's RP1 list]** Within-regime cluster-shape variation in property-management is wider than other topics — the two med clusters (regulatory-risk vs. owner-trust) and two high clusters (community-relations vs. multi-jurisdiction-compliance) genuinely target different surfaces of the same business.
