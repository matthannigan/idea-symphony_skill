## Within-Effort Consistency

### Med pair (cluster-A: business-model-foundations vs. cluster-B: capital-structure-financing)

The two med clusters target structurally different sub-topics, so a direct through-line intersection is the wrong primary metric. Per the SS1 manifest selection criterion (the two med samples should differ in cluster-shape to broaden within-effort signal), variance is largely cluster-shape-driven. The right test is whether the *prompt behavior* is consistent across cluster shape — i.e., does the synthesizer behave the same way when given different substance.

- **Through-line intersection** (TLs that surface in *both* med summaries): solo-founder-as-bottleneck (memo #4); validate-before-you-borrow (memo #7). 2 shared TLs.
- **XOR (in only one)** of the topic's persistent TLs: med/01 surfaces #1 (founder-story moat), #2 (3-yr horizon suspect), #5 (ordinance), #6 (catering, partial), #9 (single-channel); med/03 surfaces #8 (used truck), #10 (seasonality, partial). Cluster-shape entirely explains the XOR set — med/01 is identity/strategy and med/03 is capital-structure.
- **Cluster-shape attribution:** all XOR through-lines explainable by cluster substance. None unexplainable by cluster-shape.

**Stylistic similarity verdict — concerning shared instability across both clusters.** Both med summaries exhibit the *same drift patterns*:

1. Convergence-inflation of single-persona insights into apparent multi-persona consensus (med/01: TL-32 management-tax; med/03: TL-7 loan-rescue-window timing labeled "Convergent view" when only Accountant articulates it). FA1 axis C fails in both samples.
2. Single-persona reframe drops in `_summary.md` (med/01: 3 Visionary reframes dropped; med/03: 4 single-persona reframes including the load-bearing month-4–5 timing). FA1 axis A3-content-summary fails in both samples (46% and 64% respectively).
3. Devil's-Advocate interrogation lens flattened to declarative in both `_synthesis.md` and `_summary.md` of med/03; same pattern observed in med/01.

The med pair is **prompt-stable in the wrong direction** — the same failure modes recur across cluster shape, meaning the prompt itself induces them rather than cluster shape varying the result. This is good news for RP1 (a single prompt-revision will hit both cases) but bad news for the current prompt.

**Med-FA1 coverage benefit:** because food-truck has FA1 sub-findings for *both* med samples, RG1 can confidently attribute these drift items to **med-tier `phase4_full-synthesis.md` prompt failures**, not to cluster-shape outliers or to a single-sample anomaly. This is a stronger attribution than other topics get (most have only high-effort FA1 coverage, so med drift attribution requires inference).

### High pair (cluster-A: fusion-defensibility vs. cluster-B: regulatory-strategy)

- **Through-line intersection:** founder-as-moat / community-relationships are mentioned in high/02 (Themes) and gestured at in high/08 (Theme 5 "Regulatory battle = audience battle" treats community-love as load-bearing legitimacy — the same idea applied to a different question). solo-founder bandwidth surfaces in both as a Risk. catering/event diversification surfaces in both as a strategic move. ~3 cross-cluster shared TLs at the topic-level abstraction.

All XOR explainable by cluster-shape (defensibility cluster vs. regulatory cluster).
- **Cluster-shape attribution:** all XOR explainable by cluster substance.

**Stylistic similarity verdict — divergent.** Unlike the med pair, the two high samples behave *differently* on FA1 axes that should be prompt-induced rather than cluster-shape-induced:

1. **Persona-name leakage in `_summary.md` body prose:** high/02 has 2 leaks (one Themes paragraph "from the Pragmatist's ROI lens to the Visionary's cultural-stewardship frame"); high/08 has 8 token leaks across 2 sentences (a 5-persona enumeration in Theme "The separation of operational and political systems" and a 3-persona enumeration in Theme "Data framed three ways"). Same axis fails in both, but **high/08's leakage is more flagrant — explicit persona enumeration vs. high/02's more rhetorical span construction.** Both are prompt-induced (the prompt's ban on persona names in `_summary.md` prose is being violated in different stylistic registers).
2. **Convergence-count accuracy:** high/02 fails Axis C (~71% accuracy, 8 miscount events on 28 claims); high/08 passes Axis C cleanly (the only numeric claim, TL-10 = 7/7, is correct, and the rest of the synthesis uses qualitative "all seven perspectives" framings that match BL1's strong-convergent rows). 3. **Through-line content survival in `_summary.md`:** high/02 fails A3-content (75–79%, 5 TLs dropped); high/08 passes (~93%, 2 single-persona TLs dropped at executive abstraction).
4. **Categorical reframe presence:** high/02 PARTIAL (preserved but distributed across 3 questions, not staged as foundational); high/08 PASS (TL-15 "regulation is audience" elevated to Theme 5, also surfaces in Exec¶4).

**The high pair is prompt-unstable.** Same prompt produces clean-and-clean (A1, B), clean-and-fail (A3-content, A3-framing-summary, C), and fail-but-different (A2 stylistic register). This is a different signal from the med pair: the high prompt's behavior depends on cluster-shape in ways that go beyond legitimate substance variance. A regulatory cluster (high/08) with strong 7/7 convergence on most TLs is easier for the synthesizer to handle faithfully than a defensibility cluster (high/02) with 5 single-persona Provocateur dissents requiring careful preservation. The Provocateur's contrarian moves (TL-22, TL-23, TL-24, TL-25) are exactly the dissent type that the prompt's "synthesis over aggregation" pressure tends to flatten. high/08 has a Devil's Advocate caveats role that the synthesizer handles well; high/02 has a Provocateur dissent role that the synthesizer handles less well.

This is a real prompt-stability concern for RP1: at high effort, the prompt is more reliable when the cluster is strongly-convergent than when it has load-bearing single-persona dissents. The dissent-preservation guidance needs strengthening.
