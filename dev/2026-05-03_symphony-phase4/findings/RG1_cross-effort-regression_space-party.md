## Recommendations for RP1 (topic-level)

Sorted by drift-impact-on-this-topic and saturation in cross-topic FA1 evidence:

1. **Add convergence-count discipline rule with sub-pattern guardrails** (FA1 RP1 #2 saturated; aligns with FA1's space-party-high-06 Axis C FAIL). The high/06 summary inflates "Six of seven personas independently converged" — defensible — but the underlying synthesis reaches for "all seven personas" twice when ledger is 6/7 and 5/7. Although the strict overcounts surfaced in attributed/synthesis (FA1's territory), the rhetorical pattern needs a prompt-level fix before it reaches `_summary.md` text. **Cross-link:** FA1 sample `space-party_high_06`, Axis C FAIL.

2. **Add `## Central Tension` field at top of `_summary.md` for med/high** (FA1 RP1 #5 saturated; PP1 P0). Within space-party, low/03 surfaces an inline "Central Tension:" bolded line that lands cleanly; med/01, med/05, high/06, high/02 all bury the equivalent in Exec ¶1-2 prose. The med/01 immersion-vs-inclusion tension and the high/06 empowerment-vs-relief-from-responsibility tension are both load-bearing decision-inputs that get diluted without a labeled field. **Cross-link:** PP1 P0; FA1 RP1 #5.

3. **Align `stage` and `effort` frontmatter field values across the three Phase 4 prompts** (PP1 contract drift; surfaces directly at this topic). `"Phase 4: Summary Generation"` vs. `"Phase 4: Response Synthesis"`; `"min"`/`"low"` abbreviated vs. `"medium"`/`"high"` full-word. Trivial fix, eliminates downstream-tooling fragility. **Cross-link:** PP1.

4. **Align inline confidence-tag vocabulary across the three Phase 4 prompts** (PP1; topic-level evidence at this sample set). min uses `[recurring]`/`[single]`; low uses `[convergent]`/`[trade-off]`/`[unique: risk|feasibility]`; med and high use no inline tags at all. Pick one vocabulary (BL1 ledger uses `[convergent: N/M]` / `[unique: risk|feasibility|emotional|...]` / `[trade-off]` / `[single]` — recommended) and require it consistently in summary recommended-action bullets. **Cross-link:** PP1.

5. **Optional / topic-specific: address the Recommended-actions horizon-label inconsistency** (min/low/high-02 use generic "Immediate/Near-term/Long-term"; med samples and high/06 use party-relevant "this week/day-of"). The party-relevant labels are more useful to a real planner. Consider making party-relevant horizons the default at all four efforts. Lower priority than items 1–4.
