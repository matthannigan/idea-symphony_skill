## Background

### Context

FA1, LB1, and RG1 all need a ground-truth reference: **what should ideal Phase 4 output preserve from these Phase 3 responses?** BL1 builds that reference per cluster — a through-line ledger plus convergence map (low/med/high), faithfulness target, and persona-distinctiveness map (med + high).

**Strategy is from-scratch only across all efforts** (per Discussion Q4). Do not seed from existing `synthesis/attributed/{NN}_{cluster-slug}.md`. The `attributed/` file was generated by the prompt under test, so seeding from it risks circular reasoning — BL1's ground truth would inherit any biases the prompt had (dropped through-lines, fabricated attributions, smoothed convergences), and FA1 would then score the prompt against a ledger the prompt itself shaped. The existing `attributed/` files are used only as a *post-baseline cross-reference* — after the ledger is built, the subagent compares its independent ledger to `attributed/` and flags discrepancies for FA1's downstream use, but does not modify the ledger based on `attributed/`.

## Investigation Task

### I-BL1: Build per-cluster ground-truth ledgers for the 60 samples

**Key question:** For each of the 60 sampled clusters, what is the through-line ledger that all downstream scoring should use as its reference?

**Research questions addressed:** RQ-BL1a–e (methodology FQ1, FQ2, FQ4, FQ5)

**Execution model:** Fan-out by sample. **60 subagents in parallel** (batched 6 concurrent ≈ 10 waves), each handling one cluster sample in isolation. Each per-sample subagent produces one consolidated baseline file. A cross-sample synthesis subagent then compiles patterns across the 60 samples.

**Model assignment (mixed by effort tier):**

| Effort | Sample count | Model | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| min | 10 | Sonnet | Single-voice extraction; no convergence math, no persona-distinctiveness map. Task is dominantly comprehension + intra-response recurrence flagging. |
| low | 10 | Sonnet | Only 2 personas (DA + Pragmatist); convergence is binary (2/2 or unique). Limited room for judgment error. |
| med | 20 | Opus | 4 personas; substantive-similarity convergence judgment, persona-distinctiveness map, and `attributed/` cross-reference all load-bearing. |
| high | 20 | Opus | 7 personas; most complex convergence calls and most subtle voice distinctions. |
| Cross-sample synthesis | 1 | Opus | Aggregates 60 baselines; pattern abstraction work. |

The Opus tier is reserved for the steps where weaker reasoning measurably degrades the ground truth: substantive-similarity convergence calls (lump-vs-split errors propagate to FA1/LB1/RG1 convergence rates), Central Tension abstraction (Sonnet tends to produce generic tensions rather than cluster-specific ones), persona-distinctiveness framing (voice-level distinctions, not just content-level), and Step 6 resistance to circular reasoning when reading `attributed/`.

**Recommended sanity check before launching the full 40-sample Opus run:** spawn 2 med samples on both Opus and Sonnet in parallel, diff the ledgers. If they substantively agree on convergence calls and Central Tension, there is empirical license to push Sonnet onto med as well. If they disagree, keep Opus on med + high as specified.

Per-cluster granularity is the methodology default — each baseline is uncontaminated by cross-cluster assumptions, preserving the possibility that effort levels and topics produce fundamentally different through-line structures.

## Method

### Step 1: Build the through-line inventory (from `responses/` only)

Read all persona response files in your sample. For each, extract every distinct **through-line** — a topic-level claim, recommendation, observation, or stance. Count one through-line per discrete idea regardless of how many sentences it spans in the response.

**Universal across all efforts:** build the ledger from `responses/` only. Do not read `attributed/` (med + high samples) until Step 6 (post-baseline cross-reference). This rule prevents the prompt under test from contaminating its own ground truth.

### Step 2: Map convergence (low / med / high only)

For each through-line, count how many distinct personas raised it (verbatim or substantively similar). Categorize:
- **Convergent**: 2+ personas at low; 2+ personas at med; 2+ personas at high (note: at low the only convergence count possible is 2/2)
- **Unique**: only 1 persona raised it

For min, convergence is undefined (single brainstormer). Instead, identify **intra-response convergence**: ideas the brainstormer returned to in multiple responses across questions in the cluster.

### Step 3: Set the faithfulness target

Decide what the Phase 4 output should preserve:
- Every convergent through-line **should** appear in `_synthesis.md` (med/high) or `_summary.md` (universal). This is the load-bearing core.
- Every unique through-line is **should-also-appear** unless trivial — the value of high-effort over low-effort is precisely that unique single-persona insights surface.
- For min: every intra-response-recurring through-line is should-appear.

### Step 4: Build the persona-distinctiveness map (med + high only)

For each persona in the sample, name what they distinctively contributed to this cluster — the through-lines or framings that no other persona produced. This is the FA1 audit's reference for "did this persona's voice survive synthesis?"

For low: the DA/Pragmatist contrast is the distinctiveness map — name the tension.

For min: skip; single-voice has no distinctiveness comparison.

### Step 5: Flag aggregable Phase-5 signal candidates (continued below)

For each sample, identify candidates for the Phase 5 signals named in [`dev/2026-04-23_symphony-phase5-investigation.md`](../../2026-04-23_symphony-phase5-investigation.md):
- **Central Tension candidate** — what is the cluster's organizing trade-off? (universal — applies at every effort)
- **Confidence-tag candidates** — for through-lines, classify as `[convergent]` (multi-persona at low/med/high), `[trade-off]` (DA vs. Pragmatist or multi-persona disagreement), `[unique: risk]` / `[unique: feasibility]` (low only — single-lens flag), `[recurring]` / `[single]` (min only — intra-response repeat)
- **Neither-lens gap candidates (low only)** — what does the DA + Pragmatist pairing structurally not surface in this cluster? Be specific to the cluster substance; skip if nothing emerges
- **Conspicuous-absence candidates (min only)** — what stance does the brainstormer's output never take? Be specific; skip if nothing emerges

These flagged candidates serve FA1's per-cluster Phase-5-signal log (`findings/FA1_phase5-signal-log.md`).

### Step 6: Post-baseline cross-reference against `attributed/` (med + high only)

**Only after Steps 1-5 are complete and your ledger is finalized**, read the existing `synthesis/attributed/{CLUSTER_NN}_{CLUSTER_SLUG}.md` file. Compare it to your independent ledger and produce three flag lists for FA1:

- **Through-lines `attributed/` claims that are absent from your ledger** — possible prompt fabrications. List each with `attributed/`'s wording and the persona it's attributed to.
- **Through-lines you found in `responses/` that `attributed/` dropped** — possible prompt failures. List each with your ledger ID and the persona(s) that raised it.
- **Persona attributions in `attributed/` that don't match your ledger** — possible misattributions. List each with `attributed/`'s attribution and your ledger's correct attribution.

These flags inform FA1 (Axis A1 scoring) but **do not modify your ledger**. The ledger remains as built in Steps 1-5.

For min + low samples: Step 6 is n/a (no `attributed/` file exists at these efforts).

## Quality Standards

- Every through-line entry must include the persona attribution and at least an excerpt or paraphrase of the source response. Pure summary without source-anchoring fails the audit.
- For med + high samples, the hybrid-audit notes section must explicitly document additions/removals/re-attributions, even if the count is zero.
- Confidence-tag candidates must use the exact tag vocabulary from the Phase 4 prompts (`[convergent]`, `[trade-off]`, `[unique: risk]`, `[unique: feasibility]`, `[recurring]`, `[single]`) so FA1 can match the prompts' output schema.
- The faithfulness target column drives FA1 and LB1 scoring. Be deliberate about what should-appear vs. optional. A through-line that you flag as "optional" in the ledger but that the Phase 4 output drops will not count against the prompt.
